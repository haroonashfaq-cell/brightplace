# brightplace Community Voice

**A quick Q&A for the on-site team to power the community page with real content**

---

## Before you start

This takes about **10-15 minutes**. It is not a test. There are no wrong answers.

You know things about this community that no website, brochure, or AI can generate — what it is actually like to live here, what residents love, what the neighborhood feels like at 7am on a Tuesday. That is what we want.

**Why it matters:** Renters trust specific, real answers over polished marketing. And AI search engines (Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity) now prioritize exactly this kind of authentic, on-the-ground content. Your answers will rank.

**How to answer:**
- Talk like you are telling a friend. Specific beats generic every time.
- Use real names — "the Publix on Main Street" not "grocery stores nearby."
- Skip anything that does not apply. Write "N/A" and move on.
- Keep answers about the place, never about who lives here (Fair Housing).
- Honesty about trade-offs is welcome. Candor builds trust.
