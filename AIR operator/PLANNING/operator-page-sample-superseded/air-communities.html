Helpful questions

A little more context.

Does this include every AIR community?

This reference includes the ten communities in the brightplace AIR pilot. It is not a count of AIR's full portfolio.

Where can I confirm pricing and availability?

Open a community and continue to its website for current information. Confirm the full cost and availability of a specific home with that community.

How do I compare communities?

Start with the location you need, then explore the cost, floor plans and everyday details at each community. The best fit depends on your own priorities.
