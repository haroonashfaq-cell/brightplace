Where it leads

A direct path to the community team, with a clearer understanding of the next step.

01What would I pay each month?

Bring base rent and applicable recurring fees into the same conversation. Distinguish monthly costs from one-time charges and identify any estimates.

Useful context: “Does the monthly total include parking, pet rent and utilities?”

Why your team benefits: Renters can ask about the full cost before committing time to a tour.

02Does this community fit my needs?

Let renters explore community features, pet policies, transportation and nearby everyday services using approved information.

Useful context: “What are the pet rules, and where can I find nearby transit?”

Why your team benefits: The conversation can start with the renter’s specific requirements.

03What is available for my move?

Present availability from the agreed source and make freshness clear. When a detail is unknown, route the question to leasing.

Useful context: “Who can confirm availability for my move-in date?”

Why your team benefits: Renters know what is confirmed and what needs a conversation with your team.

Questions your team will ask.

Is this for operators or renters?

This page is for apartment operators evaluating Direct. The community websites, renter advisor and guides are the experience renters use.

Would we need to replace our existing website?

The proposed Direct community sites live on brightplace.ai. Whether they complement or replace an existing site is a decision for your pilot scope. No migration is assumed here.

Where do renter inquiries go?

The intent is to connect renters directly with the operator. Your team and brightplace agree the destination and verify that inquiries reach the right people before launch.

What does our team need to provide?

Start with pilot communities, approved property information, the relevant data owners and a leasing contact route. The working session identifies access, approval and ongoing maintenance responsibilities.

What does it cost, and how long does setup take?

Pricing and launch timing are not defined in this reference. They need to be agreed against the community count, data connections and rollout scope.

How will we know whether it is helping?

Agree a baseline and pilot measures before launch. Candidate measures include visits to community pages, questions renters ask and contact actions. Reporting availability and any connection to leasing outcomes must be confirmed in the pilot scope.
