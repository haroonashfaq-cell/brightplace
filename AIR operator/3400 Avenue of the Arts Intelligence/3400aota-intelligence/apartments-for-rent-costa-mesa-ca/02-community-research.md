## Costa Mesa Neighborhoods for Renters
1. **South Coast Metro / Performing Arts District** — cultural hub, employment center, South Coast Plaza, Segerstrom Center. Highest rents.
2. **Eastside** — closer to Newport Beach, more residential, surf-town vibe. Mid-range rents.
3. **Westside** — more affordable, near Huntington Beach, industrial pockets. Budget-friendly.
4. **Mesa Verde** — residential, near schools, family-oriented. Mid-range.
