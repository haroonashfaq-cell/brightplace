Apartments for Rent in Costa Mesa CA: A Neighborhood-by-Neighborhood Guide for 2026

Costa Mesa sits in the center of Orange County between Newport Beach and Huntington Beach, with apartment rents ranging from $1,800 on the Westside to $3,800+ in the South Coast Metro district as of Q3 2026. The city has four distinct rental neighborhoods, each with a different price point, commute profile, and lifestyle. Knowing which neighborhood matches your budget and priorities before touring saves time and prevents overpaying for a location that does not serve your daily needs.

This guide breaks down Costa Mesa's rental market by neighborhood, covers what to look for in a Costa Mesa apartment, and highlights the communities that offer the strongest value for what they charge.

How Much Is Rent in Costa Mesa in 2026?

Costa Mesa's rental market sits below Newport Beach but above most inland Orange County cities. Here is what to expect by unit type as of Q3 2026:

Studio apartments in Costa Mesa: $2,200 to $3,600/mo. Studios are concentrated in South Coast Metro and command premium pricing due to the district's employment density.

One-bedroom apartments in Costa Mesa: $2,200 to $3,815/mo. The citywide average one-bedroom rent is approximately $2,400, but South Coast Metro pulls that average higher.

Two-bedroom apartments in Costa Mesa: $2,900 to $4,664/mo. Two-bedrooms vary the most by neighborhood, with Westside two-bedrooms starting $1,000+ below South Coast Metro equivalents.

Three-bedroom apartments in Costa Mesa: Limited supply. Not all communities offer three-bedrooms. Expect $3,200+ where available.

How Costa Mesa compares to neighbors:

Newport Beach 1BR: $3,000+ (Costa Mesa saves $600+/mo)

Huntington Beach 1BR: $2,500+ (Costa Mesa is comparable)

Fountain Valley 1BR: $2,100+ (Costa Mesa runs $300+ higher)

Santa Ana 1BR: $1,800+ (Costa Mesa runs $600+ higher)

Costa Mesa renters pay a premium over inland cities for beach proximity and the South Coast Metro employment hub, but save significantly compared to Newport Beach for nearly the same coastal access.

Which Costa Mesa Neighborhoods Are Best for Renters?

Costa Mesa has four distinct areas, each with a different personality and price range.

South Coast Metro / Performing Arts District

What it is: Costa Mesa's cultural and business hub. Home to Segerstrom Center for the Arts, South Coast Plaza, and 1,300+ companies employing 64,000+ daily workers.

Rent range: $2,200 to $3,800+ for one-bedrooms. The highest rents in Costa Mesa.

Best for: Renters who work in the South Coast Metro district and want to walk or bike to work. Culture lovers who want Segerstrom Center and South Coast Plaza on their doorstep. Professionals who travel frequently (John Wayne Airport is 5 minutes away).

Notable community: 3400 Avenue of the Arts offers studios from $2,204 (as of Q3 2026) with a rock climbing center, two saltwater pools, movie theater, waterfront amphitheater, and complimentary fitness classes.

Eastside Costa Mesa

What it is: A residential neighborhood closer to Newport Beach with a surf-town atmosphere. Local restaurants along 17th Street and Newport Blvd.

Rent range: $2,400 to $2,800 for one-bedrooms.

Best for: Beach-oriented renters who want the shortest drive to Newport Beach (5 to 10 minutes). Renters who prefer local cafes and shops over corporate retail. Those who want a quieter, more residential feel.

Westside Costa Mesa

What it is: The most affordable part of Costa Mesa, closer to Huntington Beach. Some industrial pockets mixed with residential areas.

Rent range: $1,800 to $2,400 for one-bedrooms.

Best for: Budget-conscious renters who want a Costa Mesa address without South Coast Metro pricing. Renters who commute to Huntington Beach employers. Those who prioritize savings over walkable retail and dining.

Mesa Verde

What it is: A residential pocket with tree-lined streets, near Mesa Verde Country Club. More suburban than other Costa Mesa neighborhoods.

Rent range: $2,100 to $2,600 for one-bedrooms.

Best for: Renters who want a quiet, established neighborhood near parks and schools. Those who prefer suburban living within a city that still has beach and cultural access.

What Should You Look for in a Costa Mesa Apartment?

Based on what Costa Mesa renters actually prioritize and what separates good deals from bad ones:

In-unit washer and dryer. Sixty-seven percent of renters nationally call this a must-have. In Costa Mesa, shared laundry rooms are still common in older buildings. New and renovated communities typically include in-unit machines. This is the single amenity worth paying extra for.

Parking included vs extra. Costa Mesa parking costs range from $0 (included) to $300/mo (garage, South Coast Metro). Always ask about parking fees before comparing rents. A "$2,400 apartment" that charges $200/mo for parking is actually $2,600.

Pool quality. Orange County sun makes a pool practical 8+ months of the year. Standard chlorine pools are fine. Saltwater pools are gentler on skin and hair. Resort-style with cabanas is a genuine lifestyle upgrade. Ask which type the community has.

EV charging. If you drive an electric vehicle, designated EV parking saves the hassle of finding public chargers. Not all Costa Mesa communities offer this. 3400 Avenue of the Arts has designated EV parking.

Pet policy specifics. Costa Mesa has high pet ownership. Check weight limits, breed restrictions, monthly pet rent, and one-time deposits. Some communities charge $50+/mo per pet on top of a $500+ deposit. Others are more reasonable.

Sound insulation. Thin walls ruin the apartment experience regardless of how nice the finishes are. Ask about construction type and read reviews for noise complaints.

How Does Costa Mesa Compare to Nearby OC Cities?

Costa Mesa vs Newport Beach: Newport Beach runs $600+ more per month for similar unit sizes. Costa Mesa is 5 to 15 minutes from Newport Beach by car, so renters get nearly identical beach access at a meaningful discount.

Costa Mesa vs Irvine: Irvine offers newer construction and a master-planned suburban feel, but rents are comparable to South Coast Metro. Costa Mesa has stronger cultural amenities (Segerstrom Center, restaurant scene) and more architectural variety.

Costa Mesa vs Huntington Beach: Huntington Beach offers direct beach access and a surf-town lifestyle. Rents are similar to Costa Mesa overall, slightly higher near the coast. Costa Mesa's South Coast Metro district has stronger employment density.

Costa Mesa vs Santa Ana: Santa Ana is significantly cheaper ($600+/mo less). The tradeoff is fewer amenities, less beach access, and different neighborhood character. Budget renters who do not need beach proximity should compare Santa Ana.

Who Is Costa Mesa Best For?

South Coast Metro professionals: If you work in the district's 1,300+ companies, living in Costa Mesa eliminates the commute. Walk to work, walk to South Coast Plaza, walk to Segerstrom Center.

Beach-adjacent renters on a budget: Costa Mesa is cheaper than Newport Beach with the same coastal access. Eastside Costa Mesa is 5 to 10 minutes from the beach.

Culture and dining enthusiasts: Segerstrom Center hosts world-class performances. Costa Mesa's restaurant scene is one of the strongest in Orange County. The Camp and Lab Anti-Mall offer independent retail.

Frequent travelers: John Wayne Airport is 5 minutes from South Coast Metro. LAX is 45 minutes. San Diego is 75 minutes. Costa Mesa is one of the best-connected cities in Orange County for air travel.

Frequently Asked Questions About Apartments for Rent in Costa Mesa CA

How much is rent in Costa Mesa CA?

What is the cheapest area to rent in Costa Mesa?

The Westside of Costa Mesa offers the lowest rents, with one-bedrooms from approximately $1,800 per month. The tradeoff is fewer walkable amenities and some industrial pockets compared to South Coast Metro or the Eastside.

Is Costa Mesa a good place to live?

Yes. Costa Mesa offers beach proximity (5 to 15 minutes), world-class culture (Segerstrom Center), premier shopping (South Coast Plaza), strong employment (64,000+ daily workers in South Coast Metro), and excellent airport access (John Wayne Airport, 5 minutes). How far is Costa Mesa from the beach?

Costa Mesa's Eastside is 5 to 10 minutes from Newport Beach by car. The Westside is about 10 minutes from Huntington Beach. South Coast Metro is 10 to 15 minutes from the coast.

What should I look for in a Costa Mesa apartment?

Are there pet-friendly apartments in Costa Mesa?

Yes. Many Costa Mesa communities allow pets, though policies vary widely. 3400 Avenue of the Arts has a dedicated pet park. Some communities like Wimbledon Glen may not allow dogs. Always confirm weight limits, breed restrictions, and monthly pet rent before applying.

What is South Coast Metro?

South Coast Metro is Costa Mesa's premier business and cultural district, home to South Coast Plaza (250+ retailers), Segerstrom Center for the Arts (Broadway, symphony, ballet), and over 1,300 companies employing 64,000+ daily workers. It has the highest apartment rents in Costa Mesa but also the most amenities and walkable employment.

How does Costa Mesa compare to Newport Beach for renters?

When is the best time to rent in Costa Mesa?

Fall and winter (October through February) typically offer the best rental deals in Costa Mesa. Landlords are more willing to negotiate on pricing and move-in specials during slower leasing months compared to the peak summer season (May through August).
