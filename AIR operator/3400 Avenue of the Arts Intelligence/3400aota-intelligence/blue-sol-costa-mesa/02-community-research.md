# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Blue Sol Costa Mesa

## Sources: Yelp, ApartmentRatings (78 reviews), Apartments.com, Greystar

## Positive Themes
- **Staff:** Front office team praised, maintenance swift and effective
- **Pet-friendly:** Dog park on site. Pets welcome.
- **Location:** Easy access to restaurants, beaches, entertainment. Costa Mesa's Eastside/Westside neighborhood.
- **Floor plan variety:** 11 plans across 1-3BR. More bedroom options than many Costa Mesa competitors.

## Negative Themes
- **Amenity condition:** "Luxury amenities not in good condition." Shared spaces described as dirty.
- **Management attention to detail:** Lack of upkeep on common areas despite luxury positioning.
- **Price vs quality gap:** Positioned as luxury but delivery doesn't match.

## Key Differentiators vs 3400 AotA
- Blue Sol has a dog park (3400 AotA also has pet park — both dog-friendly)
- Blue Sol has 3BR units (3400 AotA may not — check)
- Blue Sol is on Eastside Costa Mesa (more residential, closer to beaches)
- 3400 AotA has rock climbing, movie theater, amphitheater, saltwater pools, EV, fitness classes — Blue Sol has none
- Blue Sol managed by Greystar; 3400 AotA by AIR Communities
- Both have pool and gym. 3400 AotA's are saltwater/resort-style.

## Content Angle
- Blue Sol promises luxury but reviews say delivery falls short
- 3400 AotA offers more amenities, better maintained, at competitive pricing
- Blue Sol's Eastside location is closer to Newport Beach — different vibe than South Coast Metro
- Honest: Blue Sol works for beach-proximity seekers. 3400 AotA works for amenity maximizers.
