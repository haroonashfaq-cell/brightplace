580 Anton Apartments Costa Mesa: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

580 Anton sits at the center of Costa Mesa's South Coast Metro district, steps from Segerstrom Center for the Arts and South Coast Plaza. Studios start at $2,659 and two-bedrooms reach $5,482 as of Q3 2026. The building delivers rooftop pool views and upscale finishes, but renters comparing Costa Mesa apartments should weigh pricing, amenity depth, and resident experience before committing to a lease.

This guide breaks down what 580 Anton actually offers, what 88 Yelp reviewers say after living there, and how it compares to 3400 Avenue of the Arts, the other major apartment community in the same South Coast Metro district.

How Much Does It Cost to Live at 580 Anton in 2026?

580 Anton's rents place it among the most expensive apartment communities in Costa Mesa. Here is what each unit type costs as of Q3 2026:

Studio apartments: Starting at $2,659/mo

One-bedroom apartments: Starting at $2,871/mo

Two-bedroom, one-bath: Starting at $3,208/mo

Two-bedroom, two-bath: Starting at $3,845/mo, reaching up to $5,482 for larger layouts

580 Anton is currently offering up to four weeks free on select studio homes. Mill Creek Residential manages the property.

The average one-bedroom rent in South Coast Metro is approximately $2,894 as of mid-2026. 580 Anton's studio pricing starts below that average, but one-bedrooms and two-bedrooms run $200 to $600 above it once you move past the studios.

Renters comparing 580 Anton to other South Coast Metro communities should also look at 3400 Avenue of the Arts, which starts at $2,204 for a studio (as of Q3 2026). That is $455 less per month for a studio in the same district. Over a 12-month lease, that difference adds up to $5,460.

What Amenities Does 580 Anton Offer?

580 Anton's amenity package centers on its rooftop experience and building quality.

Building amenities at 580 Anton:

Resort-style rooftop pool deck with South Coast Metro skyline views

Fitness center with cardio and strength equipment

Business center with co-working space

Parking garage

On-site pet grooming salon

Unit features at 580 Anton:

Private balconies

The rooftop pool is 580 Anton's standout. It sits above the building with views across the district, and multiple reviews describe it as well-maintained and genuinely resort-quality. The fitness center covers the basics but does not include group fitness classes or specialized equipment.

The pet grooming salon is a relatively uncommon amenity in Costa Mesa. For renters with dogs or cats who regularly need grooming, this saves a trip to an off-site groomer.

What 580 Anton does not have that renters at this price point often expect: no rock climbing wall, no movie theater, no second pool, no saltwater pools, no amphitheater, no basketball court, no interactive game room, no dedicated EV charging stations, and no complimentary fitness classes. For renters who want amenity variety beyond the rooftop pool and gym, that gap is worth noting.

How Does 580 Anton Compare to 3400 Avenue of the Arts?

Both 580 Anton and 3400 Avenue of the Arts sit in Costa Mesa's South Coast Metro district. Both are within walking distance of Segerstrom Center for the Arts and South Coast Plaza. The location is equivalent. The differences are in pricing, amenities, and unit features.

Pricing comparison (as of Q3 2026):

580 Anton studio: $2,659/mo. 3400 Avenue of the Arts studio: $2,204/mo. Difference: $455/mo, or $5,460 per year.

580 Anton one-bedroom: $2,871/mo. 3400 Avenue of the Arts one-bedroom: approximately $2,500/mo. Savings: roughly $370/mo at 3400.

Amenity comparison:

580 Anton has 5 community amenities: rooftop pool, fitness center, business center, parking garage, pet grooming salon.

3400 Avenue of the Arts has 12+ community amenities: rock climbing center, two resort-style saltwater pools with spas, waterfront amphitheater, interactive digital game room, movie theater, basketball court, billiards room, clubhouse, fitness center with complimentary group classes, designated EV parking, and a pet park.

3400 Avenue of the Arts offers more than double the amenity count at a lower monthly rent. The rock climbing center, movie theater, and waterfront amphitheater are amenities that no other apartment community in Costa Mesa provides.

Unit feature comparison:

Both communities include in-unit washer/dryer, stainless steel appliances, and private balconies. 3400 Avenue of the Arts adds built-in wine chillers and fireplaces in select units. These features are not available at 580 Anton at any price point.

Pet policies:

Both 580 Anton and 3400 Avenue of the Arts are pet-friendly. 580 Anton has a pet grooming salon. 3400 Avenue of the Arts has a dedicated outdoor pet park. Both approaches serve pet owners, but the needs differ: grooming salon for regular maintenance vs. pet park for daily exercise and play.

Management:

580 Anton is managed by Mill Creek Residential. 3400 Avenue of the Arts is managed by AIR Communities (Apartment Income REIT).

What Are 580 Anton Renters Saying in 2026?

580 Anton has approximately 88 reviews on Yelp and additional reviews across ApartmentRatings, Google, and Apartments.com. Here is what comes up consistently across platforms.

What residents praise about 580 Anton:

The rooftop pool gets the most positive mentions. Residents describe it as the building's best feature and a genuine resort-quality experience. Several residents highlight the modern finishes and open floor plan layouts. The location receives consistent praise for walking access to South Coast Plaza and the restaurant scene along Anton Blvd and Bristol Street. Multiple reviewers note that the move-in process was smooth and professional.

One long-term resident (3+ years on Yelp) credits the staff as the primary reason for staying, calling it "a pleasant experience, mainly because of staff."

What residents flag about 580 Anton:

Maintenance response times appear across multiple negative reviews. Residents report waiting weeks for AC repairs and plumbing fixes. For a community charging $2,659+ per month, delayed maintenance is a significant concern.

Value relative to rent is the second most common criticism. Multiple reviewers describe 580 Anton's apartments as "small, expensive, and poorly cleaned." Common area upkeep draws complaints about "dingy carpet in hallways and scuffed walls." Noise transfer between units is noted by several residents.

Overall impression from 580 Anton reviews:

580 Anton delivers on rooftop aesthetics and South Coast Metro location. The recurring concerns center on maintenance responsiveness, price-to-value ratio, and common area condition. Renters who prioritize responsive management, a broader amenity package, or lower rent for the same district should compare alternatives before signing.

Is South Coast Metro a Good Place to Rent in Costa Mesa?

South Coast Metro is Costa Mesa's premier business and cultural district. Regardless of which apartment community you choose, the neighborhood delivers strong fundamentals for renters.

Employment density: Over 64,000 people work in the South Coast Metro district daily across 1,300+ companies. Fifty of those companies employ 100 or more people. South Coast Plaza alone has over 9,000 retail workers. Living in the district means your commute could be a walk instead of a drive.

Culture and entertainment: Segerstrom Center for the Arts hosts Broadway touring productions, the Pacific Symphony, and the American Ballet Theatre's Orange County season. The Orange County Museum of Art (OCMA) is within the district. Costa Mesa's independent restaurant scene is one of the strongest in Orange County.

Shopping: South Coast Plaza holds the largest concentration of luxury brands on the West Coast and consistently ranks among the highest-grossing retail properties in the United States. Nordstrom, Saks Fifth Avenue, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and over 250 other retailers operate here.

Commute and transit: John Wayne Airport (SNA) is five minutes away by car. I-405 provides direct freeway access. Newport Beach and Huntington Beach are 10 minutes away. Los Angeles is approximately 45 minutes north. San Diego is 75 minutes south.

Daily needs: Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, and multiple grocery options are within a short drive. The Camp and The Lab Anti-Mall offer independent and artisan retail for a different shopping experience.
