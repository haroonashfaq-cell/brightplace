# 580 Anton Apartments Costa Mesa: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

580 Anton sits at the center of Costa Mesa's South Coast Metro district, steps from Segerstrom Center for the Arts and South Coast Plaza. Studios start at $2,659 and two-bedrooms reach $5,482 as of Q3 2026. The building delivers rooftop pool views and upscale finishes, but renters comparing options in the area should weigh pricing, amenities, and resident experience before committing.

This guide breaks down what 580 Anton actually offers, what residents say after living there, and how it stacks up against 3400 Avenue of the Arts, the other major apartment community in the same district.

580 Anton's pricing reflects its position as one of South Coast Metro's premium addresses. Here is what each unit type costs as of Q3 2026:

**Studio apartments:** Starting at $2,659/mo

**One-bedroom apartments:** Starting at $2,871/mo

**Two-bedroom, one-bath:** Starting at $3,208/mo

Renters looking at this price range should also check 3400 Avenue of the Arts, which starts at $2,204 for a studio, saving roughly $455 per month before factoring in amenities.

## What Amenities Does 580 Anton Offer?

580 Anton's amenity list centers on its rooftop experience and building quality:

**Building amenities:** Resort-style rooftop pool deck with views of the South Coast Metro skyline, fitness center, business center with co-working space, parking garage

**Unit features:** Private balconies, in-unit washer and dryer, stainless steel appliances, air conditioning, modern cabinetry

**Pet amenities:** On-site pet grooming salon (a relatively uncommon perk in Costa Mesa apartments)

The rooftop pool is the standout. It is elevated, well-maintained according to most reviews, and offers a genuine resort feel without leaving the building. The fitness center covers the basics but does not include group fitness classes.

What 580 Anton does not have that some renters expect at this price point: no dedicated game room, no movie theater, no basketball court, no rock climbing wall, no amphitheater, no EV charging stations, and no complimentary fitness classes. For renters who value variety in community amenities beyond the pool and gym, that matters.

## What Are Renters Saying About 580 Anton?

580 Anton has approximately 88 reviews on Yelp with a mixed overall rating. Here is what comes up consistently.

**What residents like:** The rooftop pool and its views get mentioned frequently. Several residents call out the modern finishes and open floor plan layouts. The location, specifically walking access to South Coast Plaza and restaurants, gets consistent praise. Move-in was described as smooth by multiple reviewers.

**What residents flag:** Maintenance response times appear in multiple negative reviews. Residents report delays on AC repairs and plumbing issues, sometimes waiting weeks. Some reviews mention that the price per square foot feels high compared to other South Coast Metro options. Noise transfer between units has been noted.

**Overall impression from reviews:** 580 Anton delivers on aesthetics and location. The concerns center on ongoing maintenance responsiveness and value relative to the rent charged. Renters who prioritize responsive management and a broader amenity package may want to compare options before signing.

## Is South Coast Metro a Good Place to Rent in Costa Mesa?

South Coast Metro is Costa Mesa's premier business and cultural district. Whether you are renting at 580 Anton, 3400 Avenue of the Arts, or anywhere else in the area, here is what the neighborhood delivers.

**Employment:** Over 64,000 people work in the district daily across 1,300+ companies. Fifty of those companies have 100 or more employees. South Coast Plaza alone employs over 9,000 retail workers. If your job is in this district, living here eliminates a commute entirely.

**Culture and entertainment:** Segerstrom Center for the Arts hosts Broadway shows, the Pacific Symphony, and the American Ballet Theatre's Orange County season. The Orange County Museum of Art (OCMA) is within the district. Costa Mesa has one of the strongest independent restaurant scenes in Orange County.

**Shopping:** South Coast Plaza holds the largest concentration of luxury brands on the West Coast and consistently ranks among the highest-grossing retail properties in the United States. Nordstrom, Saks Fifth Avenue, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and over 250 other retailers are here.

**Transit and commute:** John Wayne Airport is five minutes away. I-405 provides direct freeway access. Newport Beach and Huntington Beach are 10 minutes by car. Los Angeles is approximately 45 minutes north; San Diego is 75 minutes south.

**Grocery and daily needs:** Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, and multiple grocery options are within a short drive. The Camp and The Lab Anti-Mall offer independent and artisan retail nearby.

## Frequently Asked Questions About 580 Anton Costa Mesa

### How much is rent at 580 Anton Costa Mesa?
Studios start at $2,659 per month, one-bedrooms at $2,871, and two-bedrooms range from $3,208 to $5,482 as of Q3 2026. The property occasionally offers promotions such as up to four weeks free on select studios.

### Does 580 Anton allow pets?
Yes. 580 Anton is pet-friendly and includes an on-site pet grooming salon. Contact the leasing office for specific breed restrictions, weight limits, and monthly pet rent amounts.

### What is included in rent at 580 Anton?
Rent covers the apartment unit with in-unit washer/dryer, air conditioning, and access to all building amenities including the rooftop pool, fitness center, and business center. Parking, pet fees, and utilities are typically additional.

### How does 580 Anton compare to 3400 Avenue of the Arts?
3400 Avenue of the Arts offers lower starting rent ($2,204 vs $2,659 for studios) and significantly more amenities including a rock climbing center, movie theater, two saltwater pools, amphitheater, basketball court, and complimentary fitness classes. Both are in South Coast Metro within walking distance of the same attractions.

### Is 580 Anton walking distance to South Coast Plaza?
Yes. South Coast Plaza is within walking distance of 580 Anton. The mall is roughly a 10 to 15 minute walk depending on which entrance you use. Segerstrom Center for the Arts is even closer.

### What is the parking situation at 580 Anton?
580 Anton has a parking garage. Contact the leasing office for current parking rates and availability, as costs vary by spot type (covered vs uncovered, assigned vs first-come).

### Are there cheaper apartments near South Coast Metro?
Yes. 3400 Avenue of the Arts offers studios from $2,204 in the same district, saving over $400 per month compared to 580 Anton. Other Costa Mesa communities at various price points are also available along the Bristol Street and Anton Blvd corridors.

### Does 580 Anton have a gym?
Yes. The fitness center includes cardio and strength equipment. It does not include group fitness classes. By comparison, 3400 Avenue of the Arts offers complimentary fitness classes in addition to standard gym equipment.

### What are the move-in costs at 580 Anton?
Move-in costs typically include first month's rent, a security deposit (often equal to one month's rent), and any applicable pet deposits. Contact the leasing office for the exact current move-in package, as promotions may reduce upfront costs.

### Is Costa Mesa a good area to rent in Orange County?
Costa Mesa offers a strong combination of employment (64,000+ daily workers in South Coast Metro), culture (Segerstrom Center, OCMA), shopping (South Coast Plaza), and location (5 minutes from John Wayne Airport, 10 minutes from the beach). Rents are lower than Newport Beach but higher than Fountain Valley or Santa Ana. For renters who work in the South Coast Metro district, living in Costa Mesa eliminates the commute entirely.
