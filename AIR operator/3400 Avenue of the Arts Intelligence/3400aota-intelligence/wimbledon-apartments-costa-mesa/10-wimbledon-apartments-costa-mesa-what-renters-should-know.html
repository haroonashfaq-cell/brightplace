Wimbledon Glen Apartments Costa Mesa: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Wimbledon Glen sits at 1142 Buckingham Drive in Costa Mesa, offering studios through two-bedroom apartments and townhomes with cathedral ceilings and wood-burning fireplaces. One-bedrooms start at approximately $2,625 as of Q3 2026. The gated community has 138 Yelp reviews, two pools with spas, and sound-insulated walls. But dog owners should know upfront: Wimbledon Glen does not appear to allow dogs on the property.

This guide covers Wimbledon Glen's actual pricing, amenities, and what 236 resident reviews reveal, then compares it to 3400 Avenue of the Arts, another South Coast Metro community that allows dogs, starts at a lower price, and adds a rock climbing center, movie theater, and waterfront amphitheater to its amenity list.

How Much Does It Cost to Live at Wimbledon Glen in 2026?

Wimbledon Glen's pricing as of Q3 2026:

Studio apartments: Starting at approximately $2,350/mo

One-bedroom apartments: Starting at approximately $2,625 to $2,765/mo depending on layout

Two-bedroom apartments: Starting at approximately $3,270/mo, reaching up to $3,750 for larger layouts and townhomes

Townhome options: Available with enclosed garages in select floor plans

The community offers covered parking and enclosed garage options for an additional fee. Unit sizes and specific pricing should be confirmed with the leasing office, as availability changes frequently.

For context, the South Coast Metro average one-bedroom rent is approximately $2,894 as of mid-2026. Wimbledon Glen's one-bedroom pricing falls slightly below that average. However, multiple residents note in reviews that the pricing feels high relative to the amenity package. One ApartmentRatings reviewer wrote that Wimbledon Glen is "$200 to $300 more than other complexes with less amenities."

3400 Avenue of the Arts in the same district starts at $2,204 for studios and approximately $2,500 for one-bedrooms (as of Q3 2026). That represents $125 to $265 per month in savings on one-bedrooms, or $1,500 to $3,180 per year, with a significantly larger amenity package.

What Amenities Does Wimbledon Glen Offer?

Wimbledon Glen's amenity package leans on architectural features and a quiet residential atmosphere.

Community amenities: Two swimming pools with spas, controlled-access gate, professionally landscaped grounds, car wash area, covered parking, enclosed garages in select plans, professional on-site management

Unit features: Cathedral ceilings (a signature Wimbledon Glen feature), oversized patios and balconies, walk-in closets, wood-burning fireplaces, sound-insulated walls and ceilings, full kitchens with microwave, dishwasher, deluxe oven, and gas stove

The cathedral ceilings are what most residents mention first. They create a sense of vertical space that standard 8- to 9-foot ceilings cannot match. The wood-burning fireplaces are a genuine rarity in Costa Mesa apartments and add warmth during cooler months (November through March in Orange County).

What Wimbledon Glen does not have: no fitness center or gym, no rock climbing wall, no movie theater, no amphitheater, no basketball court, no game room, no billiards room, no dedicated EV charging, no complimentary fitness classes, no clubhouse, and no dedicated pet park.

For renters who work out, the absence of a fitness center means a gym membership ($50 to $150/mo) on top of rent. For EV owners, no charging means relying on public stations or home installation.

Does Wimbledon Glen Allow Dogs?

This is the most important question for approximately 40% of apartment renters in Orange County.

Based on available listing data, Wimbledon Glen allows cats, birds, and reptiles. Dogs are not listed as an accepted pet type. A maximum of 2 pets are allowed per apartment. Pet deposits and monthly pet rent apply.

If you have a dog, Wimbledon Glen may not be an option. Confirm the current policy directly with the leasing office before applying, as policies can change.

3400 Avenue of the Arts is pet-friendly and welcomes dogs. The community includes a dedicated outdoor pet park where dogs can exercise and play off-leash. For dog owners in the South Coast Metro area, this is a meaningful differentiator.

What Are Renters Saying About Wimbledon Glen?

Wimbledon Glen has 138 Yelp reviews and 98 ApartmentRatings reviews. Here is the consensus.

What residents praise about Wimbledon Glen:

Staff quality and responsiveness lead the positive reviews. Maintenance is described as friendly, prompt, and thorough. The community atmosphere is consistently called quiet, which matters for renters who work from home or keep early hours. Cathedral ceilings and fireplaces are mentioned as the features that made residents choose Wimbledon Glen over other Costa Mesa options. Freeway access is praised for commuters.

What residents flag about Wimbledon Glen:

The pricing-to-amenity ratio is the top concern. Multiple reviewers on ApartmentRatings describe Wimbledon Glen as overpriced compared to nearby communities with more amenities. Grounds cleanliness draws complaints: "Sappy trees and bird droppings everywhere" appeared in multiple reviews. Guest parking is limited, creating friction when visitors come by. Some residents report noise from neighbors despite the sound-insulated construction.

Overall impression from Wimbledon Glen reviews:

Wimbledon Glen excels at two things: quiet living and responsive staff. The cathedral ceilings and fireplaces create a distinctive home feel. The gap is in amenity depth and pricing value. Renters who prioritize amenity variety, fitness facilities, dog-friendliness, or rock climbing at a lower monthly cost should evaluate 3400 Avenue of the Arts.
