## Cost-of-Living Data Points
- Average 1BR rent: ~$2,400/mo
- Utilities (electric/gas): $100-$200/mo in OC
- Internet: $60-$90/mo
- Parking: $0-$300/mo depending on community
- Pet fees: $25-$75/mo + $250-$500 deposit
- Renters insurance: $15-$30/mo
- Groceries: OC avg $400-$600/person/mo
- Gas: $5.50+/gallon in CA (as of Q3 2026)
- True all-in monthly cost for 1BR: $2,800-$3,500 depending on lifestyle
