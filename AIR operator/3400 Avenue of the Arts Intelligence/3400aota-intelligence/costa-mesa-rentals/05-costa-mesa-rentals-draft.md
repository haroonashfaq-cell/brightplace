# Costa Mesa Rentals: What It Actually Costs to Live Here in 2026

The average one-bedroom apartment in Costa Mesa rents for approximately $2,400 per month as of Q3 2026. But rent is not the full picture. After adding utilities, parking, pet fees, renters insurance, and other recurring costs, most Costa Mesa renters spend $2,800 to $3,500 per month on housing. Knowing the true all-in cost before you sign a lease prevents budget surprises and helps you compare communities accurately.

This guide breaks down every line item that makes up your real monthly cost in [Costa Mesa](https://www.costamesaca.gov/), identifies the hidden fees most renters miss, and shows how to maximize value in the current market.

---

## What Is the Average Rent in Costa Mesa in 2026?

Costa Mesa rent varies significantly by unit type and neighborhood as of Q3 2026:

**Studio apartments:** $2,200 to $3,600/mo. Most concentrated in South Coast Metro.

**One-bedroom apartments:** $2,200 to $3,815/mo. Citywide average approximately $2,400.

**Two-bedroom apartments:** $2,900 to $4,664/mo. Widest price range across neighborhoods.

**Three-bedroom apartments:** $3,200+/mo where available. Limited supply in Costa Mesa.

**Rent trend:** The Orange County rental market has softened slightly from its 2024 peak. Costa Mesa renters in Q3 2026 have more inventory and slightly more negotiating leverage than they did 18 months ago. Fall and winter lease signings typically yield the best deals.

The starting rent is only the first number. Here is what the rest of the monthly cost looks like.

## What Does It Actually Cost Per Month to Live in Costa Mesa?

Here is a realistic monthly budget for a Costa Mesa renter in a one-bedroom apartment as of Q3 2026:

**Rent:** $2,400/mo (citywide 1BR average)

**Electricity and gas:** $100 to $200/mo. Orange County electricity rates are among the highest in the nation. Summer AC usage pushes electric bills to the top of this range.

**Internet:** $60 to $90/mo for reliable service (Spectrum, Cox, or AT&T depending on building).

**Parking:** $0 to $300/mo. This is the cost most renters underestimate. Some communities include one spot in rent. Others charge $100 for surface parking and $200 to $300 for garage spots. In South Coast Metro, parking costs are highest.

**Pet fees (if applicable):** $25 to $75/mo pet rent, plus a one-time deposit of $250 to $500. Some communities charge per pet, so two pets could mean $50 to $150/mo on top of rent.

**Renters insurance:** $15 to $30/mo. Required at most Costa Mesa communities.

**Total estimated monthly housing cost for a 1BR in Costa Mesa:**

- **Without a car, no pet:** $2,575 to $2,720/mo
- **With parking, no pet:** $2,675 to $3,020/mo
- **With parking and one pet:** $2,750 to $3,145/mo
- **With parking, pet, and gym membership:** $2,800 to $3,295/mo

The gap between "$2,400 rent" and "$3,000+ true cost" is $500 to $900 per month in additional expenses that do not appear on the lease.

## What Hidden Costs Do Costa Mesa Renters Miss?

Beyond the obvious monthly bills, these costs catch first-time Costa Mesa renters off guard:

**Application fees:** $35 to $75 per person. Non-refundable. If you apply to three communities, that is $100 to $225 gone before signing a lease.

**Admin or move-in fees:** Some communities charge $100 to $300 in administrative fees on top of the security deposit. These are not always disclosed upfront.

**Utility billing fees:** Some communities use third-party billing services (RUBS, Conservice) that add $5 to $15/mo in processing fees to your utility bills.

**Trash/sewer/water (if not included):** $30 to $75/mo. Some communities include these in rent. Others bill them separately. Always ask.

**Gym membership (if no on-site fitness center):** $50 to $150/mo. Communities without gyms add this to your true cost. Communities with gyms save you this amount every month. [3400 Avenue of the Arts](https://3400-avenue-of-the-arts.brightplace.ai) includes a fitness center with complimentary group classes and a rock climbing center, eliminating gym costs entirely.

**EV charging (if no on-site charging):** $50 to $100/mo at public charging stations. Communities with designated EV parking (like 3400 Avenue of the Arts) save EV owners this recurring cost.

**Lease break penalties:** Typically 2 months' rent ($4,800+). Know this before signing if your employment situation could change.

**Rent increases at renewal:** Budget 3 to 8% annually in the current Orange County market. A $2,400 apartment could be $2,592 at renewal.

## How Can You Lower Your Monthly Cost in Costa Mesa?

**Choose a community with parking included.** Saves $100 to $300/mo. Always ask whether parking is included before comparing rents.

**Choose a community with a gym.** Saves $50 to $150/mo in gym membership fees. A community that charges $50/mo more in rent but includes a fitness center actually costs less overall than a cheaper apartment plus a gym membership.

**Choose a community with EV charging (if you drive electric).** Saves $50 to $100/mo vs public charging.

**Negotiate during fall/winter.** October through February is the slow leasing season in Costa Mesa. Landlords offer concessions (free months, reduced deposits, waived fees) to fill units during this period. The same apartment could cost $100 to $200 less per month if you time your lease start right.

**Consider the Westside.** Westside Costa Mesa rents start $600+/mo below South Coast Metro. If you do not need to walk to work in the district, the savings are substantial.

**Read the fine print on utility billing.** Some communities include water/sewer/trash in rent. Others bill separately. A "$2,300 apartment" with $75/mo in separate utility billing is actually $2,375. Compare true all-in numbers.

**Avoid application fee waste.** Apply to your top choice first. Each application costs $35 to $75 per person.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Costa Mesa Rentals

### How much is rent in Costa Mesa?
One-bedroom apartments in Costa Mesa average approximately $2,400 per month as of Q3 2026. Pricing ranges from $1,800 on the Westside to $3,800+ in South Coast Metro depending on neighborhood and amenities.

### What is the true monthly cost of renting in Costa Mesa?
After adding utilities ($100-$200), parking ($0-$300), internet ($60-$90), renters insurance ($15-$30), and pet fees if applicable ($25-$75/mo), the true monthly cost for a Costa Mesa one-bedroom is $2,800 to $3,500 as of Q3 2026.

### Is Costa Mesa expensive to live in?
Costa Mesa is more affordable than Newport Beach ($600/mo savings) and comparable to Irvine and Huntington Beach. It is more expensive than Fountain Valley or Santa Ana. For renters who want beach access and cultural amenities, Costa Mesa offers the strongest value in coastal Orange County.

### What is the cheapest neighborhood in Costa Mesa?
The Westside has the lowest rents, with one-bedrooms starting around $1,800 per month. The tradeoff is fewer walkable amenities and some industrial areas compared to South Coast Metro or the Eastside.

### Is parking included in Costa Mesa apartment rent?
It depends on the community. Some include one parking spot. Others charge $100 to $300 per month separately. Always ask about parking fees before comparing apartment rents, as this can add $1,200 to $3,600 per year to your housing cost.

### When is the best time to rent in Costa Mesa?
Fall and winter (October through February) offer the best deals. Landlords are more willing to negotiate on pricing, offer free months, or waive fees during the slower leasing season.

### How much are utilities in Costa Mesa?
Electricity and gas typically cost $100 to $200 per month. Internet runs $60 to $90. Some communities include water, sewer, and trash in rent while others charge $30 to $75 separately. Total utilities: $190 to $365 per month.

### Do Costa Mesa apartments allow pets?
Many do, though policies vary. Expect $25 to $75 per month in pet rent plus a $250 to $500 one-time deposit. Some communities do not allow dogs. 3400 Avenue of the Arts has a dedicated pet park and welcomes dogs.

### What should I budget for a Costa Mesa apartment?
Budget your base rent plus $400 to $900 per month for utilities, parking, insurance, and fees. For a $2,400 one-bedroom, budget $2,800 to $3,300 total monthly housing cost.

### Are Costa Mesa rents going down in 2026?
The market has softened slightly from its 2024 peak with more inventory and modest negotiating leverage for renters. Prices are not dropping significantly but are no longer climbing. Fall lease signings offer the best opportunity for savings.
