# Sealofts at Boynton Village: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Sealofts at Boynton Village sits at 600 Sealofts Drive in western Boynton Beach, offering one- to three-bedroom apartments and townhomes starting at $2,095 per month as of Q3 2026. The community delivers updated kitchens, a dog park, and optional private garages in a residential setting. But renters exploring Boynton Beach should compare what Sealofts offers against what else the market has, particularly for walkability and lifestyle amenities.

This guide covers Sealofts pricing, amenities, and resident reviews, then compares it to One Boynton, the mixed-use community on Federal Highway with onsite restaurants and retail.

---

## How Much Does It Cost to Live at Sealofts at Boynton Village?

Sealofts at Boynton Village prices its units as follows, as of Q3 2026:

**One-bedroom apartments:** Starting at $2,095/mo

**One-bedroom with den:** Available (contact leasing for current pricing)

**Two-bedroom apartments:** Available from approximately $2,400/mo

**Three-bedroom apartments and townhomes:** Available up to 1,700 sqft (contact for pricing)

**Optional private garages:** Available for an additional monthly fee

The community ranges from 760 to 1,700 square feet across its floor plans. For Boynton Beach, the starting price of $2,095 sits slightly below the city average of $2,233 as of mid-2026.

For comparison, One Boynton on Federal Highway starts at $2,167 for a one-bedroom but offers units up to 2,000 square feet, including townhomes with open-concept kitchens and direct access to onsite restaurants and retail. The $72 monthly difference between starting prices is marginal, but the lifestyle difference between the two communities is significant.

## What Amenities Does Sealofts at Boynton Village Offer?

Sealofts focuses on residential comfort with a clean amenity package:

**Community amenities:** Swimming pool with sundeck, fully equipped fitness center with cardio machines and weights, expansive dog park, outdoor recreation areas

**Unit features:** Chef-inspired kitchens with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, subway tile backsplashes, open-concept floor plans, wood-style flooring, designer lighting, in-unit washer/dryer

**Parking:** Optional private garages available (uncommon for apartment communities in Boynton Beach)

The unit interiors are the strength. Multiple reviews highlight the kitchen finishes and open layouts as above average for the area. The fitness center and pool cover the essentials.

What Sealofts does not have: no onsite restaurants or retail, no mixed-use walkable component, no resort-style pool with cabanas, no 24-hour gym, no clubhouse or resident lounge. The community is residential and car-dependent for dining and shopping.

## How Does Sealofts Compare to One Boynton?

Both communities serve the Boynton Beach market but deliver very different living experiences.

**Pricing (as of Q3 2026):**

Sealofts 1BR: from $2,095/mo. One Boynton 1BR: from $2,167/mo. Difference: $72/mo ($864/year).

One Boynton offers units up to 2,000 sqft, including townhomes. Sealofts maxes out at 1,700 sqft.

**Amenities:**

Sealofts has a pool, fitness center, dog park, and optional garages.

One Boynton has resort-style pools, a 24-hour gym, a clubhouse/resident lounge, and, critically, onsite restaurants and retail including Sweetwater Bar and Grill, Baciami Italiano, Pure Barre, Sushi Simon, and Prime Catch within walking distance.

**Walkability:**

This is the biggest difference. One Boynton is a mixed-use community on Federal Highway. Residents walk to restaurants, retail, and services without a car. Sealofts is in a residential pocket of western Boynton Beach where a car is required for almost everything.

**Pet policy:**

Sealofts allows 2 pets per apartment with a 50 lb weight limit. One Boynton also allows 2 pets (contact leasing for weight limit specifics). Renters with larger dogs should confirm policies before applying at either community.

**Unit quality:**

Both offer quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances. Sealofts adds subway tile backsplashes and designer lighting. One Boynton offers townhomes with entertaining spaces, private balconies, and proximity to retail.

**Location:**

Sealofts: western Boynton Beach, near I-95 and Congress Avenue shopping. One Boynton: Federal Highway corridor, downtown Boynton Beach, near Oceanfront Park and the Intracoastal.

## What Are Renters Saying About Sealofts at Boynton Village?

Sealofts has a 4 out of 5 star rating with 27 reviews on Yelp and 8 reviews on ApartmentRatings.

**What residents like:** Kitchen quality comes up repeatedly. The quartz countertops, subway tile, and overall design are praised as above average for the price point. Floor plan layouts get positive mentions for feeling spacious. The dog park is appreciated by pet owners. Several residents note the quiet, residential atmosphere.

**What residents flag:** Gate security functionality is a recurring theme. Multiple reviews mention the community gates being broken or not working consistently. Maintenance responsiveness varies by review, with some noting delays on non-emergency requests. The location's car dependence is mentioned by residents who wish they could walk to more.

**Overall impression:** Sealofts delivers on unit quality and a quiet residential feel. The concerns center on community infrastructure (gates) and the reality that you need a car for daily life. Renters who prioritize walkability and lifestyle amenities should compare One Boynton's mixed-use offering.

## What Makes Boynton Beach a Good Place to Rent?

Boynton Beach sits on South Florida's Gold Coast between Delray Beach (10 minutes north) and Boca Raton (15 minutes south), offering a more affordable entry point to the Palm Beach County coastal lifestyle.

**Cost of living:** The average Boynton Beach rent of $2,233 is meaningfully lower than Delray Beach ($2,600+) and Boca Raton ($2,800+). Renters get coastal proximity without coastal pricing.

**Dining:** The Federal Highway corridor has a deep, varied restaurant scene. Independent restaurants cover Caribbean, Latin American, Italian, Middle Eastern, and Asian cuisine at prices that would be impossible a few miles south.

**Beach and waterfront:** Oceanfront Park and the Boynton Inlet provide public beach access. The Intracoastal Waterway runs through the city.

**Transit:** I-95 is nearby for north-south commuting. The Brightline station in nearby Boca Raton offers train service to Miami, Fort Lauderdale, and West Palm Beach for car-free commuters.

**Growth:** Boynton Beach's Community Redevelopment Area (CRA) is actively investing in the downtown and Federal Highway corridors, bringing new retail, restaurants, and public improvements.
