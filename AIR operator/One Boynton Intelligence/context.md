## Key Highlights
- Renovated 1, 2, and 3-bedroom apartments + townhomes (789-2,000 sq ft)
- Starting at $2,167/mo
- Mixed-use community with onsite restaurants and retail
- Resort-style swimming pools
- 24-hour gym
- Clubhouse
- Quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances
- Walking distance to neighborhood dining and entertainment
- Pet-friendly

## Location
- Downtown Boynton Beach, FL — along Federal Highway
- Walkable to Sweetwater Bar and Grill, Baciami Italiano, Pure Barre, Sushi Simon, Prime Catch
- Near I-95 and major South Florida corridors
- Between West Palm Beach and Boca Raton

## Differentiators
- Mixed-use community: restaurants and retail onsite (live-work-play)
- Townhome options (not just apartments)
- Walking distance to dining — no car needed for everyday errands
- Boynton Beach: more affordable than Boca Raton or Palm Beach with similar coastal lifestyle
- Up to 2,000 sq ft units (spacious for South Florida)
