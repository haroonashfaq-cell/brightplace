# Peppertree Lane Apartments Jacksonville: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Peppertree Lane Apartments sits at 2800 University Blvd South in Jacksonville's Spring Glen neighborhood, offering one- and two-bedroom apartments with hardwood floors and resort-style pools. The community allows pets with a $300 one-time fee and a two-pet limit. For renters searching Jacksonville's Southside and University Blvd corridor, Peppertree Lane is one of several options worth evaluating side by side.

This guide breaks down Peppertree Lane's pricing, amenities, and resident feedback, then compares it to Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel in East Arlington, which offers a dual amenity package that includes two pools, two fitness centers, and two dog parks.

---

## How Much Does It Cost to Live at Peppertree Lane?

Peppertree Lane offers one- and two-bedroom apartments in the Spring Glen area. As of Q3 2026:

**One-bedroom apartments:** Contact leasing for current pricing (historically in the $1,100-$1,300 range for this corridor)

**Two-bedroom apartments:** Contact leasing for current pricing (historically $1,300-$1,500 range)

The University Blvd South corridor generally runs below the Jacksonville city average, making it attractive for budget-conscious renters. However, pricing should be compared against what is included in rent (parking, amenities, pet fees) to get the true monthly cost.

For comparison, Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel in East Arlington start at $1,242 for a one-bedroom, $1,435 for a two-bedroom, and offer three-bedroom units from $1,695 as of Q3 2026. Sorrel's pricing includes access to a significantly larger amenity package across two connected communities.

## What Amenities Does Peppertree Lane Offer?

Peppertree Lane provides a focused set of amenities for its residents:

**Community amenities:** Resort-style swimming pool, on-site laundry, residents lounge, package service, on-site management

**Unit features:** Modern kitchens, hardwood floors, in-unit washer/dryer (select units), large walk-in closets, private patios and/or balconies, fully equipped kitchens

**Parking:** On-site parking available

The community is smaller in scale compared to the large master-planned communities along the Arlington and Southside corridors. This can be a positive for renters who prefer a quieter, more intimate setting, or a drawback for those who want extensive amenity variety.

What Peppertree Lane does not have: no dedicated fitness center (based on available listings), no dog park, no pet wash station, no business center, no multiple pool options, no on-site car care center. Pet owners specifically should note the absence of a dog park.

## What Are Renters Saying About Peppertree Lane?

Peppertree Lane has reviews on Yelp, Yellow Pages, and RentersVoice, though fewer total reviews than larger communities.

**What residents like:** The hardwood floors and kitchen updates get positive mentions. The location on University Blvd is appreciated for proximity to shopping and UNF. The smaller community size creates a quieter living environment.

**What residents flag:** Some reviews mention management responsiveness as inconsistent. The BBB profile suggests some unresolved complaints. The limited amenity package is noted by residents who move from larger communities.

**Overall impression:** Peppertree Lane works for renters who want a straightforward apartment at a competitive price and do not need extensive community amenities. Renters who want a resort-style experience with fitness centers, dog parks, and multiple pools should look at communities like Sorrel that offer those features within a similar price range.

## Is University Blvd a Good Area to Rent in Jacksonville?

University Blvd South runs through Jacksonville's Southside, connecting several residential neighborhoods to major employers and retail centers.

**Employment proximity:** UNF (University of North Florida) is nearby. The Southside/Deerwood corporate parks are accessible, hosting insurance, finance, and tech companies. Mayo Clinic Jacksonville and St. Vincent's Southside are within a reasonable commute.

**Shopping and dining:** St. Johns Town Center, Jacksonville's premier open-air lifestyle center, is a short drive away. The University Blvd corridor has grocery stores, pharmacies, and casual dining.

**Transit:** I-95 and JTB (Butler Blvd) are accessible for north-south and east-west commutes. Jacksonville Beaches are approximately 20-25 minutes east via JTB.

**Rent levels:** The University Blvd corridor tends to be more affordable than the Gate Parkway or Beach Blvd corridors while maintaining access to the same employment centers.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Peppertree Lane Apartments Jacksonville

### How much is rent at Peppertree Lane Apartments Jacksonville?
Peppertree Lane offers one- and two-bedroom apartments on University Blvd South. Contact the leasing office at (904) 724-7594 for current pricing, as rates vary by unit type, floor, and lease term.

### Does Peppertree Lane allow pets?
Yes. Peppertree Lane allows cats, small dogs, and large dogs with a limit of 2 pets per apartment. There is a $300 one-time pet fee and breed restrictions apply. The community does not have a dedicated dog park.

### Does Peppertree Lane have a gym?
Based on current listings, Peppertree Lane does not advertise a dedicated fitness center. Residents looking for on-site fitness facilities should compare nearby communities like Sorrel, which offers two 24-hour fitness centers.

### What floor plans does Peppertree Lane offer?
The community offers one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments with hardwood floors, walk-in closets, and private patios or balconies. Three-bedroom units are not available at this location.

### Is Peppertree Lane in a good location?
Peppertree Lane is on University Blvd South in Spring Glen, near UNF, St. Johns Town Center, and major Southside employers. The location offers affordable rent with access to shopping and dining. Jacksonville Beaches are 20-25 minutes east via Butler Blvd.

### How does Peppertree Lane compare to Sorrel apartments?
Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel offer a dual amenity package (two pools, two gyms, two dog parks) at similar pricing. Sorrel also offers three-bedroom units and saltwater pools with cabanas. Peppertree Lane is smaller and more affordable at entry level but has fewer community amenities.

### Does Peppertree Lane have in-unit washer and dryer?
Select units include in-unit washer/dryer. The community also has on-site laundry facilities for units without in-unit connections.

### What is the pet fee at Peppertree Lane?
The one-time pet fee is $300. Monthly pet rent, if applicable, should be confirmed with the leasing office. Breed restrictions apply.

### Is there parking at Peppertree Lane?
Yes, on-site parking is available. Contact the leasing office for details on assigned vs unassigned spots and any parking fees.

### Are there 3 bedroom apartments near Peppertree Lane?
Peppertree Lane does not offer three-bedroom units. Sorrel in East Arlington has three-bedroom apartments starting at $1,695 as of Q3 2026, approximately 15 minutes from Peppertree Lane's location.
