3 Bedroom Apartments Near Jacksonville Beach: East Arlington Options for 2026

Three-bedroom apartments are hard to find across Jacksonville, and they are even harder to find near the beach. East Arlington, approximately 15 minutes from Jacksonville Beach via Beach Blvd, is one of the few corridors where 3BR apartment options exist at competitive pricing. Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel offer three-bedrooms starting at $1,695 per month as of Q3 2026, with two saltwater pools, two dog parks with agility equipment, and a volleyball court across a dual-community campus.

Gate Parkway / Town Center: $1,800 to $2,200/mo. Near employers but further from the beach (20 min).

Southside Blvd: $1,600 to $2,000/mo. Mid-corridor option between Gate Parkway and Arlington.

Downtown / Riverside: $2,000 to $2,800/mo. Very limited 3BR apartment supply.

Westside: $1,400 to $1,800/mo. Most affordable but 30+ minutes from the beach.

Beaches: $2,200 to $3,000+/mo. Extremely limited 3BR apartment inventory. Most 3BR rentals at the beach are houses.

East Arlington delivers the best combination of 3BR availability, competitive pricing, beach proximity, and amenity depth.

3BR Apartment vs 3BR House Rental Near the Beach

3BR apartment at Sorrel: $1,695-$1,829/mo. Dual amenity package included. Zero maintenance. 15 min from beach.

3BR house rental near the beach: $2,200-$3,000+/mo. No shared amenities (no pool, no gym). Full tenant maintenance (lawn, HVAC, plumbing). On or near the beach.

The value math: Choosing Sorrel's 3BR over a beach house rental saves $500 to $1,300 per month. Over a 12-month lease, that is $6,000 to $15,600 in savings. The 15-minute beach drive is the tradeoff.

For families who visit the beach on weekends but do not need to be on the sand daily, the apartment option delivers dramatically better value with resort-style amenities that a house rental cannot match.
