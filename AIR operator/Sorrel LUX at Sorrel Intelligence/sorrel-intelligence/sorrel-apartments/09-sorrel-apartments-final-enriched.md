# Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel Jacksonville: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel are sister apartment communities in East Arlington, [Jacksonville](https://www.coj.net/), sharing a dual amenity package that includes two resort-style saltwater pools with cabanas, two 24-hour fitness centers, two dog parks with agility obstacles, and two resident lounges. One-bedrooms start at $1,242, two-bedrooms at $1,435, and three-bedrooms at $1,695 as of Q3 2026. The communities sit approximately 15 minutes from Jacksonville's beaches, offering a suburban setting with coastal access.

No other apartment community in Jacksonville has two of every major amenity. This guide covers what the dual package actually includes, what it costs, and who Sorrel fits best.

## How Much Does It Cost to Live at Sorrel in 2026?

Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel price their apartments competitively for the East Arlington market:

**One-bedroom apartments:** $1,242 to $1,430/mo (plans 1A, 1B, 1C)

**Two-bedroom apartments:** $1,435 to $1,613/mo (plans 2A, 2B, 2C, approximately 960 sqft)

**Three-bedroom apartments:** $1,695 to $1,829/mo (plans 3A20, 3A30)

**Current promotion (as of Q3 2026):** Up to one month free on select units. Conditions apply.

**True monthly cost estimate (1BR):**
- Rent: $1,242
- Utilities (electric, water/sewer): $100 to $175
- Internet: $60 to $80
- Renters insurance: $15 to $25
- **Total: approximately $1,417 to $1,522/mo**

For Jacksonville, this is below the city average of approximately $1,450 for a one-bedroom. The pricing becomes even more competitive when you factor in the dual amenity package, which eliminates the need for a gym membership ($50 to $150/mo saved).

## What Is the Dual Amenity Package?

This is Sorrel's defining feature. As sister communities, Sorrel and LUX at Sorrel share their amenities, which means residents of either community access twice the standard amenity count.

**Two resort-style saltwater pools with sundecks and cabanas.** Not chlorine pools. Saltwater is gentler on skin and hair, and the two-pool setup means less crowding on weekends and holidays. Each pool has its own sundeck and cabana area.

**Two 24-hour fitness centers.** If one gym is busy, walk to the other. Both operate around the clock. For renters who work shifts or prefer early-morning or late-night workouts, 24-hour access across two locations is a practical advantage.

**Two expansive dog parks with agility obstacles.** Not just fenced grass. Sorrel's dog parks include agility equipment for dogs who need more than a patch of ground. Two parks mean dedicated spaces even when the community is full.

**Pet wash station / pet spa.** After the dog park or a rainy walk, the wash station saves your apartment floors and bathtub.

**Two resident lounges.** Each with billiards, gaming, and media center. Social spaces for evenings and weekends without leaving the community.

**Additional amenities:** Outdoor social space with barbecues and games, volleyball court, two car care centers, modern conference room, package lockers.

**What no listing site tells you:** The "two of everything" model means that even at full occupancy, the per-resident amenity access is double what a single-amenity community offers. This is not marketing language. It is a structural advantage.

## What Are Sorrel Residents Saying?

**What residents praise:**

The dual amenity package is the most-mentioned positive. Residents specifically call out the saltwater pools and the dog parks with agility equipment as features that influenced their decision to lease. The East Arlington location provides a suburban, quiet setting while remaining accessible to beaches (15 min) and the Southside employment corridor. Unit finishes, particularly the granite countertops and crown molding, receive positive mentions for exceeding expectations at the price point.

**What residents flag:**

Some buildings show their age. Sorrel is not new construction, and older units may have wear that renovated or Premier units do not. Pest management in older buildings is mentioned in some reviews, consistent with many established Jacksonville communities. East Arlington is car-dependent: there is no walkable retail or dining within the immediate community. Parking in certain areas of the community can be tight during peak evening hours.

**Overall impression:**

Sorrel delivers more amenity value per dollar than any other Jacksonville apartment community. The dual package is genuine, not marketing. The tradeoffs are the community's age and East Arlington's car-dependent setting. Renters who prioritize amenity access (especially pools, gyms, and dog parks) over walkable urban living will find Sorrel hard to beat at this price.

## Is East Arlington a Good Area for Renters?

East Arlington sits between Jacksonville's urban Southside and the beach communities. Here is what the area delivers.

**Beach access:** Approximately 15 minutes to Jacksonville Beach, Neptune Beach, and Atlantic Beach via Beach Blvd (US-90). This is the primary lifestyle draw of East Arlington.

**Shopping and dining:** Beach Blvd corridor has grocery stores (Publix, Winn-Dixie), restaurants, and retail. St. Johns Town Center is approximately 15 to 20 minutes south on I-295.

**Employment:** The Southside employment corridor (Deerwood Park, Southpoint, Gate Parkway) is 15 to 20 minutes south. Mayo Clinic and other healthcare employers are accessible via I-295. Naval Air Station Jacksonville is approximately 25 minutes west.

**Schools:** Duval County Public Schools. Multiple elementary, middle, and high schools serve the East Arlington area.

**Highway access:** I-295 (outer beltway) and Beach Blvd provide access to all Jacksonville corridors.

**The tradeoff vs Southside:** East Arlington rents are lower than Gate Parkway/Town Center, and beach access is closer. The Southside has more walkable retail and shorter commutes to corporate employers. Your priority (beach vs employment proximity) determines which is the better fit.
