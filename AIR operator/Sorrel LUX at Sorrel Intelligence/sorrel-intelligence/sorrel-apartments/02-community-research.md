# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Sorrel Apartments

## The Dual Amenity Story
- Sorrel + LUX at Sorrel are sister communities sharing amenities
- TWO saltwater resort pools with cabanas
- TWO 24-hour fitness centers
- TWO dog parks with agility obstacles
- TWO resident lounges (billiards, gaming, media)
- TWO car care centers
- Plus: pet wash station, volleyball court, conference room, package lockers
- No other Jacksonville community has "two of everything"

## Positive Themes from Reviews
- Saltwater pools (not chlorine) praised
- Dog park agility obstacles unique
- Crown molding + pendant lighting elevated for the price
- East Arlington: 15 min to beaches, suburban but accessible
- 3BR from $1,695 — competitive for families

## Negative Themes from Reviews
- Older community (not new construction)
- Some pest mentions in older buildings
- East Arlington less walkable than Southside/Riverside
- Parking can be tight in specific areas

## Key Differentiator
- "Dual amenity package" — this is the HEADLINE. No competitor can match 2 pools, 2 gyms, 2 dog parks.
- Saltwater pools (health-conscious, gentler on skin/hair)
- Agility obstacles in dog parks (not just open grass)
- 3BR available from $1,695 (family option)
