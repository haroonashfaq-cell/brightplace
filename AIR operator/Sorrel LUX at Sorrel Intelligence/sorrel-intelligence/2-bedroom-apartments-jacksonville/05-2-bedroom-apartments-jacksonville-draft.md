## How Much Is a 2 Bedroom Apartment in Jacksonville by Area?

Two-bedroom pricing across Jacksonville as of Q3 2026:

**Gate Parkway / St Johns Town Center:** $1,600 to $2,200/mo. Closest to Southside employers and Town Center shopping. Premium for the corridor.

**Southside Blvd / Baymeadows:** $1,400 to $1,900/mo. Established suburban corridor with grocery stores and restaurants.

**East Arlington (Sorrel's area):** $1,300 to $1,700/mo. Beach access in 15 minutes. More affordable than Gate Parkway with similar unit sizes.

**Downtown / Riverside / San Marco:** $1,500 to $2,200/mo. Walkable urban living. Limited 2BR apartment supply.

**Westside / Orange Park:** $1,100 to $1,500/mo. Most affordable but longest commutes to Southside employers and beaches.

**Jacksonville Beaches:** $1,600 to $2,400/mo. Ocean proximity premium. Limited 2BR apartment inventory.

For renters who want a two-bedroom with full amenities and beach access without premium pricing, East Arlington delivers the strongest value.

## What Should You Look for in a Jacksonville 2 Bedroom?

**In-unit washer and dryer.** A two-bedroom household generates more laundry than a single person. In-unit machines are not a luxury at this point. They are practical. Sorrel includes in-home washer/dryer in every unit.

**Square footage above 900 sqft.** Some Jacksonville 2BRs compress to 800 sqft, which makes the second bedroom feel like a large closet. Sorrel's 2BR at approximately 960 sqft provides functional space in both bedrooms.

**Pool access.** Jacksonville heat runs from April through October. Two adults sharing a 2BR will both use the pool. Sorrel has two saltwater pools, meaning less crowding and no chlorine.

**Dog park (if you have a pet).** Two-bedroom households are more likely to have a dog. A fenced, on-site dog park saves daily drives to public parks. Sorrel has two dog parks with agility equipment.

**Parking for two cars.** A 2BR household typically has two vehicles. Verify parking availability and fees before signing. Some communities charge per spot.
