# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Citigate Apartments Jacksonville

## Sources: ApartmentRatings (275 reviews), Yelp, Apartments.com, ForRent, RentCafe

## Positive Themes
- **Staff:** Leasing office praised, especially Sara Gomez for professionalism. Staff described as friendly and willing to assist.
- **Pool/dive-in theater:** The poolside movie screening is the most-mentioned unique amenity.
- **Spacious apartments:** Floor plans described as large with good layouts.
- **Move-in experience:** Smooth and well-organized.
- **Pricing:** Described as reasonable for the Gate Parkway corridor.
- **Spin studio:** Dedicated cycling space gets positive mentions from fitness-oriented residents.

## Negative Themes
- **Gate never works:** "A gated community with a gate that never works" — recurring complaint across multiple reviews.
- **Parking at night:** "Parking is horrible in the evening/night, having to park far away from actual building."
- **Trash/cleanliness:** "Attention to cleanliness and trash management remains a concern." Litter in common areas.
- **Maintenance speed:** Some delays reported on non-emergency requests.
- **Rule enforcement:** Some residents frustrated with inconsistent enforcement of community rules.

## Key Data for Article
- 275 ApartmentRatings reviews = largest review sample of all 10 communities
- Pet deposit: $400 (1 pet) / $600 (2 pets). Monthly: $25/$50. Breed restrictions.
- 1BR from $1,446, 3BR up to $2,002 — competitive for Gate Parkway
- Dive-in theater + fire lounge + spin studio = unique amenity trifecta nobody else on the corridor has
- Crown molding + cyber desks = elevated finish details
