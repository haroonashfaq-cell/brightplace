# Apartments Near Mayo Clinic Jacksonville: A Guide for Healthcare Workers in 2026

[Mayo Clinic's](https://www.mayoclinic.org/) Jacksonville campus draws thousands of healthcare workers, from permanent staff and medical residents to travel nurses and clinical rotation students. The Gate Parkway corridor on Jacksonville's Southside offers the shortest commute to Mayo Clinic's Primary Care Center, with one-bedroom apartments starting at $1,446 per month as of Q3 2026. For healthcare workers pulling 12-hour shifts, commute time and 24-hour amenity access are not luxuries. They are practical requirements.

This guide covers the apartment options closest to Mayo Clinic Jacksonville, what healthcare workers should prioritize when choosing housing, and why the Gate Parkway corridor has become the default for Mayo employees looking for convenience and value.

## How Far Is Mayo Clinic From Gate Parkway Apartments?

Mayo Clinic operates two key Jacksonville locations:

**Mayo Clinic Main Campus:** 4500 San Pablo Road, Jacksonville, FL 32224. The flagship hospital and specialty care campus.

**Mayo Clinic Gate Parkway Primary Care Center:** Located on Gate Parkway itself. Primary care, lab, and radiology services.

From the Gate Parkway corridor, commute times are:

**To Gate Parkway Primary Care Center:** 2 to 5 minutes. Citigate at 8451 Gate Parkway West is on the same road.

**To Mayo Clinic Main Campus (San Pablo Road):** Approximately 15 to 20 minutes via JTB (Butler Blvd) and I-95 or San Pablo Road.

For healthcare workers assigned to the Gate Parkway clinic, living on the corridor means a commute measured in blocks, not miles. For those working the main campus, Gate Parkway still offers a reasonable drive with easy highway access.

## What Should Healthcare Workers Look for in a Jacksonville Apartment?

Healthcare workers have specific needs that general apartment guides miss:

**24-hour fitness center.** Hospital shifts end at 7 AM or 7 PM. You need a gym that is open when you get off work, not one that closes at 10 PM. Communities with 24-hour gyms eliminate the need for a separate gym membership ($50 to $150/mo saved).

**Short commute.** After a 12-hour shift, every minute of commute counts. Living on Gate Parkway puts you minutes from both Mayo locations instead of 30+ minutes from Riverside or the Northside.

**In-unit washer and dryer.** Scrubs go through the wash constantly. Shared laundry rooms are impractical for healthcare workers who change clothes daily.

**Built-in desk or work space.** Charting, studying, and continuing education happen at home. A built-in cyber desk (like those at Citigate) means you do not need to buy furniture for a home office.

**Pet-friendly with a dog park.** Healthcare workers have high rates of pet ownership. A dog that has been home alone for 12 hours needs outdoor space immediately when you get back. A bark park on-site solves this.

**Parking availability at night.** If you work night shifts and come home at 7 AM, you need parking near your building. Ask about overnight parking availability before signing.

## What Is [Jacksonville's](https://www.coj.net/) Southside Like for Healthcare Workers?

The Southside is Jacksonville's commercial and residential hub south of the St Johns River. For healthcare workers, it delivers:

**Employment concentration:** Mayo Clinic, Baptist Health South, Ascension St. Vincent's Southside, and UF Health Jacksonville South are all on the Southside. Multiple healthcare employers in one corridor means career flexibility without moving.

**St Johns Town Center:** 150+ retailers and an extensive restaurant row within a short drive of any Gate Parkway apartment. Grocery stores, pharmacies, and daily necessities are nearby.

**Beach access:** JTB (Butler Blvd) connects the Southside to Jacksonville's beaches in approximately 20 minutes. After a stretch of shifts, the beach is a 20-minute decompression drive.

**Highway access:** I-95 runs north to downtown Jacksonville (20 minutes) and south to St. Augustine (45 minutes). The airport is approximately 30 minutes north.

**Cost of living:** Jacksonville's cost of living is below the national average. Healthcare workers relocating from cities like Boston, San Francisco, or New York find that their salary goes significantly further in Jacksonville.
