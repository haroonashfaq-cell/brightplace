# Best Apartments Near St Johns Town Center Jacksonville: 2026 Guide

St Johns Town Center is the shopping and dining hub of Jacksonville's Southside, with over 150 retailers and a deep restaurant row. Renters searching the Gate Parkway corridor want walking or short-drive access to this center while staying close to major employers like Mayo Clinic and the Deerwood corporate parks. Apartments in the area range from $1,400 to $2,200 per month depending on size and amenities as of Q3 2026.

Citigate at 8451 Gate Parkway West sits in the heart of this corridor, starting at $1,446 for a one-bedroom with a resort pool featuring a dive-in theater, spin studio, fire lounge, and lakeside social spaces.

---

## What Makes the Town Center Area Jacksonville's Most In-Demand Rental Corridor?

St Johns Town Center is an open-air lifestyle center anchored by Apple, Nordstrom, Tesla, Dillard's, and over 150 other retailers. Beyond shopping, the restaurant row includes chains and local spots that draw crowds every evening. The adjacent Markets at Town Center adds more retail and dining options.

**Employment:** The Gate Parkway and Deerwood Park corridor hosts insurance companies, financial firms, IT companies, and professional services offices. Mayo Clinic Jacksonville's Primary Care Center is on Gate Parkway itself. Johnson and Johnson, Ascension St. Vincent's Southside, and UNF are all within 15 minutes.

**Highway access:** I-95 runs north-south. JTB (Butler Blvd/SR 202) connects east to Jacksonville Beaches in about 20 minutes. Downtown Jacksonville is approximately 20 minutes north.

For renters who work on the Southside and want shopping, dining, and healthcare within a 5-minute drive, the Town Center corridor checks every box.
