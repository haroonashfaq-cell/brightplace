Apartments Near St Johns Town Center Jacksonville: 2026 Renter Guide

St Johns Town Center is Jacksonville's largest open-air lifestyle center with over 150 retailers and an extensive restaurant row on the Southside. Apartments within a short drive of Town Center range from $1,400 to $2,200 per month for one-bedrooms as of Q3 2026. The Gate Parkway corridor offers the most options, with communities that pair competitive pricing with resort-level amenities minutes from the shopping and dining.

This guide covers what it costs to rent near St Johns Town Center, what the surrounding area offers for daily life, and which apartment communities deliver the best value for renters who want Town Center access without overpaying.

What Makes the St Johns Town Center Area Attractive for Renters?

St Johns Town Center is not just a shopping mall. It is the social, dining, and retail center of Jacksonville's Southside, and living near it means most of your daily needs are within a 5-minute drive.

Shopping: Over 150 retailers including Apple, Nordstrom, Tesla, Dillard's, Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn, Anthropologie, and Lululemon. The adjacent Markets at Town Center adds Target, Publix, and additional dining.

Dining: The restaurant row at Town Center includes The Capital Grille, Seasons 52, Bonefish Grill, P.F. Chang's, Maggiano's, and a growing list of local restaurants. This is where Southside Jacksonville goes for dinner on weeknights and weekends.

Entertainment: Tinseltown movie theater is nearby. The IKEA Jacksonville store draws visitors from across the region.

Employment: St Johns Town Center and the surrounding retail corridor employ thousands of workers. More importantly, the Deerwood Park and Southpoint business parks adjacent to the corridor host insurance, finance, IT, and professional services companies. Mayo Clinic's Gate Parkway Primary Care Center is on the same corridor.

For renters who work on the Southside and want dining, shopping, and services within minutes, the Town Center area is the default choice.

What Is the Southside Jacksonville Lifestyle Like?

Living near St Johns Town Center means living on Jacksonville's Southside, a distinct lifestyle from downtown or the beaches.

Car-dependent convenience. The Southside is not walkable in the urban sense. But everything you need is within a 5-to-15-minute drive: groceries (Publix, Whole Foods), dining (Town Center restaurant row), healthcare (Mayo Clinic, Baptist South), and retail (Town Center, IKEA, Target). The convenience is car-based but comprehensive.

Beach access. JTB (Butler Blvd / SR 202) connects the Southside to Jacksonville's beaches in approximately 20 minutes. Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach are the destinations. On a Saturday morning, you can be on the sand before the crowds arrive.

Employment hub. Deerwood Park, Southpoint, and Gate Parkway business parks concentrate thousands of corporate jobs within the corridor. Insurance, financial services, IT, and professional services are the primary industries. Living on the Southside often means a sub-10-minute commute.

Nightlife and social. Town Center's restaurant row is the Southside's primary evening destination. The fire lounge and dive-in theater at Citigate provide alternatives for nights when you want entertainment without a drive.

Highway access. I-95 runs north to downtown Jacksonville (20 minutes) and south toward St. Augustine (45 minutes). JTB runs east to the beaches (20 minutes).
