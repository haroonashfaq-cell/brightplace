# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: 3 Bedroom Apartments Jacksonville FL

## Why 3BR Search Is Different
- 3BR apartments are rare in Jacksonville's apartment market (most communities only offer 1-2BR)
- Searchers are typically: families with children, roommate groups (2-3 people), remote workers needing a home office
- Price sensitivity is high — 3BR renters compare against townhomes and SFR rentals
- Amenities that matter: playground (families), parking (multiple cars), in-unit W/D (larger households), pet-friendly

## Citigate's 3BR Facts
- 3BR up to $2,002/mo (as of Q3 2026)
- Up to 1,411 sqft
- Crown molding, quartz/granite, hardwood-style flooring
- Built-in cyber desk (home office ready)
- Full-size in-unit washer/dryer
- Private terrace/balcony
- Bark park + pet wash for families with dogs
- Dive-in theater = family entertainment at home

## Jacksonville 3BR Market Context
- Average 3BR apartment rent in Jacksonville: ~$1,600-$2,200/mo
- 3BR townhome rental: $1,800-$2,500/mo
- 3BR SFR rental: $1,800-$3,000/mo
- Citigate's $2,002 ceiling is competitive with both apartments and townhomes
