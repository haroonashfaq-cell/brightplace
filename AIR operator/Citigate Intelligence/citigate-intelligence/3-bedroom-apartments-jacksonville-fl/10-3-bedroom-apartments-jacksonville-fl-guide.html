3 Bedroom Apartments in Jacksonville FL: Where to Find Space in 2026

Three-bedroom apartments in Jacksonville are harder to find than one- or two-bedrooms because most apartment communities focus their inventory on smaller units. Of the thousands of apartment communities in Jacksonville, only a fraction offer three-bedroom floor plans. Pricing for 3BR units ranges from approximately $1,600 to $2,200 per month as of Q3 2026, depending on location and amenities. On the Southside's Gate Parkway corridor, Citigate offers three-bedroom apartments up to 1,411 square feet for up to $2,002 per month with a dive-in theater pool, spin studio, and fire lounge.

This guide covers where to find three-bedroom apartments in Jacksonville, what they cost by area, and what to prioritize when you need the extra space.

Why Are 3 Bedroom Apartments Hard to Find in Jacksonville?

Most apartment developers build one- and two-bedroom units because they generate higher revenue per square foot. A building with forty one-bedroom units at $1,400 each generates more income than a building with twenty three-bedroom units at $2,000 each, while using the same total square footage.

The result: three-bedroom apartments make up a small percentage of Jacksonville's apartment inventory. When you search for a 3BR apartment, you are competing with more renters for fewer units.

Who needs a 3BR apartment in Jacksonville:

Families with children: A master bedroom, a kid's room, and a playroom or second kid's room. The third bedroom provides space that a 2BR cannot.

Roommate groups (2-3 people): Splitting a $2,000 three-bedroom between three people brings per-person cost to approximately $667 per month, which is cheaper than a $1,400 one-bedroom for each person individually.

Remote workers: A dedicated home office occupies one bedroom. This leaves a master and a guest room or second living space. After 2020, the demand for a third room as a workspace has driven much of the 3BR search growth.

3 Bedroom Apartment vs Townhome vs House: Which Should You Rent?

Jacksonville renters needing three bedrooms have three options. Here is how they compare:

3BR apartment (like Citigate):

Rent: $1,600 to $2,200/mo

Amenities: pool, gym, dog park, social spaces included

Maintenance: handled by management (no lawn care, no repairs)

Parking: on-site, typically included or low-cost

Lease terms: 12-month standard, sometimes shorter

Best for: renters who want amenities and maintenance-free living

3BR townhome rental:

Rent: $1,800 to $2,500/mo

Amenities: varies. Some communities have pools. Many do not.

Maintenance: often your responsibility for interior. Exterior handled by HOA.

Parking: usually private driveway or garage

Lease terms: 12-month standard

Best for: renters who want a house feel with a driveway and two floors

3BR single-family home rental:

Rent: $1,800 to $3,000/mo

Amenities: none (you provide your own)

Maintenance: fully your responsibility (lawn, repairs, HVAC, etc.)

Parking: private driveway and garage

Lease terms: 12-month standard, sometimes flexible

Best for: renters who want maximum space and privacy, and do not mind handling maintenance

The value case for a 3BR apartment: At Citigate, $2,002 per month includes a dive-in theater, spin studio, fire lounge, 24-hour gym, bark park, and zero maintenance responsibility. A townhome or house at the same price offers more private space but no amenities and full maintenance burden.

What Should You Look for in a Jacksonville 3 Bedroom Apartment?

In-unit washer and dryer. Non-negotiable for a 3BR household. Three bedrooms means more people, more laundry. Shared laundry rooms are impractical. Citigate includes full-size machines in every unit.

Parking for multiple cars. A 3BR with roommates or a family likely has 2 to 3 cars. Ask about parking availability and fees before signing. Some communities charge per spot.

Storage space. Three bedrooms worth of belongings need closet space. Look for walk-in closets and ask about additional storage options.

Pet-friendly with a dog park. Families with three bedrooms often have a dog. A bark park eliminates the need to drive to a public park for daily dog exercise.

Noise insulation. More people in the unit means more noise going in both directions. Ask about wall construction and read reviews for noise complaints.

Proximity to schools (if applicable). If the third bedroom is for a child, check school district assignments. Jacksonville's Duval County Public Schools vary significantly by zone.
