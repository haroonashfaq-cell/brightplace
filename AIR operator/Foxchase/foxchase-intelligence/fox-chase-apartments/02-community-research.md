## Pain Points (by frequency)

### Tier 1 (30%+ of negative reviews)
- **Pest infestations:** Mice weekly, roaches when it rains, holes behind dishwashers, ineffective pest control
- **Management responsiveness:** Emails/calls ignored, "feel like a number not a client"
- **Rent increases:** Up to $300/mo increase (~25% over 4 years), 15% single-year jumps

### Tier 2 (15-30%)
- **Parking:** No spots after 8 PM, $40/mo reserved parking sign removed and car towed, no cameras
- **Thin walls:** "Paper thin," hear every neighbor conversation
- **Move-out charges:** $130 cleaning + $294.67 painting charged regardless of condition, 60-day notice enforced strictly

### Tier 3 (notable)
- **Security:** Front door broken for months, police say empty units left unlocked, no parking cameras
- **Aging infrastructure:** "Dirty, old and worn" for non-Premier units

## Common Misconceptions

1. **"Foxchase is walkable to Metro"** — King Street Metro is 737 yards from one edge, but complex spans 88 acres. Shuttle runs weekday AM/PM only. DASH Route 30 is more reliable.
2. **"Seminary Hill is walkable"** — Only near Duke Street. Most of neighborhood is car-dependent.
3. **"Renovated units = whole complex updated"** — Only "Premier" units renovated. Standard units are original condition. Premier adds $125-$325/mo.
4. **"Foxchase is unsafe"** — Seminary Hill violent crime gets an A grade (88th percentile), 56% below national average. Property crime is C+ (average).
5. **"It's cheap so it must be terrible"** — Real tradeoff: older buildings + pest challenges + corporate management friction, but $500-900 cheaper than Arlington for double the space.

## Top Resident Advice

1. Ask to see the ACTUAL unit, not a model
2. Document everything at move-in (photos of every wall, floor, appliance)
3. Budget for parking separately ($35-$80/mo)
4. Request top-floor, interior-facing unit for noise
5. Use DASH bus Route 30, not just the shuttle
6. Premier upgrade worth it if budget allows
7. Set calendar reminders for 60-day notice
8. Port City Brewing is the neighborhood gem ($9 tour, 6 drinks)
