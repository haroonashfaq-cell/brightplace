# What Renters Should Know About Foxchase Apartments in Alexandria, VA

Foxchase Apartments covers 88 wooded acres in Seminary Hill, Alexandria, making it the largest apartment community in the city. Managed by AIR Communities, the property includes 2,113 units across garden-style apartments and townhomes. Rents start at $1,487 for a one-bedroom and $2,159 for a two-bedroom as of Q3 2026, running $500 to $900 less per month than comparable units across the river in Arlington.

That price gap draws government workers commuting to the Pentagon, healthcare staff at Inova Alexandria Hospital three minutes away, and families who need space without paying Arlington premiums. But Foxchase is not luxury housing. The community trades newer finishes (in standard units) and walkability for square footage, green space, and amenity density that smaller Alexandria complexes cannot match.

## How Much Does It Cost to Live at Foxchase in 2026?

One-bedroom apartments at Foxchase start at $1,487 per month for The Washington floor plan (460 sq ft), while two-bedroom townhomes top out around $2,484 per month for The Jefferson Townhome Premier (1,050 sq ft) as of Q3 2026. Actual monthly costs run $200 to $350 higher than base rent once you factor in fees most listing pages leave out.

Here is every floor plan with current starting rents (as of Q3 2026):

**The Washington (1BR/1BA, 460 sq ft):** $1,487/mo. Standard finishes. Smallest and most affordable unit.

**The Lee (1BR/1BA, 580 sq ft):** $1,534/mo. Standard finishes. 120 sq ft more than The Washington for $47 extra.

**The Washington Premier (1BR/1BA, 460 sq ft):** $1,612/mo. Renovated with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, vinyl wood flooring.

**The Lee Premier (1BR/1BA, 580 sq ft):** $1,688/mo. Same Premier upgrades in the larger one-bedroom layout.

**The Jefferson (2BR/2BA, 1,050 sq ft):** $2,159/mo. Standard two-bedroom apartment.

**The Gadsby (2BR/2.5BA, 1,180 sq ft):** $2,355/mo. Largest apartment floor plan with half bath.

**The Gadsby Townhome Premier (2BR/2.5BA, 1,180 sq ft):** $2,471/mo. Townhome with in-unit washer/dryer and renovated finishes.

**The Jefferson Townhome Premier (2BR/2BA, 1,050 sq ft):** $2,484/mo. Townhome with private entrance and Premier upgrades.

[Explore all floor plans and current pricing at Foxchase.](https://www.foxchaseofalexandriaapts.com/residences.html)

The true monthly cost goes beyond advertised rent. Budget for parking at $35 to $80 per month, depending on the spot type. Pet owners pay a $250 one-time fee plus $40 per month per pet (maximum two pets). Renters insurance through Assurant is required. Conservice handles water, sewer, trash, and pest control billing separately, adding an estimated $80 to $120 per month (as of Q3 2026) that does not appear in the advertised rent. Storage units run $40 to $55 per month if you need one.

Income requirements sit at 3x monthly rent. Guarantors must earn 5x. A deposit-free option through Obligo is available for qualified applicants, and Foxchase currently offers up to one month free on select units. Lease terms extend up to 24 months, with pricing that varies by term length and move-in date.

## What Is the Difference Between Standard and Premier Units at Foxchase?

Premier units at Foxchase cost $125 to $325 more per month than their standard counterparts (as of Q3 2026), and the difference is visible the moment you walk in. Standard units retain the original finishes: older cabinetry, basic countertops, and carpet or dated flooring. Premier units have been fully renovated.

The Premier renovation package includes white quartz countertops, soft-close designer cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, vinyl wood flooring, and smart home technology including keyless entry and smart thermostats. Townhome Premiers add in-unit washers and dryers, on-suite bathrooms, and closets with custom built-ins.

Resident feedback consistently says the Premier upgrade is worth the cost if your budget allows it. The standard units are described in reviews as "dirty, old and worn," while Premier units feel like a different property entirely. If you tour Foxchase, ask to see the actual unit you would lease, not a model. The gap between standard and [Premier floor plans](https://www.foxchaseofalexandriaapts.com/floor-plan/1-bedroom/the-washington-premier.html) is substantial enough that the model may not represent what you get.

## What Amenities Does Foxchase Offer Across 88 Acres?

Foxchase packs four pools (including a resort-style pool), a hot tub, three 24-hour fitness centers, 8 tennis courts, a dog park, a playground, and a business center across its 88-acre campus in Seminary Hill. Wi-Fi is included in rent, which is unusual for Alexandria apartments at this price point.

The sheer scale separates Foxchase from every other Alexandria apartment community. The next largest property in the area is roughly 22 acres. Walking the full 88 acres of Foxchase takes about 25 minutes on the wooded paths that connect the buildings.

Security features include key fob building access and 24-hour emergency maintenance. The business center and clubhouse provide workspace options for remote workers. Capital Bikeshare stations operate nearby along the Duke Street corridor, connecting the community to Alexandria's broader bike network.

One honest tradeoff: the amenity package is spread across a large campus. You will not be steps from every pool and gym the way you would at a smaller complex. Depending on your building assignment, some amenities may be a 5- to 10-minute walk. [Tour the community amenities](https://www.foxchaseofalexandriaapts.com/community.html) and ask which buildings are closest to the facilities you care about most.

## Does Foxchase Have Townhomes, and How Do They Compare?

Foxchase offers two townhome floor plans, both in the Premier renovation tier: The Gadsby Townhome Premier (2BR/2.5BA, 1,180 sq ft, $2,471/mo) and The Jefferson Townhome Premier (2BR/2BA, 1,050 sq ft, $2,484/mo) as of Q3 2026. Both include in-home washer/dryer, on-suite bathrooms, closets with custom built-ins, and private entrances.

These prices sit well below the Alexandria townhome market average of $2,952 per month (as of Q3 2026). The Gadsby Townhome gives you a half bath for guests plus an extra 130 sq ft over the Jefferson, yet costs $13 less per month. That makes the Gadsby the better value for families or anyone who needs space for a home office.

All Foxchase floor plans are named after Virginia historical figures: Washington, Jefferson, Lee, and Gadsby. The naming reflects the property's proximity to Old Town Alexandria's historical character, about 10 minutes south on Seminary Road.

[View townhome floor plans and availability.](https://www.foxchaseofalexandriaapts.com/townhomes.html)

## Is Foxchase Apartments in a Safe Neighborhood?

Seminary Hill ranks in the 88th percentile for low violent crime according to neighborhood-level data, with violent incidents running 56% below the national average. Property crime sits closer to the national average. On the Foxchase campus itself, residents report mixed experiences with security infrastructure.

Key fob access controls building entry, and 24-hour maintenance staff provides some overnight presence. Common concerns from resident reviews include parking lot camera coverage, front door locks that go unrepaired for extended periods, and empty units reportedly left unlocked between tenants.

Practical steps to maximize personal security at Foxchase: request a unit on an upper floor with interior-facing windows, document every lock and entry point during your move-in walkthrough, and report maintenance issues related to building access in writing. The [Alexandria Police Department](https://www.alexandriava.gov/Police) maintains a community resources page with neighborhood-specific reporting tools and non-emergency contacts.

## How Do You Get to DC and Metro from Foxchase?

The fastest free transit option from Foxchase is DASH Route 30 on Duke Street, which runs all day and connects to multiple Alexandria Metro stations at no cost. Foxchase also operates its own shuttle to King Street Metro, but only during weekday morning and afternoon commute hours.

Foxchase scores a 55 for walkability and a 50 for transit access. Those numbers tell the real story: you can walk to Duke Street for groceries, restaurants, and the bus, but most errands beyond that corridor require a car. Seminary Hill as a whole is car-dependent once you leave the Duke Street strip.

Driving distances from Foxchase put most D.C.-area employers within reach. The Pentagon is roughly 15 minutes north via I-395. Old Town Alexandria sits about 10 minutes south. Washington, D.C. is under 10 miles away. I-395 and I-495 (Capital Beltway) access points are nearby, making Foxchase practical for commuters who drive to work and want lower rent in exchange for a suburban setting.

For transit riders, [DASH bus routes](https://www.dashbus.com/) serve all five Metro stations in Alexandria for free. The [WMATA Metro system](https://www.wmata.com/) connects King Street to downtown D.C., the Pentagon, and Reagan National Airport. Budget for parking ($35 to $80/mo as of Q3 2026) if you own a car, because street parking fills up after 8 PM according to resident reviews.

## What Are Foxchase Residents Saying in 2026?

Across 835+ reviews on major platforms, Foxchase Apartments earns average ratings between 2.8 and 3.0 out of 5. Maintenance response is consistently the highest-rated category. Space for the price, the wooded campus setting, pool access, and proximity to Inova Alexandria Hospital and the Pentagon draw the most praise.

The most frequent complaints, appearing in roughly 30% or more of negative reviews, center on pest management (mice and roaches, particularly in older buildings), management responsiveness to emails and calls, and rent increases at renewal that residents describe as 15% single-year jumps or $300 per month over a four-year period.

Parking after 8 PM is a recurring frustration. Thin walls earn the phrase "paper thin" across dozens of reviews. Move-out charges (covered in the next section) generate some of the most detailed negative feedback.

The honest summary: Foxchase is not luxury, but it delivers strong value for the price. Residents who accept the tradeoffs (older standard units, corporate management friction, pest challenges in some buildings) tend to stay for the 88 acres of wooded space, the four pools, and rent that runs hundreds below Arlington equivalents. Request a top-floor, interior-facing unit for noise. Document everything at move-in with photos of every wall, floor, and appliance. Use DASH Route 30 as your primary transit, not just the weekday shuttle.

## What Should You Know About Move-In and Move-Out Fees?

Foxchase charges a $130 flat cleaning fee and a $294.67 painting fee at move-out regardless of the unit's condition (as of Q3 2026). These charges appear on nearly every final statement according to resident reviews, so budget for approximately $425 in unavoidable move-out costs.

Written notice to vacate must be submitted 60 days before your lease end date. Foxchase enforces this strictly. Late notice triggers financial penalties. Set a calendar reminder at least 65 days out to give yourself a buffer.

The security deposit is refundable. Alternatively, qualified applicants can use Obligo's deposit-free program to avoid the upfront cost. Pet fees total $250 one-time plus $40 per month per pet, with a two-pet maximum and breed restrictions. Renters insurance through Assurant is required before move-in.

Document every scratch, stain, and appliance condition on your move-in day. Photograph everything. Email the photos to the leasing office with a timestamp. This protects you when the move-out inspection happens. The [Foxchase FAQ page](https://www.foxchaseofalexandriaapts.com/faq.html) covers additional leasing details, and [Virginia's landlord-tenant information page](https://www.dhcd.virginia.gov/landlord-tenant-information) outlines your legal rights regarding security deposit deductions and required itemization.

## How Does Foxchase Compare to Other Alexandria Apartments?

Foxchase dominates on scale and amenity count but trails newer competitors on unit condition and building age. Here is how the three closest Seminary Hill alternatives stack up as of Q3 2026.

**Foxchase (2,113 units, 88 acres):** $1,487 to $2,484/mo (as of Q3 2026). Four pools, three gyms, 8 tennis courts, townhomes, Wi-Fi included. Tradeoff: older standard units, pest management challenges in some buildings.

**The Fields of Alexandria (Duke Street):** Studios from $1,495, 1BR from $1,595, 2BR from $1,860 (as of Q3 2026). Studios available (Foxchase has none). Smaller community, fewer amenities. Lower per-unit pricing.

**Sinclaire on Seminary (212 units, built 2022):** Starting at $1,778/mo (as of Q3 2026). Brand new with pickleball court, yoga studio, smart locks. Currently offering two months free on 12- to 15-month leases. Higher per-square-foot cost, no townhomes, far smaller amenity footprint.

Foxchase wins on raw value: 88 acres of wooded space, four pools, townhome options, and included Wi-Fi for less per square foot than either competitor. The tradeoff is building age. If brand-new finishes matter more than space and amenity density, Sinclaire is the obvious alternative. If you need a studio, only The Fields offers that in Seminary Hill.

## Who Is Foxchase Best For?

Foxchase fits specific renter profiles better than others. The property's size, location, and price point make it an obvious match for several groups in the Alexandria area.

**Healthcare workers at Inova Alexandria Hospital:** Three-minute commute. Night-shift parking is available. Townhome layouts offer quiet daytime sleeping space. [Inova Alexandria Hospital](https://www.inova.org/locations/inova-alexandria-hospital) is the closest major employer.

**Defense and government workers:** The Pentagon is roughly 15 minutes via I-395. Mark Center (BRAC-133) is even closer. The USPTO campus in nearby Carlyle adds another cluster of government jobs within a short drive.

**Families needing space:** Townhomes with in-unit washer/dryer, a playground, 88 acres of green space, and [Fort Ward Park](https://www.alexandriava.gov/FortWard) within walking distance. The Gadsby Townhome Premier at 1,180 sq ft runs $481 below the Alexandria townhome average.

**Dog owners:** Secure on-site dog park, 88 acres of wooded walking paths, and Fort Ward Park nearby for longer outings. Pet fees are $250 one-time plus $40/mo (as of Q3 2026).

**Budget-conscious renters who want D.C. metro access:** Seminary Hill rents run $500 to $900 below Arlington for comparable square footage, and free DASH buses connect to Metro.

**Foxchase is not ideal for:** renters who prioritize walkability (Seminary Hill scores a 55), anyone requiring brand-new finishes in a standard unit, or those who prefer boutique-sized communities with hands-on management.

### How much is rent at Foxchase Apartments in 2026?

### Does Foxchase have a pest problem with mice or roaches?

Foxchase contracts pest control services, but residents in standard units report recurring issues, especially during rainy seasons. Ask the leasing office for the pest control treatment history of any specific unit before signing.

### Is Foxchase Apartments safe to live in?

Seminary Hill ranks in the 88th percentile for low violent crime, with incidents running 56% below the national average as of Q3 2026. Property crime sits near average. On the Foxchase campus, key fob access controls building entry. Resident reviews mention occasional concerns about parking lot camera coverage and door lock maintenance.

### What is the pet policy at Foxchase Apartments?

Foxchase allows up to two pets per unit with a $250 one-time fee and $40 monthly pet rent per animal as of Q3 2026. Breed restrictions apply, so confirm your dog's breed with the leasing office before applying. The community includes a secure on-site dog park and 88 acres of wooded walking paths.

### How much is parking at Foxchase?

Parking at Foxchase costs $35 to $80 per month depending on the spot type as of Q3 2026. Unreserved surface parking is cheapest. Reserved and covered spots cost more. Street parking within the community fills up after 8 PM according to resident reviews, so a paid spot may be worth the cost for evening arrivals.

### What is the difference between standard and Premier units?

Premier units at Foxchase include white quartz countertops, soft-close designer cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, vinyl wood flooring, and smart home technology. Standard units retain the original, older finishes. The Premier upgrade adds $125 to $325 per month (as of Q3 2026). Residents consistently recommend the upgrade if budget allows.

### Does Foxchase have townhomes?

Foxchase offers two townhome floor plans: The Gadsby Townhome Premier (2BR/2.5BA, 1,180 sq ft, $2,471/mo) and The Jefferson Townhome Premier (2BR/2BA, 1,050 sq ft, $2,484/mo) as of Q3 2026. Both include in-home washer/dryer, on-suite bathrooms, custom built-in closets, and private entrances with Premier renovation finishes.

### How far is Foxchase from the Metro?

Foxchase operates a free shuttle to King Street Metro during weekday morning and afternoon commute hours. DASH Route 30 on Duke Street provides all-day, fare-free bus service connecting to multiple Alexandria Metro stations. Driving to King Street Metro takes roughly 10 minutes. The community scores a 50 for transit access.

### What are the move-out fees at Foxchase?

Foxchase charges a $130 flat cleaning fee and a $294.67 painting fee at move-out regardless of the unit's condition as of Q3 2026. A 60-day written notice to vacate is required, and late notice triggers financial penalties. Budget approximately $425 in unavoidable move-out costs on top of your final month's rent.

### Is Seminary Hill Alexandria walkable?

Seminary Hill scores a 55 for walkability. The Duke Street corridor offers grocery stores (Harris Teeter, Aldi), restaurants, and bus stops within walking distance. Beyond that strip, most of the neighborhood is car-dependent. Foxchase residents who rely on walking should prioritize buildings closest to the Duke Street side of the 88-acre campus.

### Does Foxchase include Wi-Fi in the rent?

Yes. Foxchase includes Wi-Fi in the monthly rent at no additional charge as of Q3 2026. This is unusual for Alexandria apartments in this price range. The included Wi-Fi covers standard internet access. Water, sewer, trash, and pest control are billed separately through Conservice, adding an estimated $80 to $120 per month.

### Does Foxchase accept Section 8 housing vouchers?

Foxchase has 423 Section 8 approved units as of Q3 2026. Voucher holders should contact the leasing office directly to confirm current availability and any additional documentation requirements. The [Alexandria Office of Housing](https://www.alexandriava.gov/Housing) provides local resources for tenants navigating the voucher application and transfer process.
