## Section 6: Independent Research Findings

**Verdict: NEEDS REVISION (missing angles)**

Independent web searches for "foxchase apartments alexandria va" and related queries revealed several gaps in the brief:

### Missing Data Points

**1. Utility costs via Conservice (IMPORTANT)**
The brief's "true monthly cost" section includes rent + parking + pet + renters insurance but OMITS utility costs. Foxchase bills water, sewer, trash, and pest control through a third-party service called Conservice. Residents report:
- Sewer bills averaging $55/month on small apartments
- A $5/month administrative fee for the Conservice billing
- Total utility add-on estimated at $80-$120/month

This is a major gap in the "true monthly cost" breakdown. The writing agent must include Conservice utility charges to give an honest cost picture.

**2. Section 8 / HUD housing (NOTABLE)**
Foxchase has 423 Section 8 approved units. This is significant context that affects the community's character and price positioning. The community research mentions "Does Foxchase accept Section 8?" as a real question from renters (item #10), but the brief does not address it in any H2 or FAQ. At minimum, add an FAQ: "Does Foxchase accept Section 8 housing vouchers?"

**3. Tennis court count discrepancy**
The brief says "Tennis court" (singular). The property website and multiple listing sites confirm 8 tennis courts. The keyword research file correctly states "8 tennis courts." The brief should say "8 tennis courts" for accuracy and entity richness.

**4. Capital Bikeshare verification**
The brief claims "Capital Bikeshare station on property." Independent web searches did not confirm a Capital Bikeshare station specifically on the Foxchase property. The closest confirmed stations are in the broader Seminary Hill / Duke Street area. The writing agent should verify this claim before publishing, or soften the language to "Capital Bikeshare station nearby."

**5. Walk Score data**
Independent research found Foxchase has a Walk Score of 55 ("somewhat walkable") and a Transit Score of 50 ("good transit"). These specific scores should be included in the transit/walkability H2 for credibility and featured snippet eligibility.

**6. Lease term flexibility**
Foxchase offers lease terms up to 24 months, with pricing varying by lease length and move-in date. This is relevant to the pricing section and is not mentioned in the brief.

### PAA Questions the Brief Missed

From independent SERP analysis and review platform data, these questions surface frequently but are NOT covered in the brief's 12 FAQs:

1. **"Does Foxchase accept Section 8?"** -- Confirmed yes (423 units). Add to FAQ.
2. **"What utilities are included at Foxchase?"** -- Critical for cost transparency. Add to FAQ.
3. **"How much do utilities cost at Foxchase with Conservice?"** -- High-frequency complaint in reviews. Could be combined with #2.
4. **"Does Foxchase have a sand volleyball court?"** -- Confirmed yes (1 court). Minor but adds entity depth.
