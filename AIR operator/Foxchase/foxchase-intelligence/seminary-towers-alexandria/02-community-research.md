# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Seminary Towers Alexandria

## Sources: ApartmentRatings (254 reviews), Yelp, Apartments.com

## Positive Themes
- Pet-friendly with spacious, well-maintained apartments
- Exceptional staff service praised
- Smooth application and move-in
- High-rise with rooftop fitness center views
- Concierge service
- Wide floor plan variety (studios to 3BR)

## Negative Themes
- Pest issues (reported by multiple residents)
- Utility charges: $300-$450/mo reported by residents — extremely high
- High-rise challenges: elevator waits, noise from higher-density living
- Older building infrastructure despite updates
