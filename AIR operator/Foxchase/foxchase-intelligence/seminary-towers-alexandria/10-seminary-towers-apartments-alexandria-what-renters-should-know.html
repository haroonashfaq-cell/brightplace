Seminary Towers Apartments Alexandria: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Seminary Towers at 4701 Kenmore Avenue in Alexandria, VA is a high-rise apartment community offering studios through three-bedrooms starting at $1,452 per month as of Q3 2026. The building features a concierge, two fitness centers (one with rooftop views), a pool, and controlled building access. With 254 reviews on ApartmentRatings, Seminary Towers has extensive resident feedback. The standout concern: multiple residents report monthly utility bills of $300 to $450, which dramatically changes the true cost of living here.

This guide covers Seminary Towers' actual pricing, amenities, and what 254 reviews reveal, then compares the true all-in monthly cost to Foxchase, the garden-style community on 88 wooded acres where Wi-Fi is included and utility costs run significantly lower.

How Much Does It Actually Cost to Live at Seminary Towers?

Seminary Towers' advertised rents look competitive at first glance:

Studio apartments: Starting at $1,452/mo

One-bedroom apartments: Starting at $1,578/mo

Two-bedroom apartments: Starting at $1,989/mo

Three-bedroom apartments: Starting at $2,594/mo

Unit sizes: 450 to 1,295 square feet

These rents appear affordable for Alexandria. But residents report a significant hidden cost.

The utility bill problem: Multiple Seminary Towers residents on ApartmentRatings report monthly utility charges of $300 to $450. In a high-rise building, the HVAC system and shared infrastructure drive utility costs higher than garden-style communities. Several residents describe these charges as unexpectedly high and difficult to control.

True monthly cost comparison (1BR estimate):

Seminary Towers: $1,578 rent + $300 to $450 utilities + $60 to $90 internet = $1,938 to $2,118/mo total

Foxchase: $1,467 rent + $100 to $175 utilities + $0 internet (Wi-Fi included) = $1,567 to $1,642/mo total

The true monthly cost gap: $296 to $476 in favor of Foxchase. The advertised rent difference is only $111/mo, but after utilities and Wi-Fi, Foxchase saves $296 to $476 every month. That is $3,552 to $5,712 per year.

What Amenities Does Seminary Towers Offer?

Seminary Towers provides a high-rise amenity package:

Community amenities: Pool, two fitness centers (one with rooftop views), clubroom, business center with high-speed internet, concierge, controlled building access, elevators

Unit features: Fully equipped kitchens, sleek finishes, pet-friendly

The rooftop fitness center is Seminary Towers' standout. Working out with views across Alexandria is a genuine perk. The concierge and controlled building access provide a level of security and service that garden-style communities cannot match.

What Seminary Towers does not have: no hot tub, no tennis court, no dog park (pet-friendly but no dedicated outdoor space), no Wi-Fi included in rent, no Capital Bikeshare, no playground, no car care center.

Foxchase's amenity comparison: 4 pools (including resort-style + hot tub), 3 fitness centers (24-hour), tennis court, dog park, playground, Wi-Fi included, Capital Bikeshare, 88 wooded acres. More total amenities, different style (campus vs building).

What Are 254 Seminary Towers Residents Saying?

What residents praise:

Pet-friendly with spacious, well-maintained apartments

Staff receives exceptional praise for service and responsiveness

Smooth application and move-in process described by multiple reviewers

Concierge service valued for package handling and guest management

Floor plan variety (studios to 3BR) gives options most buildings lack

What residents flag:

Utility bills of $300 to $450/mo. This is the #1 concern. Multiple residents describe it as the biggest surprise after moving in. High-rise HVAC systems and shared infrastructure billing contribute to these costs.

Pest issues. Several reviews mention pest management concerns. High-rise buildings can have pest challenges that spread between floors through shared systems.

Older building infrastructure. Despite cosmetic updates, some residents note the building's age shows in plumbing, electrical, and elevator performance.

Overall impression: Seminary Towers delivers strong staff service and a convenient high-rise format with concierge. The concerns center on utility costs that significantly increase the true monthly expense and pest management that multiple residents have flagged.

Accept higher utility costs ($300-450/mo) as a tradeoff

What Is Seminary Hill Like for Renters?

Both Seminary Towers and Foxchase are in Alexandria's Seminary Hill neighborhood:

Transit: Free DASH bus (Route 30 on Duke Street). King Street Metro (Yellow/Blue Lines) accessible via Foxchase shuttle or DASH. WMATA Commuter Rail at King Street station.

Employment: Inova Alexandria Hospital (3 min from Foxchase). Mark Center / BRAC-133 (Department of Defense, 10 min). The Pentagon (15 min). Downtown DC (under 10 miles).

Shopping: Harris Teeter on Duke Street. Shoppes of Foxchase. Seminary Plaza (Aldi, Planet Fitness).

Dining: Port City Brewing, La Casa Restaurant, Smoking Kow BBQ (Washington Post top-10 BBQ), Duke Street restaurants.

Parks: Fort Ward Park (Civil War history, trails, picnics). Brookvalley Park and All Veterans Park (creekside, jogging, dog walking).

Old Town Alexandria: 10 minutes. Waterfront dining, boutiques, King Street.
