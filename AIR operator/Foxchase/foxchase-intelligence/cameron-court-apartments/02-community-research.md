# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Cameron Court Apartments Alexandria

## Sources: ApartmentRatings (203 reviews), Yelp, Apartments.com

## Positive Themes
- Floor plan variety and spaciousness praised. Unique layouts.
- Gated setting appreciated for safety and quiet.
- Traditional architectural charm with tree-lined grounds.
- Some long-term residents call it their favorite community across multiple DC-area buildings.

## Negative Themes
- Rising rent: "not nearly nice enough to justify the climbing price point"
- Amenity maintenance: grills and common areas "always broken"
- Leasing office: "completely disorganized"
- Inconsistent management communication
