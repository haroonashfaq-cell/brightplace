Cameron Court Apartments Alexandria: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Cameron Court is a gated, garden-style apartment community at 2700 Williamsburg Street in Alexandria, VA. Built in 1997, the 460-unit community offers one- and two-bedroom apartments and townhomes starting at approximately $2,000 per month as of Q3 2026. The property sits in the Cameron Station neighborhood near the Eisenhower Avenue corridor, with tree-lined grounds and a traditional architectural style.

This guide covers Cameron Court's actual pricing, amenities, and resident experience, then compares it to Foxchase in Seminary Hill, which offers 88 wooded acres, 4 pools, townhomes, and rents starting at $1,487 for a one-bedroom.

How Much Does It Cost to Live at Cameron Court in 2026?

Cameron Court prices its apartments as follows, as of Q3 2026:

One-bedroom apartments: Starting at approximately $2,000/mo

Two-bedroom apartments and townhomes: Starting at approximately $2,200/mo

Unit sizes: 694 to 1,287 square feet across available floor plans

For Alexandria, Cameron Court's pricing sits in the mid-range. The starting rent of $2,000 for a one-bedroom is competitive for a gated community but notably higher than some larger communities in the Seminary Hill and Duke Street corridors.

For comparison, Foxchase in Seminary Hill starts at $1,487 for a one-bedroom (The Washington plan) and offers two-bedroom townhomes from $2,159. That is a $513 monthly savings on entry-level one-bedrooms, or $6,156 per year, with access to a significantly larger amenity package.

What Amenities Does Cameron Court Offer?

Cameron Court markets itself on its gated, garden-style setting:

Community amenities: Gated entry, garden-style grounds with tree-lined drives, on-site management, fitness center, swimming pool, grilling areas, business center

Unit features: Spacious layouts (up to 1,287 sqft for 2BR), traditional charm with modern updates, in-unit washer/dryer (select plans), walk-in closets

Parking: Garage and surface parking available

The gated entry is Cameron Court's primary selling point. The community feel is quieter and more contained than a large campus-style community.

What residents flag about amenities: Multiple reviews mention that grills and amenity areas are frequently broken or poorly maintained. The pool and fitness center are described as adequate but not resort-style. There is no dog park, no pet wash station, no tennis court, and no hot tub.

For renters who want extensive amenity variety, Cameron Court's package is limited compared to larger communities in Alexandria.

What Are Renters Saying About Cameron Court?

Cameron Court has 203 reviews on ApartmentRatings and additional reviews on Yelp and Google.

What residents like: Floor plan variety and spaciousness get consistent praise. Several residents describe Cameron Court as their favorite community after living in multiple DC-area buildings. The gated setting and quiet atmosphere are valued. Some residents highlight the traditional architectural charm and tree-lined grounds.

What residents flag: Rising rent prices are a common concern, with multiple reviewers saying units are "not nearly nice enough to justify the climbing price point." Amenity maintenance is a recurring issue: grills and common areas frequently broken. Leasing office organization has drawn criticism. Some residents report inconsistent management communication.

Overall impression: Cameron Court delivers a quiet, gated living experience with spacious layouts. The concerns center on value relative to rising rents and amenity maintenance. Renters seeking a strong amenity-to-price ratio may find better options at larger communities like Foxchase.

Is Alexandria a Good Place to Rent Near DC?

Alexandria offers DC metro access without DC pricing. Here is what the city delivers for renters.

Transit: Eisenhower Avenue Metro (Yellow Line near Cameron Court), King Street Metro (Yellow/Blue Lines near Foxchase via shuttle). DASH bus is free citywide. I-395 and I-495 provide highway access.

Employment: Inova Alexandria Hospital, Mark Center (Department of Defense), Patent and Trademark Office (Carlyle), National Science Foundation. The Pentagon is 15 minutes away. Downtown DC is under 10 miles.

Cost: Alexandria rents average $1,800 to $2,400 for one-bedrooms depending on neighborhood. Seminary Hill and the Duke Street corridor tend to run lower than Old Town and Carlyle.

Lifestyle: Old Town Alexandria offers waterfront dining, boutique shopping, and a historic Main Street feel. Fort Ward Park (near Foxchase) has trails, picnic areas, and Civil War history. Port City Brewing is Alexandria's original craft brewery.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cameron Court Apartments

How much is rent at Cameron Court Alexandria?

One-bedroom apartments start at approximately $2,000 per month, and two-bedrooms start at approximately $2,200 as of Q3 2026. Units range from 694 to 1,287 square feet.

Is Cameron Court a gated community?

Yes. Cameron Court has a gated entry, which is one of its primary selling points. The community is garden-style with tree-lined drives inside the gates.

Does Cameron Court allow pets?

Yes, Cameron Court is pet-friendly. Contact the leasing office for current breed restrictions, weight limits, and pet fees. The community does not have a dedicated dog park.

How does Cameron Court compare to Foxchase?

Foxchase starts $513 less per month for one-bedrooms ($1,487 vs $2,000), offers 4 pools, 3 fitness centers, a dog park, tennis court, and Wi-Fi included in rent across an 88-acre wooded campus. Cameron Court offers a gated, garden-style setting with a single pool and fitness center.

What is the parking situation at Cameron Court?

Cameron Court has garage and surface parking. Contact the leasing office for current parking fees and availability.

Is Cameron Court near the Metro?

Cameron Court is near the Eisenhower Avenue Metro station on the Yellow Line. Foxchase offers a shuttle to King Street Metro (Yellow/Blue Lines) on weekday mornings and evenings.

Does Cameron Court have a fitness center?

Yes. The community has a fitness center, though reviews describe it as adequate rather than resort-style. Foxchase offers three 24-hour fitness centers across its campus.

What do reviews say about Cameron Court?

Residents praise the spacious floor plans and quiet, gated atmosphere. Common concerns include rising rent prices relative to unit quality, broken amenity equipment (grills), and inconsistent leasing office communication. The community has 203 reviews on ApartmentRatings.

Are there cheaper apartments in Alexandria VA?

Yes. Foxchase in Seminary Hill starts at $1,487 for a one-bedroom, which is $513 less per month than Cameron Court. Other communities along the Duke Street and Seminary Road corridors also offer competitive pricing.

Does Cameron Court include Wi-Fi in rent?

Based on available information, Wi-Fi is not included in rent at Cameron Court. Foxchase includes Wi-Fi in rent, which is an uncommon value-add in the Alexandria market.
