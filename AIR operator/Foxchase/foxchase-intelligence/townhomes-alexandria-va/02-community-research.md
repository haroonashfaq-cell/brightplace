# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Townhomes for Rent Alexandria VA

## Why Renters Search "Townhomes" Separately
- Want multi-level layout (not flat apartment)
- Need in-home washer/dryer (not shared)
- Want private entrance or more house-like feel
- Often have families or pets needing more space
- Willing to pay premium over standard apartments
