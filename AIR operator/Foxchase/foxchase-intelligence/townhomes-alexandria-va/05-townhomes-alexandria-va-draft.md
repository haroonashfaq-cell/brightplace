## Why Do Renters Choose Townhomes Over Standard Apartments?

**Multi-level layout.** Townhomes have two floors, creating physical separation between living and sleeping areas. This matters for couples working different schedules, families with children, or anyone who wants a floor dedicated to bedrooms and a floor dedicated to living space.

**In-home washer and dryer.** Foxchase's Premier townhomes include full-size in-home machines. No shared laundry rooms. No quarters. No carrying baskets down hallways.

**On-suite bathrooms.** Each bedroom in the Gadsby Townhome Premier has its own bathroom access (2BR/2.5BA). Privacy that standard apartments rarely offer.

**Custom built-in closets.** The Premier townhome closets at Foxchase have custom built-ins, not wire shelving. Organized storage from day one.

**More space.** The Gadsby Townhome Premier at approximately 1,180 square feet provides significantly more room than a standard 2BR apartment (typically 800 to 950 sqft).

**A house-like feel.** Two floors, private entrance feel, and more square footage create a living experience closer to a house than a flat.

## Community Townhome vs Standalone Townhome: Which Is Right for You?

**Choose a standalone townhome if you:**
- Want a private yard (community townhomes typically do not have private yards)
- Want a private driveway and garage
- Prefer no shared walls on one or both sides
- Do not mind handling maintenance, lawn care, and repairs
- Budget allows $2,500 to $4,000+/mo with no included amenities
- Want complete independence from community rules

**Choose a community townhome (like Foxchase) if you:**
- Want the townhome layout (2 floors, in-home W/D, on-suite baths) without maintenance
- Want resort-style amenities (pools, gym, tennis, dog park) included
- Prefer 24-hour maintenance on call for any issue
- Want Wi-Fi included in rent
- Budget targets $2,471 to $2,501/mo with a full amenity package
- Want to live on 88 wooded acres with walking paths and mature trees

**The practical tradeoff:** Standalone townhomes offer more privacy and a private yard. Community townhomes offer more amenities, zero maintenance, and lower monthly cost. For most Alexandria renters, the community townhome delivers more value per dollar.
