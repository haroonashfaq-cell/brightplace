# Sinclaire on Seminary Alexandria: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Sinclaire on Seminary is a new-construction apartment community at 4900 Seminary Road in [Alexandria, VA](https://www.alexandriava.gov/), offering studios through two-bedrooms starting at $1,797 per month as of Q3 2026. The building opened in 2022 with modern finishes, a pickleball court, bocce court, and smart lock technology. It is one of the newest apartment buildings in the Seminary Hill area, but with only one review on ApartmentRatings (rated 2.2 out of 5), the resident experience is not yet well-documented.

This guide covers what Sinclaire on Seminary actually offers, what the limited review data tells us, and how it compares to [Foxchase](https://foxchase.brightplace.ai), the established 88-acre community down the road that starts at $1,467 with 4 pools, 3 fitness centers, and Wi-Fi included in rent.

---

## How Much Does It Cost to Live at Sinclaire on Seminary in 2026?

Sinclaire on Seminary positions itself as a premium option in the Seminary Hill area. Here is what each unit type costs as of Q3 2026:

**Studio apartments:** Starting at $1,797/mo

**One-bedroom apartments:** Starting at approximately $2,035/mo

**Two-bedroom apartments:** Ranging from approximately $3,401 to $4,387/mo

The pricing places Sinclaire on Seminary at the top of the Seminary Hill market. For context, the Seminary Hill neighborhood average rent is approximately $1,615 per month (as of Q3 2026). Sinclaire's studio starts $182 above the neighborhood average, and its two-bedrooms reach nearly triple that average.

For comparison, [Foxchase](https://foxchase.brightplace.ai) in Seminary Hill starts at $1,467 for a one-bedroom (The Washington plan). That is $568 less per month than Sinclaire's one-bedroom, or $6,816 less per year. The savings are even larger on two-bedrooms: Foxchase townhomes start at approximately $2,471 versus Sinclaire's $3,401+.

## What Amenities Does Sinclaire on Seminary Offer?

Sinclaire on Seminary leans into newer wellness and lifestyle trends:

**Community amenities:** Wellness-focused gym with yoga room, pickleball court, bocce court, outdoor lounge areas with BBQ, co-working spaces and conference room, off-leash pet park with grooming facilities

**Unit features:** Stainless steel appliances, hardwood flooring, smart lock technology, modern kitchen and bathroom finishes

The pickleball court and bocce court are Sinclaire's standout amenities. Pickleball has surged in popularity nationally, and Sinclaire is one of the few Alexandria apartments to offer it. The yoga room and wellness gym appeal to renters who prefer a curated fitness experience over a standard gym.

What Sinclaire on Seminary does not have that some renters expect: no resort-style pool (verify with leasing), no hot tub, no tennis court, limited review data to confirm amenity quality, and no Wi-Fi included in rent.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Sinclaire on Seminary

### How much is rent at Sinclaire on Seminary?
Studios start at $1,797, one-bedrooms at approximately $2,035, and two-bedrooms range from $3,401 to $4,387 per month as of Q3 2026. Sinclaire is the most expensive apartment option in the Seminary Hill area.

### Is Sinclaire on Seminary new construction?
Yes. Sinclaire on Seminary opened in 2022. All finishes, appliances, and building systems are new. Smart lock technology is included in every unit.

### Does Sinclaire on Seminary have a pool?
Verify with the leasing office. Sinclaire's marketed amenities include a wellness gym, yoga room, pickleball court, bocce court, and outdoor lounge areas. A resort-style pool is not prominently listed.

### What is the pet policy at Sinclaire on Seminary?
Sinclaire charges a $500 one-time pet fee and $50 per month pet rent. Breed and weight restrictions apply. The community has an off-leash pet park and pet grooming facilities.

### How does Sinclaire on Seminary compare to Foxchase?
Foxchase starts at $1,467 for a one-bedroom ($568 less than Sinclaire), offers 4 pools, 3 gyms, a hot tub, tennis court, dog park, townhome options, and Wi-Fi included in rent. Sinclaire offers new construction with pickleball, bocce, and smart locks at a higher price point.

### Does Sinclaire on Seminary have a pickleball court?
Yes. Sinclaire is one of the few Alexandria apartment communities with a dedicated pickleball court. It also has a bocce court.

### Does Sinclaire on Seminary include Wi-Fi?
Wi-Fi is not included in rent at Sinclaire. Foxchase includes Wi-Fi, saving residents $60 to $90 per month.

### What reviews does Sinclaire on Seminary have?
As of Q3 2026, Sinclaire has only 1 review on ApartmentRatings with a 2.2 out of 5 rating. The limited review data makes it difficult to assess the resident experience. Foxchase has 835+ reviews for comparison.

### Is Seminary Hill a good area to rent in Alexandria?
Yes. Seminary Hill offers free DASH bus transit, proximity to Inova Alexandria Hospital (3 minutes), Pentagon access (15 minutes), and Old Town Alexandria (10 minutes). Fort Ward Park provides hiking trails and green space.

### How much can I save by choosing Foxchase over Sinclaire?
A one-bedroom renter saves approximately $568 per month in rent, $60 to $90 in Wi-Fi, and $370 per year in pet costs. Total annual savings: approximately $7,900 to $8,300 for a one-bedroom with one pet.
