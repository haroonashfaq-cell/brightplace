# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Sinclaire on Seminary

## Positive Themes
- Brand new construction (2022) — modern finishes throughout
- Pickleball court and bocce court — trendy amenities
- Wellness gym with yoga room
- Pet park with grooming facilities
- Smart lock technology
- Multiple WFH spaces and conference room

## Negative Themes
- Only 1 ApartmentRatings review at 2.2/5 — too new for reliable review data
- High pricing: studios from $1,797, 2BR reaching $4,387
- Pet fee: $500 one-time + $50/mo (higher than Foxchase)
- Smaller community on Seminary Road (less campus/grounds than Foxchase)

- Sinclaire pet: $500 + $50/mo. Foxchase pet: $250 + $40/mo. Foxchase saves $250 deposit + $10/mo.
- Sinclaire: on Seminary Road, walking distance to nothing notable.
- Foxchase: Seminary Hill, near Inova Hospital (3 min), Duke Street corridor, Old Town (10 min).
