## What Are Income-Based Apartments?

Income-based apartments (also called income-restricted, subsidized, or affordable housing) set rent based on a percentage of the tenant's income, typically 30% of gross monthly income. These are funded through federal programs like Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), and public housing authorities.

**How they work:** If you earn $2,000/mo gross, your rent would be set at approximately $600/mo (30% of income). The government or tax credit program covers the difference between your rent and the market rate.

**Eligibility:** Income limits vary by program and household size. In [Wake County](https://www.wakegov.com/), limits for affordable housing programs are typically tied to Area Median Income (AMI). A single person earning below 60% AMI generally qualifies for LIHTC properties.

**Waitlists:** Demand far exceeds supply. Most income-based apartments in Raleigh have waitlists of months to years. Applying early and to multiple properties is essential.

## Where to Find Income-Based Apartments in Raleigh

**Raleigh Housing Authority:** Manages public housing and Section 8 vouchers. Apply through their online portal. Waitlists may be closed periodically.

**LIHTC properties:** Search through the North Carolina Housing Finance Agency (NCHFA) website or AffordableHousing.com for tax-credit properties in Wake County.

**HUD resource locator:** hud.gov provides a searchable directory of subsidized housing by ZIP code.

**Key areas with income-restricted options:** Southeast Raleigh, Garner, parts of East Raleigh. Fewer options in North Hills, Cary, or downtown where land costs make affordable development difficult.
