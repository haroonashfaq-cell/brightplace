## Why Does New Construction Matter?

Villages at Raleigh Beach is not a renovated older building. It is new from the ground up. Here is why that matters for renters:

**Zero pest history.** Older apartment buildings accumulate pest issues over decades. New construction has no prior tenants, no established pest populations, and no deferred maintenance that creates entry points.

**New appliances and systems.** Every stainless steel appliance, HVAC system, water heater, and in-unit washer/dryer is new and under warranty. No inheriting a previous tenant's worn-out equipment.

**Modern insulation and construction.** Newer buildings have better sound insulation between units, more energy-efficient windows, and updated electrical and plumbing. This translates to lower utility bills and less noise.

**Current design standards.** 9-foot ceilings, granite countertops, wood-style flooring, and built-in desks are standard throughout. These are the finishes you see in $1,800+ communities, delivered here at $1,132.

## What Is Northeast Raleigh Like for Renters?

Northeast Raleigh is one of the fastest-growing corridors in the Triangle region.

**Downtown access:** 13 minutes to Downtown Raleigh via I-440. State government offices, Red Hat/IBM headquarters, law firms, and financial services are all in the downtown core.

**Neuse River Trail:** Nearly 30 miles of paved greenway for running, cycling, and walking. Accessible from the community. This is one of the longest urban greenway systems in North Carolina.

**[Wake County](https://www.wakegov.com/) employers:** WakeMed Health and Hospitals (10 to 15 min), UNC Health / Rex Hospital (15 to 20 min), Wake Technical Community College (5 min).

**Research Triangle Park (RTP):** 25 to 30 minutes via I-40. IBM, Cisco, Fidelity, and hundreds of biotech and pharma companies.

**Shopping:** Midway Plantation, Wildersgrove Shopping Center, Tower Shopping Center, ALDI grocery nearby.

**Dining:** Saltgrass Steak House, El Tapatio, Sopranos Grill, and a growing number of local restaurants along the corridor.

**Museums (via downtown):** NC Museum of Natural Sciences, NC Museum of History, NC Museum of Art, all within 15 minutes.

**Growth trajectory:** New retail, restaurants, and residential construction are expanding along New Bern Avenue and into Knightdale. Northeast Raleigh is where the metro is building next.
