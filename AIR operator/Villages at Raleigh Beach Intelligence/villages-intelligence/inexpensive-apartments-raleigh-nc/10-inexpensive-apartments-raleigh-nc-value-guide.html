Where Should Budget Renters Look in Raleigh?

Northeast Raleigh (best value): The corridor along New Bern Avenue and Capital Blvd toward Knightdale offers the lowest rents in the Raleigh metro while maintaining access to downtown (13 minutes via I-440) and Research Triangle Park employers. New construction is happening here, which means modern units at below-average prices as communities fill up.

Southeast Raleigh: Garner and the I-40 corridor south of downtown offer competitive rents, though with fewer new-construction options and longer commutes to North Raleigh employers.

Knightdale: Just east of Raleigh, Knightdale offers apartment rents 10 to 15% below Raleigh averages with growing retail and dining options.

What to avoid for budget renters: Downtown, North Hills, and the Glenwood South corridor all run $400 to $800 above the city average with limited parking and smaller units.

Is Northeast Raleigh a Good Neighborhood?

Northeast Raleigh is one of the fastest-growing corridors in the Triangle region.

Growth: New retail, restaurants, and residential construction are expanding along New Bern Avenue and into Knightdale. This is where Raleigh is building next.

Shopping: Midway Plantation, Wildersgrove Shopping Center, Tower Shopping Center, and ALDI grocery are all nearby.

Dining: Saltgrass Steak House, El Tapatio, Sopranos Grill, and a growing list of local restaurants along the corridor.

Schools: Wake County Public Schools serves the area. Wake Tech Community College is 5 minutes away.
