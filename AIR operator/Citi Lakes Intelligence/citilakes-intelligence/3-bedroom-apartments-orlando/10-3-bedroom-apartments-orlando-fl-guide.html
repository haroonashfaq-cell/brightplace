3 Bedroom Apartments in Orlando FL: Where to Find Space Near Theme Parks

3BR Apartment vs 3BR House Rental in Orlando

3BR apartment (Citi Lakes): Waterfront on 2 lakes, zero-entry pool, spin studio, dog park, no maintenance. Near SeaWorld/Universal/Disney.

3BR house rental in Orlando: $1,800 to $3,000/mo. No shared amenities. Full tenant maintenance (lawn, pool if any, HVAC, plumbing). May be further from theme parks.

The value case: An apartment community 3BR includes resort amenities, 24-hour maintenance, and zero lawn/pool upkeep. A house rental at the same price offers more private space but requires you to handle everything yourself.
