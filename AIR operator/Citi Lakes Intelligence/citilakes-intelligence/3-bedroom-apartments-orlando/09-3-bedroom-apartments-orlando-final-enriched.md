## How Much Is a 3 Bedroom Apartment in Orlando?

**Downtown Orlando:** $2,200 to $3,200/mo. Limited 3BR inventory. Walkable nightlife.

**Lake Nona / Medical City:** $2,000 to $2,800/mo. Newer construction, suburban.

**Winter Park:** $2,000 to $2,800/mo. Boutique feel, premium pricing.

**Southwest Orlando (Citi Lakes area):** $1,600 to $2,200/mo. Theme park proximity. Waterfront options.

**East Orlando / UCF area:** $1,500 to $2,200/mo. Student-heavy. Demand-driven pricing.

**Kissimmee / Hunters Creek:** $1,400 to $1,900/mo. Budget-friendly, longer commutes north.

Southwest Orlando delivers the best combination of 3BR availability, theme park proximity, and waterfront amenities.
