# 2 Bedroom Apartments in Orlando: Best Value Options for 2026

Orlando's two-bedroom apartment market sits at approximately $1,800 per month on average as of Q3 2026, though the range stretches from $1,300 in budget corridors to $3,000+ in downtown and Lake Nona. The Orlando rental market has softened compared to its 2024 peak, giving renters more negotiating power and more inventory to choose from.

For two-bedroom renters seeking waterfront living without downtown pricing, Citi Lakes in Southwest Orlando offers lake-view apartments with a zero-entry resort pool, dedicated spin studio, and no pet weight limit, starting below the city average.

## What Does a 2 Bedroom Apartment Cost in Orlando by Area?

Two-bedroom pricing varies significantly across Orlando's submarkets as of Q3 2026:

**Downtown Orlando/Thornton Park:** $2,200 to $3,200/mo. Walkable nightlife and dining. Highest density, smallest units per dollar.

**Lake Nona:** $2,000 to $2,800/mo. Medical City employers, newer construction, suburban feel.

**Winter Park:** $1,900 to $2,600/mo. Boutique shops, Park Avenue dining. Older charm with premium pricing.

**MetroWest:** $1,400 to $1,900/mo. Residential neighborhood near theme parks. Strong value.

**Southwest Orlando (I-Drive/SeaWorld corridor):** $1,400 to $2,000/mo. Theme park proximity, International Drive entertainment, and communities like Citi Lakes offering waterfront living.

**East Orlando/UCF area:** $1,500 to $2,000/mo. Student-heavy. Demand driven by university enrollment.

**Kissimmee/Hunters Creek:** $1,300 to $1,800/mo. Budget-friendly but longer commutes to north Orlando employers.

Southwest Orlando delivers the best combination of price, amenities, and location for two-bedroom renters who want waterfront or resort-style living without paying downtown premiums.
