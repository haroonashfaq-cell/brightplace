What Makes Citi Lakes Different From Other Orlando Apartments?

Two lakes with waterfront living. This is Citi Lakes' headline. Most Orlando apartments have a retention pond and call it a "water feature." Citi Lakes sits between two actual lakes. Select units have waterfront balconies and patios with direct lake views. The lakeside gazebo provides a gathering space on the water. A scenic jogging trail circles the lakes. In flat-terrain Florida, waterfront is a genuine premium that most communities cannot offer.

Zero-entry resort pool with private cabanas. Not a standard rectangular pool with a ladder. Citi Lakes' pool has a gradual beach-style walk-in entry, private cabanas for shade, and dedicated lounge seating. This is the type of pool design you find at resort hotels, delivered at an apartment community.

Dedicated spin studio. A cycling-specific room separate from the main fitness center. Most Orlando apartment gyms have a few bikes in a corner. Citi Lakes has a room built for cycling. For renters who cycle regularly, this replaces a cycling studio membership ($150+/mo saved).

No pet weight limit. This is the differentiator that matters most to approximately 40% of renters. Most Orlando apartment communities cap pet weight at 50 to 75 pounds. Citi Lakes does not impose a weight limit. Large-breed dog owners (German Shepherds, Labs, Golden Retrievers, Huskies) are welcome. The pet deposit is $450 (non-refundable) and monthly rent is $20 per pet, which is below the Orlando average of $35 to $50.

Smoke-free property. The entire community is smoke-free. No secondhand smoke in hallways, on balconies, or in shared spaces.

How Close Is Citi Lakes to Orlando's Theme Parks?

This is why most people live in Southwest Orlando.

SeaWorld Orlando: Minutes away. For the thousands of employees at SeaWorld and its affiliated parks, Citi Lakes is one of the closest residential options.

Universal Orlando Resort: Approximately 15 minutes. Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, and the Volcano Bay water park are accessible without highway driving from SW Orlando.

Walt Disney World Resort: Approximately 20 to 25 minutes. Disney's four theme parks, two water parks, and Disney Springs are the region's largest employer.

Orlando Vineland Premium Outlets: Approximately 7 minutes. Premium shopping at discount prices.

International Drive: The I-Drive entertainment corridor with restaurants, attractions, and retail is nearby. This is Orlando's most active visitor corridor outside the parks.

For theme park and hospitality industry workers, Citi Lakes' Southwest Orlando location minimizes commute time to all three major resort complexes.

What Is Southwest Orlando Like for Renters?

Employment hub: Walt Disney World (largest employer in Central Florida), Universal, SeaWorld, and the International Drive hospitality corridor employ tens of thousands within a 15- to 25-minute radius.

Shopping: Orlando Vineland Premium Outlets (7 min), International Drive shopping, The Florida Mall (15 min).

Outdoor recreation: Bill Frederick Park at Turkey Lake offers 200 acres of fishing, camping, hiking, and sports. Lake Eola Park downtown is 15 minutes away.

Commute: I-4 connects north to downtown Orlando. The BeachLine Expressway (SR 528) connects east to Melbourne and the Space Coast.

Groceries: Publix, Walmart, and ALDI within short driving distance.
