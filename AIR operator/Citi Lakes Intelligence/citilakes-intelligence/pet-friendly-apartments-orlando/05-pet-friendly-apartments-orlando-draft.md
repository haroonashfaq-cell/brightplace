## Why Do Most Orlando Apartments Have Weight Limits?

Apartment communities set weight limits primarily for liability and insurance reasons. Larger dogs are perceived (rightly or wrongly) as higher risk for property damage, noise complaints, and bite incidents. Insurance companies charge higher premiums for properties that allow large breeds, so many communities cap weight at 50 to 75 pounds to keep insurance costs down.

**The problem for large-dog owners:** Weight limits do not distinguish between a calm 80-pound Golden Retriever and an energetic 30-pound terrier. A well-trained large dog may be a better apartment resident than a small dog, but the weight cap does not account for behavior.

**Breed restrictions add another layer.** Many communities that technically allow large dogs restrict specific breeds (Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, German Shepherds, Dobermans, etc.). Even if your dog is under the weight limit, breed restrictions can still disqualify you.
