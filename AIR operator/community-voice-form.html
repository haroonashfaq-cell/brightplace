AIR Operator Community Voice

A quick Q&A for the on-site team to power the community page with real, authentic content.

Before you start — this takes about 10-15 minutes. There are no wrong answers. You know things about this community that no website or AI can generate.
  • Talk like you are telling a friend. Specific beats generic.
  • Use real names — "the Publix on Main Street" not "grocery stores nearby."
  • Skip anything that does not apply. Write "N/A" and move on.
  • Keep answers about the place, never about who lives here (Fair Housing).
  • Honesty about trade-offs is welcome. Candor builds trust.
