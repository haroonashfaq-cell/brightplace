# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Verdant Peachtree Creek

## Sources: Yelp, Apartments.com, ApartmentGuide, ApartmentHomeLiving, TikTok

## Positive Themes
- Gated community — security and privacy at this price point is rare
- Vaulted ceilings + hardwood floors as STANDARD features
- Spacious floor plans (742-1,395 sqft for 1BR alone)
- I-85 direct access — commuter-friendly
- Bilingual Spanish-speaking leasing team
- Strong move-in promo: 1 month free + $1,000 gift card
- Near Emory/Oglethorpe/CDC employers
- Resort-style pool

## Negative Themes
- Mildew around windows noted in reviews
- Gate entry problems reported
- Maintenance responsiveness mixed
- Built 2004 — not new construction (but renovated)
- Chamblee/Doraville area perception (changing rapidly with restaurant growth)

## Key Differentiators
- GATED at $1,131 starting price — most gated communities in Atlanta cost $1,500+
- Vaulted ceilings are the signature feature — creates dramatic vertical space
- Fireplaces in select units
- 3-bedroom available (from research)
- Buford Highway international food corridor nearby — Korean, Vietnamese, Chinese, Mexican, Ethiopian
- MARTA Gold Line at Chamblee/Doraville stations
