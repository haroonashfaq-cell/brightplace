# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Trestle Tree Atlanta

## Trestletree Village: Budget option in Grant Park/Ormewood Park
- 2BR from ~$1,112. Basic garden-style.
- Community center (computer lab, laundry, arts/crafts). Renovated 2014.
- NO pool, NO gym, NO gated entry, NO in-unit W/D.
- Near BeltLine Eastside Trail. Near Georgia State, Morehouse, Spelman.
- Reviews: affordable, basic, maintenance mixed.

## Verdant advantage for $78 more/mo:
- Gated community, resort pool, gym, clubhouse with billiards
- Vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors (vs basic finishes)
- Near I-85, Emory, CDC (different employer market)
- The $78/mo gap is the smallest price-to-value jump in the portfolio
