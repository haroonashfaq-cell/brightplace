Trestle Tree Apartments Atlanta: What Renters Should Know Before Signing

Trestletree Village is a garden-style apartment community spread across two sites in Atlanta's Grant Park and Ormewood Park neighborhoods, offering two-bedroom apartments starting at approximately $1,112 per month as of Q3 2026. The community was significantly renovated in 2014, adding new community centers with computer labs, laundry facilities, and security features. Located near the BeltLine and Georgia State University, Trestletree serves renters looking for affordable intown living.

This guide covers what Trestletree actually offers, what renters experience living there, and how it compares to Verdant Peachtree Creek, a gated community near I-85 with resort-style amenities, vaulted ceilings, and one-bedrooms starting at $1,190.

How Much Does It Cost to Live at Trestle Tree in 2026?

Trestletree Village positions itself as one of Atlanta's more affordable options:

Two-bedroom apartments: Starting at approximately $1,112/mo

The community primarily offers two-bedroom units in a garden-style, two-story walk-up format. Unit sizes and pricing should be confirmed with the leasing office, as availability varies by location (Trestletree North vs Trestletree Village).

At $1,112 for a two-bedroom, Trestletree is well below Atlanta's average rent. The tradeoff is in amenities and finishes, which reflect the price point.

For comparison, Verdant Peachtree Creek near Chamblee offers one-bedrooms starting at $1,190 and features a gated community with resort-style pool, fitness center, vaulted ceilings, and hardwood floors. The $78 difference between Trestletree's 2BR and Verdant's 1BR means Verdant offers a dramatically different living experience for nearly the same monthly cost.

What Amenities Does Trestle Tree Offer?

Trestletree's amenity package reflects its affordable positioning:

Community amenities: Community center with computer lab, laundry facilities, arts and crafts room, public gathering spaces, on-site parking, recycling program, resident services coordinator

Unit features: Cable-ready, ceiling fans, ADA accessible units available

Security: Security features added during 2014 renovation

The community centers at both Trestletree sites were upgraded in 2014, which was a meaningful improvement. The computer labs and laundry facilities serve a practical need.

What Trestletree does not have: no swimming pool, no fitness center, no gated entry, no in-unit washer/dryer, no resort-style amenities, no dog park, no business center, no clubhouse with lounge areas. For renters who want amenities beyond the basics, the gap is significant.

How Does Trestle Tree Compare to Verdant Peachtree Creek?

These communities serve different price points but are closer in monthly cost than most renters expect.

Pricing (as of Q3 2026):

Trestletree 2BR: from $1,112/mo. Verdant Peachtree Creek 1BR: from $1,190/mo. Difference: $78/mo.

Verdant 2BR: contact leasing for pricing. The point is that for $78 more per month, a renter goes from a basic two-bedroom without a pool to a one-bedroom in a gated community with resort-style amenities.

Amenities:

Trestletree has a community center with computer lab, laundry room, and parking.

Verdant Peachtree Creek has a gated entry, resort-style swimming pool, fully equipped fitness center with cardio and strength equipment plus TVs, resident clubhouse with kitchen, billiards, and lounge areas, business center with conference room, outdoor barbecue and grilling areas, courtyard, covered parking, and a car care center.

Unit quality:

Trestletree: garden-style walk-ups, cable-ready, ceiling fans. Basic but functional.

Verdant: vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, walk-in closets, energy-efficient appliances, private patios or balconies. Select homes include built-in desks, fireplaces, and in-unit washer/dryer.

The interior quality gap is the largest difference between these two communities.

Location:

Trestletree: Grant Park/Ormewood Park area, near the BeltLine, Georgia State University, Morehouse, and Spelman.

Verdant: near Chamblee/Doraville, direct I-85 access, near Emory University, Oglethorpe University, Brookhaven dining and nightlife.

Both locations have distinct advantages. Trestletree is closer to intown Atlanta and the BeltLine. Verdant is closer to I-85 and the Peachtree Industrial corridor employers.

Security:

Trestletree: security features added in 2014 renovation but not a gated community.

Verdant: fully gated community with controlled entry.

What Are Renters Saying About Trestle Tree?

Trestletree has reviews on Yelp and ApartmentHomeLiving with a mixed profile.

What residents like: The affordability is the top positive. The location near the BeltLine and Grant Park is valued by residents who prioritize intown access. The 2014 renovation improved the community centers meaningfully. Some residents appreciate the community's involvement through resident services programming.

What residents flag: Multiple reviews mention maintenance responsiveness as inconsistent. Unit finishes are described as basic. The lack of a pool and fitness center is noted by residents coming from communities with those amenities. Parking availability can be tight during peak hours.

Overall impression: Trestletree delivers on price. It is one of the most affordable options in intown Atlanta. Renters who can increase their budget by $78 to $200 per month should evaluate communities like Verdant Peachtree Creek that offer dramatically different amenity and quality levels.

Is the Grant Park Area a Good Place to Rent in Atlanta?

BeltLine access: The Eastside Trail of the BeltLine runs through the area, connecting Grant Park to Inman Park, Old Fourth Ward, and Ponce City Market. This is one of the best walkable/bikeable corridors in Atlanta.

Zoo Atlanta and Grant Park: The park itself is a major green space with walking paths, playgrounds, and Zoo Atlanta. Free to walk through, with ticketed zoo access.

Dining and nightlife: The Grant Park and Ormewood Park neighborhoods have seen rapid restaurant growth. Local spots along Memorial Drive and Glenwood Avenue provide variety.

Transit: MARTA stations are accessible (Georgia State, King Memorial). Bus routes serve the area.

Employment: Georgia State University is nearby. Downtown Atlanta employers are a short commute via MARTA.

Is the Chamblee/Doraville Area a Good Place to Rent in Atlanta?

For comparison, here is what Verdant Peachtree Creek's neighborhood offers:

I-85 commuter access: Direct I-85 access puts Midtown Atlanta 15 to 20 minutes away. The corridor is one of Atlanta's primary commuter routes.

Employers: Emory University and Emory Healthcare, CDC (Centers for Disease Control), Children's Healthcare of Atlanta, AT&T Southeast hub, Deloitte, and companies along the Peachtree Industrial corridor.

Dining and culture: Buford Highway is a nationally recognized international food corridor with Korean, Vietnamese, Chinese, Mexican, and Ethiopian restaurants. Chamblee and Doraville have vibrant, diverse dining scenes.

Transit: MARTA's Gold Line serves Chamblee and Doraville stations.

Who Is Each Apartment Best For?

Choose Trestle Tree if you:

Need the lowest possible rent in intown Atlanta

Want BeltLine access and walkable/bikeable intown living

Are a student at Georgia State, Morehouse, or Spelman

Do not need a pool, fitness center, or in-unit washer/dryer

Prioritize location over amenities

Choose Verdant Peachtree Creek if you:

Can budget $1,190+/mo and want resort-style amenities (pool, gym, clubhouse)

Want a gated community with controlled entry

Value vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, and modern finishes

Work at Emory, CDC, or anywhere along the I-85 corridor

Have a car and commute via I-85

Want a bilingual leasing team (Spanish-speaking staff available)

The deciding factor: For $78 more per month than Trestletree's two-bedroom, Verdant Peachtree Creek's one-bedroom offers a gated community, resort pool, fitness center, vaulted ceilings, and hardwood floors. The value gap at that price difference is one of the largest across the Atlanta rental market.

Frequently Asked Questions About Trestle Tree Apartments Atlanta

How much is rent at Trestle Tree Atlanta?

Two-bedroom apartments at Trestletree Village start at approximately $1,112 per month as of Q3 2026. Contact the leasing office for current availability and pricing at both the North and Village sites.

Where is Trestle Tree located in Atlanta?

Trestletree has two sites in the Grant Park and Ormewood Park neighborhoods on the southeast side of Atlanta, near the BeltLine and Zoo Atlanta. Trestletree North is at 794 Ormewood Ave.

Does Trestle Tree have a pool?

No. Trestletree does not have a swimming pool. The community's amenities include a community center with computer lab and laundry facilities.

Does Trestle Tree allow pets?

Contact the leasing office for current pet policies. The community does not have a dedicated dog park.

How does Trestle Tree compare to Verdant Peachtree Creek?

Verdant Peachtree Creek is $78 more per month for a one-bedroom ($1,190) compared to Trestletree's two-bedroom ($1,112). Verdant offers a gated community, resort pool, fitness center, clubhouse, vaulted ceilings, and hardwood floors, amenities that Trestletree does not have.

Is Trestle Tree near public transit?

Yes. MARTA bus routes serve the area, and the Georgia State and King Memorial MARTA stations are accessible for rail service.

Does Trestle Tree have in-unit laundry?

No. Trestletree does not have in-unit washer/dryer. Laundry facilities are available in the community center. Verdant Peachtree Creek offers in-unit washer/dryer in select apartments.

What was renovated at Trestle Tree?

The 2014 renovation added new community centers at both sites with computer labs, laundry facilities, arts and crafts rooms, public gathering spaces, and security improvements.

Is Trestle Tree near the Atlanta BeltLine?

Yes. Trestletree's location in Grant Park and Ormewood Park puts it near the BeltLine's Eastside Trail, which connects to Inman Park, Old Fourth Ward, and Ponce City Market.

Are there apartments near Atlanta with a pool under $1,200?

Verdant Peachtree Creek near Chamblee offers one-bedrooms from $1,190 with a resort-style swimming pool, fitness center, and gated community, all under $1,200 per month.
