# COMMUNITY RESEARCH: Apartments Chamblee GA

## Chamblee Neighborhood Profile
- Small city within DeKalb County, northeast of Atlanta along I-85
- Population ~30,000. Rapidly transforming from suburban to urban-suburban.
- MARTA Gold Line: Chamblee station provides direct train to Midtown/Downtown
- Buford Highway: nationally recognized international food corridor
- Downtown Chamblee: growing restaurant and retail district
- Major employers nearby: Emory, CDC, Children's Healthcare, AT&T, Deloitte, Peachtree Industrial corridor
- Schools: DeKalb County School District

## Chamblee Rental Market
- Average 1BR: $1,100-$1,800 (well below Atlanta's $1,700 avg)
- Mix of older garden-style and newer construction
- Verdant Peachtree Creek is the largest gated option from $1,131

## What Renters Ask About Chamblee
- Is Chamblee safe? (Generally yes, improving with investment)
- How is the commute to Midtown? (15-20 min I-85 or MARTA)
- What restaurants are nearby? (Buford Highway = world-class international dining)
- Is it walkable? (Improving but still car-dependent for most errands)
- Are there gated communities? (Yes, Verdant is gated from $1,131)
