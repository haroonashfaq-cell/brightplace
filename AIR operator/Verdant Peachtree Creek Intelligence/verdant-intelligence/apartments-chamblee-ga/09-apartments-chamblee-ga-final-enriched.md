# Apartments in Chamblee GA: What Renters Should Know About This Atlanta Neighborhood

Chamblee is a small city within DeKalb County northeast of Atlanta, sitting directly on the I-85 corridor and the MARTA Gold Line. Apartment rents in Chamblee start at approximately $1,100 for a one-bedroom as of Q3 2026, which is $400 to $700 below the Atlanta city average. The neighborhood has transformed rapidly over the past five years, with new restaurants, retail, and residential investment changing Chamblee from a suburban pass-through into one of metro Atlanta's most interesting rental corridors.

[Verdant Peachtree Creek](https://verdant-peachtree-creek.brightplace.ai) at 3001 Northeast Expressway is the largest gated apartment community in the Chamblee area, offering one- to three-bedroom apartments with vaulted ceilings and hardwood floors starting at $1,131 per month.

## What Is Chamblee Like for Renters?

Chamblee delivers a specific lifestyle that is different from Midtown, Buckhead, or the BeltLine neighborhoods. Here is what to expect.

**Buford Highway is the headline.** The Buford Highway international food corridor runs through the Chamblee/Doraville area and is recognized nationally as one of the best dining streets in the Southeast. Korean barbecue, Vietnamese pho, Chinese dim sum, Mexican taquerias, Ethiopian injera, and more. The food is exceptional and affordable. This is not a tourist attraction. It is where residents eat every week.

**MARTA Gold Line access.** Chamblee station connects directly to Midtown Atlanta (20 minutes by train), Downtown (25 minutes), and Hartsfield-Jackson Airport (45 minutes). For renters who want to avoid I-85 traffic entirely, MARTA makes Chamblee viable without a car for the Atlanta commute.

**I-85 direct access.** For drivers, I-85 puts Midtown 15 to 20 minutes south and the Peachtree Industrial corridor minutes north. Verdant Peachtree Creek has direct I-85 access from the community.

**Downtown Chamblee is growing.** New restaurants and bars have opened in downtown Chamblee over the past two years. The area is transitioning from suburban to a walkable, mixed-use district. It is still early, but the trajectory is clear.

**International community.** Chamblee and Doraville have significant Korean, Vietnamese, Chinese, and Latino populations. This diversity drives the restaurant scene and creates a multicultural neighborhood character that is rare in suburban Atlanta.

## What Should You Look for in a Chamblee Apartment?

**Gated entry.** Not all Chamblee communities are gated. At the sub-$1,200 price point, gating is rare. Verdant Peachtree Creek is gated from $1,131, which is an unusual value.

**I-85 or MARTA proximity.** Your commute depends on one of these. Check drive time to the I-85 on-ramp and walking distance to Chamblee MARTA station before signing.

**Pool.** Atlanta summers are hot and long. A community pool is practical from May through October. Not all Chamblee communities have one.

**In-unit washer/dryer.** Older Chamblee apartments may not include this. Verify before assuming. Select Premier units at Verdant include in-unit machines.

**Ceiling height.** Standard 8-foot ceilings feel small. Verdant's vaulted ceilings are a differentiator that makes the same square footage feel dramatically more spacious.

**Buford Highway access.** If international dining is part of why you choose Chamblee, check your drive time to Buford Highway. Communities along Northeast Expressway (like Verdant) are minutes from the corridor.

## How Is the Commute from Chamblee?

**To Midtown Atlanta:** 15-20 min (I-85) or 20 min (MARTA Gold Line)

**To Downtown Atlanta:** 20-25 min (I-85) or 25 min (MARTA)

**To [Emory University](https://www.emory.edu/):** 15 min (I-85 to Clairmont Rd)

**To [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/):** 15 min (Clifton Rd near Emory)

**To Buckhead:** 10-15 min (I-85 or GA-400)

**To Hartsfield-Jackson Airport:** 30-40 min (I-85 South to I-285)

**To Peachtree Industrial corridor:** 5-10 min north on I-85

**The tradeoff is clear:** 15 to 20 extra minutes of commute time saves $400 to $900 per month in rent. For most renters, the math favors Chamblee.
