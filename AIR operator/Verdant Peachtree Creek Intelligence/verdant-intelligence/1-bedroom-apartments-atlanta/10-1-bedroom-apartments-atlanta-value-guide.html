1 Bedroom Apartments in Atlanta: Where to Find Value in 2026

The average one-bedroom apartment in Atlanta rents for approximately $1,700 per month as of Q3 2026, but pricing swings from $1,100 in the Chamblee/Doraville corridor to $2,800 in Midtown depending on neighborhood. For renters seeking a one-bedroom that delivers gated security, vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, and I-85 commuter access well below the city average, the Chamblee/Doraville corridor northeast of Atlanta has emerged as the value leader.

Verdant Peachtree Creek in Chamblee starts at $1,131 for a one-bedroom, which is $569 below the Atlanta average, in a gated community with a resort-style pool.

How Much Is a 1 Bedroom Apartment in Atlanta by Neighborhood?

One-bedroom pricing varies dramatically across Atlanta's neighborhoods as of Q3 2026:

Midtown: $1,800 to $2,800/mo. Walkable, high-rise living near Piedmont Park and the arts district. The most expensive 1BR market in Atlanta.

Buckhead: $1,600 to $2,500/mo. Upscale shopping (Lenox Square, Phipps Plaza), nightlife, corporate offices. Premium pricing for the address.

West Midtown / Westside Provisions: $1,500 to $2,200/mo. Converted warehouse districts, restaurants, galleries. Trendy and rising.

Old Fourth Ward / Inman Park: $1,600 to $2,300/mo. BeltLine access, Ponce City Market, Krog Street Market. Walkable and popular with young professionals.

Decatur: $1,400 to $2,000/mo. Downtown Decatur has restaurants, shops, and MARTA access. A more suburban feel near Emory.

Chamblee / Doraville: $1,100 to $1,800/mo. The value corridor. I-85 access, MARTA Gold Line, Buford Highway international dining, proximity to Emory and the CDC. Rapidly improving with new restaurants and investment.

East Atlanta / Grant Park: $1,300 to $1,800/mo. Eclectic neighborhood feel near Zoo Atlanta and the BeltLine Southside Trail.

For renters who work near I-85, Emory, or the CDC, the Chamblee/Doraville corridor delivers the lowest 1BR rents in the metro with direct highway and MARTA access to everywhere else.

What Should You Look for in an Atlanta 1 Bedroom?

In-unit washer and dryer. Atlanta's 2026 renter survey confirms this as the #1 must-have. Shared laundry rooms cost time. Select units at Verdant Peachtree Creek include in-unit machines.

Parking included. Midtown and Buckhead charge $100 to $250/mo for parking. Chamblee/Doraville communities typically include parking or charge minimal fees. Always ask before comparing rents.

Gated entry. Security is a top renter concern. Most Atlanta communities under $1,500 are NOT gated. Verdant Peachtree Creek is gated at $1,131, which is extremely rare.

Ceiling height. Standard 8-foot ceilings feel cramped in a 1BR. Vaulted ceilings (like those at Verdant) create vertical space that makes the same square footage feel significantly larger.

Highway or MARTA access. Atlanta traffic is among the worst in the country. Living near I-85 or a MARTA station saves hours per week. The Chamblee/Doraville corridor has both.

Pool access. Atlanta summers run from May through October with temperatures regularly above 90°F. A community pool is practical, not luxury.

Why Is Chamblee/Doraville the Best Value for 1 Bedroom Apartments?

The Chamblee and Doraville corridor along I-85 northeast of Atlanta has become the metro's value leader for several reasons:

Rents are $400 to $700 below the Atlanta average. A one-bedroom in Chamblee starts at $1,100 compared to $1,700 citywide and $1,800+ in Midtown.

I-85 puts Midtown 15 to 20 minutes away. The highway commute is direct. You save $600/mo on rent and add 15 minutes to your commute. That math works for most renters.

MARTA Gold Line connects to the city. Chamblee and Doraville stations are on the Gold Line. Midtown is 20 minutes by train. No car needed for downtown access.

Buford Highway is one of the best food corridors in the Southeast. Korean barbecue, Vietnamese pho, Chinese dim sum, Mexican taquerias, and Ethiopian restaurants line the highway. The dining is better and cheaper than most of Atlanta's trendier neighborhoods.

Employers are nearby. Emory University and Emory Healthcare (15 min), CDC (15 min), Children's Healthcare of Atlanta, AT&T Southeast hub, and the Peachtree Industrial corridor are all within a short commute.

The area is improving rapidly. New restaurants, retail, and residential development are transforming Chamblee's downtown and the Buford Highway corridor. Renters who move now get pre-gentrification pricing with improving amenities.

How Does the Commute from Chamblee Compare?

To Midtown Atlanta: 15 to 20 minutes via I-85 or 20 minutes via MARTA Gold Line

To Downtown Atlanta: 20 to 25 minutes via I-85/I-75 or 25 minutes via MARTA

To Buckhead: 10 to 15 minutes via I-85 or GA-400

To Emory University / CDC: 15 minutes via I-85 to Clairmont Road

To Hartsfield-Jackson Airport: 30 to 40 minutes via I-85 South to I-285

The commute reality: A Midtown 1BR costs $1,249/mo more than a Verdant 1BR. The commute adds 15 to 20 minutes each way. For renters whose budget is tighter than their schedule, the Chamblee commute is a rational tradeoff.
