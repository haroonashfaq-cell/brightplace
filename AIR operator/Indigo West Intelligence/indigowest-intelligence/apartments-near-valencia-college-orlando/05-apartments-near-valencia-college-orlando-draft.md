## Is MetroWest Orlando a Good Neighborhood for Renters?

MetroWest is a master-planned community in west Orlando that balances residential quiet with urban access. Here is what the neighborhood delivers.

**Employment:** Hard Rock Cafe's corporate headquarters is in MetroWest. Lockheed Martin has a campus east of the area. Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and SeaWorld, Orlando's three largest employers, are all within a 25-minute drive. The Mall at Millenia and International Drive employers are within 10 minutes.

**Commute to downtown:** SR 408 (East-West Expressway) puts downtown Orlando 12 minutes away during normal traffic.

**Shopping and groceries:** MetroWest Village is the neighborhood's commercial center with Publix, Keke's Breakfast Cafe, and a mix of local restaurants. Izziban Sushi and BBQ is a local favorite for all-you-can-eat Korean barbecue and sushi. The Mall at Millenia is 3 minutes away.

**Food trucks:** Every fourth Thursday of the month, the MetroWest Food Truck Connection gathers mobile restaurants at MetroWest Golf Club.

**Theme park access:** Universal Studios is approximately 25 minutes away. Disney World is roughly 30 minutes. SeaWorld is about 20 minutes. This matters for residents who work at the parks.

**Highway access:** I-4, SR 408, and Florida's Turnpike are all accessible from MetroWest, connecting residents to every part of the Orlando metro area.
