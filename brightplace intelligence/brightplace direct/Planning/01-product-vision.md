# 01 — Product Vision

## One-Line
brightplace direct is a content engine that turns apartment operator websites into traffic-generating, lead-capturing, AI-cited properties.

## The Problem
Apartment operators have websites with property pages but zero editorial content. Their sites don't rank for informational queries. AI models don't cite them. Renters researching neighborhoods, amenities, and pricing find listing sites (Apartments.com, Zillow) instead of the operator's own site.

## The Solution
A content engine that:
1. Researches what renters search for (keywords)
2. Writes expert articles targeting those searches
3. Publishes them to the operator's website
4. Tracks the traffic, leads, and AI citations generated

## Who It's For
- Apartment operators with 5-500 properties
- Property management companies
- REITs with apartment portfolios
- Any multifamily operator who wants organic traffic

## What Makes It Different
1. **AI-first SEO** — structured for ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude citation, not just Google
2. **All-in pricing transparency** — a content angle no competitor owns
3. **Plug-and-play** — works with operator's existing site (any CMS)
4. **Automated pipeline** — keyword research to published article with minimal human input
5. **Proven agents** — 8 specialized AI agents with learned patterns from real deployments

## Success Metrics
- Articles ranking on page 1 for target keywords
- AI models citing operator content when asked about properties
- Tour bookings and CTA clicks attributed to content
- Organic traffic growth month-over-month
