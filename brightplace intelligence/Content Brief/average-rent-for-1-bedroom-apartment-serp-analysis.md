## Differentiation Strategy

**Our edge:**

1. **Affordability-first framing:** Answer "what can you actually afford on $X salary?" not just "here's the average rent." The PAA questions prove this is what renters really want.
2. **30% rule applied:** Run the math at $40K, $50K, $60K, $75K, $100K salary levels. Show which cities are affordable at each income.
3. **City-by-city comparison with renter context:** Not just rent data — include what $1,500/mo actually gets you (size, location, amenities) in 10 major cities.
4. **Trend analysis:** Rent is DOWN 3.4% YoY nationally. Nobody in the top 10 leads with this good news for renters.
5. **Honest tradeoffs:** "Average 1BR in Mississippi is $750/mo, but here's the job market reality."
6. **Date freshness:** We can publish with Q3 2026 data while competitors have Q1/Q2 data.
