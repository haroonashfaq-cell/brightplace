# Keyword Strategy

Migrated: 2026-09-16. Source: Claude project `MEMORY.md`, Keyword Targeting
Strategy; `Agents/WORKFLOW.md` Stage 0 decision rule.

- DO target "what is [topic]", "how to [action]", "[topic] vs [topic]", and
  property-specific queries with thin competition.
- DON'T target listing-dominated, template/form, or high-authority-dominated SERPs
  with an article that cannot satisfy the actual intent.
- Historical caution examples: "apartments with [amenity]" and "lease agreement".
  They are not permanent banned keywords: inspect the current SERP and proposed angle.
- Classify the actual results as article/guide, listing/tool, template/form, or mixed.
  Record evidence and check date in the brief. Proceed when the article angle fits;
  otherwise propose a viable angle before writing. Do not invent keyword metrics.
