The story behind brightplace

In May 2026, Online Marketplaces published a Ten Questions interview with Brian Lichtenberger, founder and CEO of brightplace. The interview covered the founding of the company, the problems the platform was built to solve, and the team's approach to building AI-native rental search from the ground up.

Lichtenberger, who previously founded 7Park Data and Markerr (a CRE data business acquired by Real Estate Business Analytics in December 2025), described how the platform combines "discovery, research and diligence into one conversational experience."

Key insights from the interview

On what incumbents miss: "The conversational layer is table stakes. Renters actually want tools for informed decision-making: understanding fee impacts on total monthly costs, property insights, tenant experiences, and self-directed tours."

On building for the future: "The renter is not always going to be a person. It is going to be an AI agent. We are building for that."

On the company's core philosophy: "Very few companies truly build first for renters. We prioritize renter experience as our core rationale, shaping decisions across product, content, messaging, and hiring, trusting that everything else will take care of itself."

The company has four employees, was selected for the inaugural RET Ventures Proptech AI Accelerator, and reports strong early renter engagement despite minimal brand awareness and marketing investment.
