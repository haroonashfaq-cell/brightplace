## A new financial layer for renters

On June 10, 2026, brightplace launched a financial intelligence layer that covers every US rental market. The update adds income-after-tax calculations, cost of living benchmarks, affordability ratios, and local hiring trend data directly into the apartment search experience.

The brightplace Rental Advisor now answers affordability questions in real time. Renters can ask what they can afford in a specific city, how rent compares to local income levels, and what the true monthly cost of living looks like beyond base rent.

## What the financial tools cover

The financial intelligence features include estimated budget breakdowns, rent-to-income comparisons, total cost of renting calculations (including estimated fees, utilities, and move-in costs), and cost comparisons between apartments and markets.

All financial information is provided for general informational and educational purposes. brightplace is not a financial advisor, and the estimates are designed to help renters think through their housing decisions, not make those decisions for them. Full details are available in the [Financial Information Disclosure](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightplace-brings-financial-intelligence-to-apartment-search-302795862.html).

## Try it now

The financial tools are available to all renters at [app.brightplace.ai](https://app.brightplace.ai). Ask the Rental Advisor about affordability in any US city.
