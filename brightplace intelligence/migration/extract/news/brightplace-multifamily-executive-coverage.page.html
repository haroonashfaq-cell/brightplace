brightplace Featured in Multifamily Executive

Industry recognition

In May 2026, Multifamily Executive featured brightplace in a comprehensive article examining how AI, transparency, and integration are driving the latest wave of innovation across the multifamily rental industry.

The article, written by Christine Serlin, examined how technology companies are using AI to remove friction throughout the rental journey, from discovery through move-in to daily resident living.

What the coverage highlighted

The coverage focused on brightplace's AI Rental Advisor and its ability to answer questions no platform has previously been able to address. Brian Lichtenberger, founder and CEO, was quoted: "For the first time, renters can get answers to questions no platform has ever been able to answer. 'What's it like to live in this unit? In this property? In this neighborhood?'"

The article positioned brightplace alongside other proptech innovators introducing AI-native search tools, conversational leasing platforms, enhanced fee transparency, and smarter screening alternatives.
