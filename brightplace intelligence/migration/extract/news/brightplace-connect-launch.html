Apartment search goes fully agentic

On June 16, 2026, brightplace launched brightplace Connect, infrastructure that allows AI agents and third-party applications to access its Rental Advisor directly. The platform enables complete apartment searches, from discovery through tour scheduling, without human intervention.

"When an agent shows up, it needs more than listings. It needs judgment, context and the ability to act. brightplace Connect gives it all three," said Brian Lichtenberger, founder and CEO.

What brightplace Connect enables

Built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the emerging standard for agent-to-application interoperability, brightplace Connect provides five core capabilities to AI agents:

  • Discovery and recommendation: Converts natural-language preferences into matched properties with explicit tradeoffs
  • Listing details: Provides comprehensive unit information, property context, and financial intelligence
  • Tour availability: Displays real-time tour slots against actual inventory
  • Tour requests: Moves from shortlist to scheduled appointments within conversation
  • Fair Housing guardrails: All responses comply with Fair Housing protections regardless of source
