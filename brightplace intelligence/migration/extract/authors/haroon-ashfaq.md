# About Haroon

Haroon Ashfaq is the founding GTM engineer at brightplace, where he builds the systems that connect renter-first content to the platforms where renters actually search. He designed and operates the agentic content pipeline that earned brightplace over 100 AI citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews in under three months.

## GTM Engineering

Haroon's work spans content operations, SEO infrastructure, AEO strategy, and Webflow CMS automation. He builds the tooling that turns keyword research into published, schema-equipped, citation-ready articles without manual intervention, letting a four-person team operate at the scale of a much larger content organization.
