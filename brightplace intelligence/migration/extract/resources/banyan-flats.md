# Banyan Flats: A Practical Look at Dallas's Oak Cliff Apartments

*Last reviewed: August 2026. Pricing reflects available listing data and should be verified directly with the property.*

## What Banyan Flats Is

Banyan Flats is a 289-unit apartment community at 2020 N Beckley Ave in Dallas's Oak Cliff neighborhood, opened in March 2024. Developed by Banyan Residential and managed by Bridge Property Management, it offers studio through three-bedroom apartments across five stories. The community includes both market-rate units and income-restricted units at 60% and 80% AMI tiers.

JHP served as the architect of record. The property sits adjacent to the Bishop Arts District, one of Dallas's most concentrated corridors of independent restaurants, coffee shops, and retail. For renters comparing apartments in the Banyan Flats area or the broader Oak Cliff market, this guide covers rent prices, amenities, location context, and the income restriction structure that most competing sources skip.

## Floor Plans and Rent Ranges

Banyan Flats offers four bedroom configurations. The property uses the label "Essential" for income-restricted units, a naming convention most listing sites do not explain. At Banyan Flats, floor plans labeled "Essential" correspond to income-restricted units: the same physical layout at a lower rent, available only to qualifying applicants. Pricing below reflects available listing data (as of Q3 2026). Confirm current figures with the leasing office.

**Studio (415 to 593 sq ft).** Market-rate: $1,160 to $1,383/mo (as of Q3 2026). Income-restricted ("Essential"): $1,141 to $1,360/mo (as of Q3 2026). At 415 sq ft and $1,141/mo, the smallest studio works out to approximately $2.75/sq ft.

**1 Bedroom (600 to 1,059 sq ft).** Market-rate: $1,371 to $2,228/mo (as of Q3 2026). Income-restricted ("Essential"): $1,277 to $1,761/mo (as of Q3 2026). At the top of the range, a 1,059 sq ft one-bedroom at $2,228/mo equals approximately $2.10/sq ft.

**3 Bedroom (1,374 sq ft).** Market-rate: $2,748/mo (as of Q3 2026). Income-restricted ("Essential"): $2,294/mo (as of Q3 2026).

Lease terms run 12 to 15 months. The application fee is $65 (as of Q3 2026).

## Apartment Features

Interior finishes at Banyan Flats go beyond what is standard in this price range for Oak Cliff. Every unit includes wood-style flooring, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, in-unit washer and dryer, walk-in closets, and keyless entry.

Select units also include private balconies, soaking tubs, walk-in glass showers, and high ceilings. These features vary by floor plan and floor level, so renters should confirm which finishes apply to a specific unit before signing. ## What the Income Restriction Means for Renters

Banyan Flats includes income-restricted units alongside market-rate apartments. No competing source explains what this means in practical terms.

AMI, or Area Median Income, is a benchmark published annually by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development ([HUD](https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance)) used to determine income eligibility for restricted-rate housing programs. An income-restricted unit is a conventionally built apartment reserved for households whose income falls at or below a specified percentage of the local AMI. A market-rate unit carries no income eligibility requirement and is priced at whatever the current rental market supports.

At Banyan Flats, the "Essential" units are available at two AMI tiers: 60% AMI and 80% AMI. Income limits for qualification (as of Q3 2026):

**1 person:** up to $65,760/year. **2 people:** up to $75,120/year. **3 people:** up to $84,480/year. **4 people:** up to $93,840/year.

Applicants must provide income documentation at the time of application. The income-restricted apartments at Banyan Flats are conventional units with the same amenities as market-rate homes. Eligibility is the only difference.
