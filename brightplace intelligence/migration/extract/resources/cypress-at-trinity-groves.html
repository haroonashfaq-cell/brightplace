What Cypress at Trinity Groves Is

Cypress at Trinity Groves is a three-phase luxury apartment campus on Singleton Boulevard in Dallas's Trinity Groves neighborhood, a mixed-use dining, entertainment, and residential district on the west bank of the Trinity River at the foot of the Margaret Hunt Hill Bridge. The property includes approximately 1,015 total apartments across three buildings (as of Q3 2026), managed by Willow Bridge Property Company. Studio apartments start at approximately $1,036/month (as of Q3 2026). For renters evaluating West Dallas apartments at a lower price point than Uptown alternatives, Cypress at Trinity Groves is one of the largest communities in the corridor.

The Three Phases and How They Differ

Cypress at Trinity Groves operates as three separate buildings under one brand, each with its own address, leasing portal, and application process. This distinction matters because applying to the wrong phase is a common renter mistake.

Phase I (320 Singleton Blvd): 352 units, built 2017 (as of Q3 2026). The oldest building on the campus. Residents have reported slower maintenance response times and extended elevator outages in this phase.

Phase II (411 Broadway Ave): 296 units, built 2018 (as of Q3 2026). This phase includes income-restricted units with eligibility tied to household income limits. Income-restricted units are apartments with rent eligibility tied to household income limits; at Phase II the income limit for a one-person household is approximately $53,000 (as of Q3 2026). Confirm current thresholds with the Phase II leasing office. Maintenance in Phase II is generally rated more favorably by residents.

Phase III (500 Singleton Blvd): 386 units, built 2022 (as of Q3 2026). The newest phase and typically the highest-priced. Phase III benefits from newer finishes and fewer maintenance backlogs.

What the Location Actually Means for Daily Life

Trinity Groves is restaurant-walkable but car-necessary for daily errands. The distinction matters.

Dining and coffee within walking distance of Cypress at Trinity Groves includes spots like Soiree Coffee Bar and Tacos Mariachi, and the broader Trinity Groves entertainment strip sits steps from the property. The Margaret Hunt Hill Bridge connects directly to Downtown Dallas, roughly 5-10 minutes by car. The Design District is under 2 miles away. Bishop Arts District, a dining and retail corridor in the Oak Cliff area, is also under 2 miles by car.

The honest tradeoff: grocery runs and everyday errands require a car. DART access is limited in this corridor; bus lines run on West Commerce Street and Singleton Boulevard, but frequency is modest and rail access is not walkable from the property. Community Amenities Worth Knowing

Three resort-style pools with grilling stations (one per phase), three sky lounges with Downtown Dallas views, and two fitness centers with instructor-led classes anchor the shared amenity package. The sky lounge views are consistently praised by residents, with panoramic sightlines of the Dallas skyline.

The community also includes a beer garden, four pet parks, co-working and conference room space, EV charging stations, and bike storage. Some amenities are shared across phases; others are phase-specific. Confirm which amenities your specific phase includes before signing.

The no-breed-restriction pet policy is a genuine differentiator. Many Dallas apartment communities restrict breeds or enforce weight limits. Cypress at Trinity Groves does not (as of Q3 2026), making it one of the more accessible options for renters with larger dogs. That said, residents have noted dog waste throughout the community despite the property's DNA testing program.

In-Unit Features and What to Verify

Standard in-unit features across Cypress at Trinity Groves include quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, wood-style flooring, walk-in closets, smart thermostats, USB outlets, ceiling fans, and full-size washer/dryer connections.

Several features are available "in select homes" only. Select homes is a designation used by the property to indicate that certain advertised features are available only in specific units, not all apartments. The select-home list includes built-in shelving, private yards, balconies and patios, soaking tubs, and walk-in showers. Ask the leasing office exactly which features apply to the specific unit you are considering. Renters have reported surprises when features shown in marketing materials did not match their actual unit, so verifying before signing is worth the extra conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cypress at Trinity Groves

Does Cypress at Trinity Groves have outdoor seating?

Yes. Cypress at Trinity Groves includes outdoor grilling stations and lounge areas at each of its three pools. The sky lounges also offer outdoor-adjacent gathering space with Downtown Dallas views. The broader Trinity Groves district surrounding the property has multiple restaurants with patio dining within walking distance.

How walkable is Trinity Groves for everyday errands?

Trinity Groves is walkable for dining and entertainment, with restaurants and coffee shops steps from the property. Daily errands like grocery shopping require a car. DART bus service runs along Singleton Boulevard and West Commerce Street, but frequency is limited. Rail access is not within walking distance of the Cypress at Trinity Groves campus.

What is the pet policy at Cypress at Trinity Groves?

Cypress at Trinity Groves allows up to 2 pets per unit with no breed or weight restrictions (as of Q3 2026). The pet deposit is $500, the non-refundable pet fee is $300, and monthly pet rent is $10 per pet. This no-breed-restriction policy is uncommon among Dallas apartment communities of this size.

How do I contact the leasing office at Cypress at Trinity Groves?

Each of the three phases has its own leasing office and contact information. Phase I is at 320 Singleton Blvd, Phase II at 411 Broadway Ave, and Phase III at 500 Singleton Blvd. Visit the property's official website to find the specific phone number and portal for the phase you are interested in.

What is the difference between Cypress at Trinity Groves I, II, and III?

The three phases are separate buildings with different addresses, construction years, and pricing tiers. Phase I (2017, 352 units) is the oldest. Phase II (2018, 296 units) includes income-restricted apartments. Phase III (2022, 386 units) is the newest and generally highest-priced. Each phase has its own leasing office and application process.
