What Is The Quaye at Palm Beach Gardens?

The Quaye at Palm Beach Gardens, Palm Beach Gardens, FL, is a 340-unit gated rental community located at 10000 South Gardens Drive. The property sits on 33.9 acres and is managed by The Dermot Company. A majority of units are townhome-style, giving the community a lower-density feel uncommon in South Florida apartment living.

The community offers 1- through 4-bedroom layouts ranging from 888 to 2,090 square feet. Gated entry with controlled access is standard, and the property includes a 10,000-plus square foot clubhouse. Floorplans and Unit Types

The Quaye offers four bedroom configurations spread across townhome and garden apartment formats. Units range from 888 square feet for a one-bedroom up to 2,090 square feet for the largest four-bedroom townhome. Roughly 72% of the community consists of townhome-style units with direct-access garages, a format that appeals to renters who want something closer to a single-family experience without the commitment of ownership. Townhome Units

The townhome layouts at The Quaye are built across 2- to 3-story structures with private, direct-access garages. These units start at two bedrooms and go up to four bedrooms, with square footage ranging from approximately 1,429 to 2,090 square feet. The multi-level layout and garage entry give them a single-family feel, which is part of the draw for renters relocating from houses.

Garden Apartments

The community includes 96 one-bedroom garden apartment units. These are the entry-level option at The Quaye, offering approximately 888 square feet at a lower price point than the townhomes. For renters who do not need a garage or multiple floors, these units provide a more affordable path into a gated community with full amenity access.

Amenities and Community Features

The Quaye's amenity package centers on a 10,000-plus square foot clubhouse that anchors the community. Indoor offerings include a 24-hour fitness center with a boxing ring and yoga area, an indoor sports court, a coworking space with a conference room, a demonstration kitchen, and a multi-sport simulator. Outdoor amenities include a resort-style pool, grilling areas, a playground, a 3,000-square-foot bark park, scenic lakes, and walking trails through an upland preserve. EV charging stations are available on-site.

The property is pet-friendly, which means the bark park and trails see regular use. Confirm pet deposits, monthly pet rent, breed restrictions, and weight limits with the leasing office before applying. Green Building Credentials

The Quaye holds NGBS Gold certification from the National Green Building Standard, a residential-focused sustainability rating that evaluates energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor air quality, and resource use. This is less common in rental communities than LEED, which focuses primarily on commercial buildings.

Specific green features include 16 SEER HVAC systems, Cardinal CC LoE3 340 solar control glass (the first residential use of this glass in the United States at the time of construction), low-VOC paints and carpets, and water-saving fixtures throughout. Every garage includes an EV outlet. The practical benefit for renters is lower monthly utility costs compared to standard South Florida construction. Concrete construction throughout also means less noise transfer between units, a meaningful advantage in attached-wall living.

Location and What Is Nearby

The Quaye is located at 10000 South Gardens Drive, positioned off I-95 and PGA Boulevard in Palm Beach Gardens. The surrounding area has well-developed dining and retail within a short drive.

Dining options nearby include Tommy Bahama Marlin Bar, Avocado Cantina, The Cooper, Grimaldi's, and Tap 42. For retail, The Gardens Mall and Downtown at the Gardens are both within minutes, and a Whole Foods is nearby for grocery runs.

Outdoor access is a strength of this location. Frenchman's Forest Natural Area and John D. MacArthur Beach State Park are both accessible, and the property itself borders 9-plus acres of upland preserve with on-site walking trails. Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center is nearby for healthcare access.

For transit, Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) is approximately 18 miles south, and the Brightline station in West Palm Beach is roughly 14 miles away. The honest tradeoff: this area is car-dependent. There is no practical public transit serving the immediate area, so a personal vehicle is effectively required for daily life.

Property History and Current Ownership

The Quaye was completed in 2016 as a $75 million development. The property sold in 2017 to PGIM Real Estate for $118 million. In February 2026, The Dermot Company acquired The Quaye for $131.8 million, approximately $387,647 per unit, financed with a $110 million loan from Affinius Capital.

Dermot has indicated plans for a value-add renovation, which typically means upgraded unit finishes, refreshed common areas, and amenity improvements. What this means for current and prospective renters: expect some construction activity during the renovation period, but renovated units will likely feature improved finishes. It is worth asking the leasing office which units have already been updated and whether any move-in specials are available on unrenovated units, as some properties discount those during the transition.
