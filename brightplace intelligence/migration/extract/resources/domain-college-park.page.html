Domain College Park: Rents, Amenities, and Who It Actually Fits

Domain College Park is a 256-unit luxury apartment community at 3711 Campus Drive, College Park, MD 20740, managed by UDR and located directly across from the University of Maryland campus. One-bedroom apartments start at approximately $1,698 per month (as of Q3 2026), with required monthly fees adding $125 to $145 on top of base rent. Graduate students, university staff, and young professionals affiliated with UMD make up the primary renter base.

What Is Domain College Park?

Domain College Park is a five-story, 408,000-square-foot apartment building containing 256 units above a two-level parking garage with 380 spaces and roughly 10,000 square feet of ground-floor retail. The Hanover Company developed the property, which opened in June 2013 as the first market-rate apartment complex built in College Park since 2007. UDR, a publicly traded real estate investment trust that manages upscale residential communities across the United States, currently operates the building. The project earned LEED Silver certification and a Delta Associates Award for Best Mid-Rise Apartment in the Mid-Atlantic region.

What Are the Current Rent Ranges at Domain College Park?

Rents at Domain College Park vary by floor plan and availability, and the property periodically offers move-in specials. Based on current leasing data (as of Q3 2026), here is what to expect.

2 Bedroom (1,075-1,115 sq ft): Starting at approximately $2,736/month (as of Q3 2026). Standard two-bedroom configurations.

2 Bedroom (up to 1,300 sq ft): Starting at approximately $2,935/month (as of Q3 2026). Larger two-bedroom layouts with den or office space.

Required monthly fees are separate from base rent. These include an amenity fee of roughly $25/month, a smart home and internet package of roughly $70/month, trash service of roughly $30/month, and package locker access of roughly $10 to $15/month (as of Q3 2026). Required fees are charges that every resident pays regardless of usage, on top of base rent, unlike optional add-on services. A $400 security deposit and $35 application fee apply before move-in.

For a one-bedroom starting at $1,698/month, the realistic monthly floor after required fees lands closer to $1,823 to $1,843/month (as of Q3 2026) before electricity or optional services. What Is Included at Domain College Park (and What Is Not)?

Units at Domain College Park include stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, hardwood-style flooring, in-unit washer and dryer, air conditioning, and additional storage. Every apartment uses a full-unit leasing structure, meaning one party signs for the entire apartment rather than individual bedrooms, which requires roommates to split rent privately rather than holding separate leases.

Community amenities include a 24-hour fitness center, resort-style heated pool, outdoor grilling areas, landscaped courtyards, a business center with conference rooms, a billiards and TV lounge, concierge service, controlled building access, EV charging stations, and package lockers.

What is not included in base rent: internet (billed through the required smart home package), trash removal, the amenity fee, and parking. Parking is available in the underground and first-floor garage but is not automatically included. Rates vary by space type, so confirm the current monthly parking fee with the leasing office directly.

How Close Is Domain College Park to Campus and Transit?

Domain College Park sits at 3711 Campus Drive, directly across from Van Munching Hall (UMD's Robert H. Smith School of Business). The main UMD campus is approximately 0.7 miles away, roughly a 13 to 14 minute walk. That proximity makes it practical for graduate students and faculty who commute to campus daily.

The College Park-U of MD Metro Station serves the Green and Yellow Lines, which are Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority rail lines connecting College Park to destinations including Silver Spring, Bethesda, and downtown Washington, DC. The station is approximately 1.3 miles from the property, a 24 to 25 minute walk. Most residents use the free UM Shuttle Bus 104, which departs the Metro station every 15 minutes, or bike to reach the station. MARC Train Camden Line service is accessible at the same station. Reagan National Airport is roughly a 28-minute drive.

The Purple Line light rail, currently under construction, will add a Campus Drive-UMD station within walking distance of the property when service begins (estimated late 2027 or early 2028). That addition will improve transit connectivity significantly.

College Park Shopping Center is approximately a 14-minute walk. No major grocery store sits within easy walking distance of Campus Drive. Residents consistently note that a car or delivery service is useful for grocery runs. Biking infrastructure in the area is strong, with dedicated lanes and trails connecting to campus and surrounding areas.

Who Does Domain College Park Work Best For?

Not every renter in College Park is looking for the same thing. Domain College Park fits some situations well and others less so.

Graduate students and postdocs: Strong fit. The 0.7-mile walk to campus, built-in study and conference spaces, full-unit leases, and professional environment suit renters who need stability and proximity. The price point (starting around $1,698/month for a one-bedroom as of Q3 2026) is more realistic for students with stipends, fellowships, or dual-income households.

University faculty and staff: Strong fit. Quality unit finishes, concierge service, controlled access, and a quieter residential setting compared to student-oriented complexes make this practical for professionals.

Young professionals commuting to DC: Moderate fit. Metro access exists, but the 25-minute walk to the station adds friction. Factor commute time carefully. The Green and Yellow Lines reach downtown DC in roughly 30 to 40 minutes from the station.

Undergraduates on a budget: Weak fit. Per-bedroom student housing options along the Route 1 corridor offer lower per-person costs. Domain College Park is priced at the upper end of the College Park market.

Remote workers wanting a home office: Moderate fit. Den and office floor plans are available in larger units. The business center provides additional workspace. Internet, however, is an add-on cost through the required smart home package.

Is College Park, MD a Good Place to Live?

College Park is a university-anchored city in Prince George's County, Maryland, about 10 miles northeast of Washington, DC. The University of Maryland campus shapes the city's character, bringing dining options, cultural programming, athletic venues, and a consistent population of students, researchers, and academics.

The area has strong biking infrastructure, moderate walkability for campus-adjacent blocks, and Green and Yellow Line Metro access for DC commutes. Paint Branch Stream Valley Park and Lake Artemesia Natural Area Park provide outdoor recreation within a short drive or bike ride. Retail options outside the campus corridor are limited without a car, and grocery access for residents along Campus Drive typically requires driving. The Purple Line light rail will improve east-west connectivity when it opens.
