What Is Redstone Ranch in Denver?

Redstone Ranch Apartment Homes is an apartment community at 4775 Argonne St, Denver, CO 80249, in the Green Valley Ranch area of northeast Denver. Green Valley Ranch is a planned residential and commercial development spanning zip codes 80239 and 80249, encompassing multiple apartment communities, townhomes, and single-family rentals. One-bedroom apartments at Redstone Ranch start at approximately $1,399/month (as of Q3 2026), and the community is roughly 15-20 minutes from Denver International Airport via Pena Boulevard. Location and Commute Context

Redstone Ranch sits in northeast Denver, approximately 12-15 miles from downtown. This is a car-dependent area. Daily errands, grocery runs, and most dining require driving. Primary road access is via I-70 and Pena Boulevard.

The nearest rail connection is the RTD A Line (University of Colorado A Line), with the 40th and Airport / Gateway Park Station as the closest stop. The A Line connects to Denver Union Station in approximately 35-40 minutes. Bus routes serve the Green Valley Ranch corridor but frequency is limited compared to central Denver.

The honest tradeoff: DIA proximity is a genuine advantage for frequent travelers and airport workers. But renters who prioritize walkability to restaurants, coffee shops, or nightlife will find central Denver neighborhoods (Capitol Hill, RiNo, Highland) a significantly better fit. brightplace's Denver city orientation covers the full metro rental picture.

Apartment Features and Community Amenities

Redstone Ranch includes a swimming pool, hot tub, fitness center, clubhouse, outdoor grilling stations, business center, elevator access, and garage parking. 24-hour maintenance is available for emergency requests.

In-Unit Features

Apartments include in-unit washer/dryer, walk-in closets, fireplaces (select units), patios or balconies, and ceiling fans. Kitchen finishes vary by floor plan.

What the Surrounding Area Is Like

The Green Valley Ranch Town Center corridor along Tower Road provides the nearest retail. Walmart Supercenter on Chambers Road and King Soopers in Green Valley Ranch handle grocery needs. Dining options along Tower Road include casual chains and a few independent restaurants, but this is not a walkable dining corridor.

Redstone Park, adjacent to the community, has sports fields and a playground. The Rocky Mountain Arsenal National Wildlife Refuge, one of the largest urban wildlife refuges in the country, sits roughly 10 minutes west. It offers hiking trails, wildlife viewing, and open space that is uncommon this close to an urban apartment community.

The second honest tradeoff: the surrounding area has limited walkable dining. Green Valley Ranch Town Center has casual options but not a full restaurant or coffee corridor. Renters who value that infrastructure should look at communities closer to central Denver.
