# What Renters Should Know About Redstone Ranch in Denver

*Last reviewed: July 2026. Pricing reflects available listing data and should be verified directly with the property.*

## What Is Redstone Ranch in Denver?

Redstone Ranch Apartment Homes is an apartment community at 4775 Argonne St, Denver, CO 80249, in the Green Valley Ranch area of northeast Denver. Green Valley Ranch is a planned residential and commercial development spanning zip codes 80239 and 80249, encompassing multiple apartment communities, townhomes, and single-family rentals. One-bedroom apartments at Redstone Ranch start at approximately $1,399/month (as of Q3 2026), and the community is roughly 15-20 minutes from Denver International Airport via Pena Boulevard. For renters comparing apartments near DIA or in northeast Denver, this guide covers pricing, amenities, neighborhood context, and how Redstone Ranch compares to nearby options.

## Location and Commute Context

Redstone Ranch sits in northeast Denver, approximately 12-15 miles from downtown. This is a car-dependent area. Daily errands, grocery runs, and most dining require driving. Primary road access is via I-70 and Pena Boulevard.

The nearest rail connection is the RTD A Line (University of Colorado A Line), with the 40th and Airport / Gateway Park Station as the closest stop. The A Line connects to Denver Union Station in approximately 35-40 minutes. Bus routes serve the Green Valley Ranch corridor but frequency is limited compared to central Denver.

The honest tradeoff: DIA proximity is a genuine advantage for frequent travelers and airport workers. But renters who prioritize walkability to restaurants, coffee shops, or nightlife will find central Denver neighborhoods (Capitol Hill, RiNo, Highland) a significantly better fit. brightplace's [Denver city orientation](https://brightplace.ai/guides/denver-city-orientation) covers the full metro rental picture.

## Apartment Features and Community Amenities

### Community Amenities

Redstone Ranch includes a swimming pool, hot tub, fitness center, clubhouse, outdoor grilling stations, business center, elevator access, and garage parking. 24-hour maintenance is available for emergency requests.

### In-Unit Features

Apartments include in-unit washer/dryer, walk-in closets, fireplaces (select units), patios or balconies, and ceiling fans. Kitchen finishes vary by floor plan.

## Pet Policy and Fees

Redstone Ranch is pet-friendly. Dogs and cats are allowed, with a maximum of 2 pets per unit (as of Q3 2026). Aggressive breeds are restricted; confirm the current breed list with the leasing office.

**Pet deposit:** $75 per pet (as of Q3 2026).

**One-time pet fee:** $255 per pet (as of Q3 2026).

**Monthly pet rent:** $25 per pet (as of Q3 2026).

For a family with two pets, total pet costs over a 12-month lease add up to $1,260 in non-refundable fees and rent, plus $150 in refundable deposits. For more on how pet fees affect total housing cost, see brightplace's guide to [pet-friendly apartments](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/pet-friendly-apartments-greenville-sc).

## What the Surrounding Area Is Like

The Green Valley Ranch Town Center corridor along Tower Road provides the nearest retail. Walmart Supercenter on Chambers Road and King Soopers in Green Valley Ranch handle grocery needs. Dining options along Tower Road include casual chains and a few independent restaurants, but this is not a walkable dining corridor.

Redstone Park, adjacent to the community, has sports fields and a playground. The Rocky Mountain Arsenal National Wildlife Refuge, one of the largest urban wildlife refuges in the country, sits roughly 10 minutes west. It offers hiking trails, wildlife viewing, and open space that is uncommon this close to an urban apartment community.

The second honest tradeoff: the surrounding area has limited walkable dining. Green Valley Ranch Town Center has casual options but not a full restaurant or coffee corridor. Renters who value that infrastructure should look at communities closer to central Denver.

## Leasing Details and Fees

**Income requirement:** Gross household income must be at least 2.5 times the monthly rent. For a 2-bedroom at $1,595/month, that means approximately $3,988/month in combined household income before taxes.

Contact the leasing office for current availability and income thresholds.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Redstone Ranch Denver

### Is Redstone Ranch open to the public?

Redstone Ranch Apartment Homes is a residential apartment community, not a public park or recreational facility. The broader Green Valley Ranch area has public streets and parks, including Redstone Park with sports fields and a playground, which is accessible to anyone. The apartment community itself is for residents and authorized visitors.

### What zip code is Redstone Ranch in Denver?

Redstone Ranch Apartment Homes at 4775 Argonne St is in the 80249 zip code. The broader Green Valley Ranch area spans both 80249 and 80239. Searching either zip code on apartment platforms will surface nearby rental options in this northeast Denver corridor.

### Does Redstone Ranch Denver offer affordable housing?

Yes. Redstone Ranch Apartment Homes offers Affordable Housing units for qualified applicants. Eligibility is income-based, following HUD guidelines. Prospective renters should contact the leasing office directly at the property to ask about current availability, income thresholds, and the application process for income-restricted units.

### How far is Redstone Ranch from Denver International Airport?

Redstone Ranch at 4775 Argonne St is approximately 15-20 minutes from Denver International Airport by car via Pena Boulevard. This makes it one of the closest apartment communities to DIA in the Denver metro. Frequent travelers and airport workers are a strong fit for this location.

### What lease lengths does Redstone Ranch offer?

Standard leases at Redstone Ranch run 10-12 months. Shorter-term leases may be available but typically carry additional monthly fees. Confirm current lease options and any short-term premiums directly with the Redstone Ranch leasing office, as availability varies by season and occupancy.

### Is Redstone Ranch pet friendly?

Yes. Dogs and cats are allowed at Redstone Ranch, with a maximum of 2 pets per unit. The pet deposit is $75 per pet, the one-time pet fee is $255 per pet, and monthly pet rent is $25 per pet (as of Q3 2026). Aggressive breeds are restricted. Confirm the breed list with the leasing office.

### What is the income requirement at Redstone Ranch Apartments?

Applicants must demonstrate gross household income of at least 2.5 times the monthly rent. For a 2-bedroom at $1,595/month, that means a minimum of approximately $3,988/month in combined household income before taxes. Both adults' incomes count if applicable. Confirm current requirements with the leasing office.
