How to Find an Apartment with an Attached Garage

What Does "Attached Garage" Actually Mean in a Rental?

Most rental listings use the word "garage" without specifying which type. That distinction matters because only one of the four common parking types gives you direct interior access. Renters who skip this verification step waste application fees on units that do not match what they expected.

Attached garage: A private garage that shares a wall with your unit and connects to the interior. You walk from your car into your home without stepping outside.

Detached garage: An enclosed parking structure assigned to your unit but located in a separate building. You walk outside to reach it.

Covered parking / carport: A sheltered space with a roof but no enclosed walls or lockable door. No security, no climate control.

Reserved parking: An assigned surface or deck space, typically open-air. No enclosure at all.

Before scheduling a tour, ask the leasing office one question: "Does the garage share a wall with my unit and connect to the interior?" If they cannot confirm, it is not an attached garage. The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's rental checklist recommends verifying all amenities in writing before signing any lease.

What Building Types Actually Have Attached Garages?

Attached garages require a specific structural configuration. The garage must sit at ground level and share a wall with the living space. That rules out most traditional apartment buildings. Here is where this amenity actually exists (as of Q3 2026):

Townhouse / rowhouse: High availability. $1,400-$2,500/mo. Ground-floor garage with living space above. Most common format for attached garages in the rental market. Before choosing this route, read what changes when you rent inside an HOA in brightplace's guide to townhouse vs apartment renting.

Single-family rental home: High availability. $1,800-$3,500/mo. Usually one or two car garage. Higher price point but the most space.

Condo rental: Moderate availability. $1,500-$2,800/mo. May share a building garage structure. Verify whether the garage connects to the unit or to a common hallway.

Garden-style apartment: Low availability. $1,100-$2,000/mo. Parking is usually detached or covered. Attached garages are structurally rare in this format.

Mid-rise / high-rise apartment: Very low availability. $1,600-$4,000+/mo. Parking is almost always structured deck or underground. Attached garages are structurally impossible in most high-rise designs.

Townhouses are the most reliable building type for this amenity. If you want an attached garage, start your search by filtering for townhouse-style units rather than traditional apartments. For a broader look at what monthly costs include beyond base rent, see your true monthly cost as a renter.

How Much Does a 1-Car vs. 2-Car Attached Garage Cost?

Garage size directly affects your rent premium and what you can actually fit inside. Most attached garages in rental townhouses are single-car, but two-car options exist in larger build-to-rent communities (as of Q3 2026).

1-car garage: Standard dimensions are roughly 12 feet wide by 20 feet deep (about 240 square feet). Fits one sedan or compact SUV with minimal room for storage bins along the walls. Typical rent premium over a comparable unit without a garage is $150 to $250 per month.

2-car garage: Standard dimensions are roughly 20 feet wide by 20 feet deep (about 400 square feet). Fits two vehicles or one vehicle plus significant storage, a workbench, or bike and gear organization. Typical rent premium over a comparable unit without a garage is $250 to $400 per month.

The 2-car option is more common in single-family rental homes than in townhouse communities. How to Search for an Apartment with an Attached Garage

Start by filtering for townhouse or single-family rental formats. A generic "parking" amenity filter aggregates multiple parking types (attached, detached, covered, reserved) into a single category. Filtering by "parking available" and assuming that includes an attached garage is the most common search mistake brightplace sees for this amenity.

Narrow your search to suburban zones. Attached garage inventory is concentrated in suburban rings, not urban cores. Expanding your search radius by 10-15 miles from a city center can triple your available options in most metros.

Check private landlord listings. Individual homeowners who rent one or two properties are more likely to offer homes with attached garages than large institutional operators. Local real estate agents who handle rental placements can connect you with this inventory.

Verify before applying. Contact the leasing office or landlord directly and confirm three things: (1) the garage connects to the unit interior, (2) the garage is included in the listed rent or billed separately, and (3) there are no HOA restrictions on what you can store in the garage. Application fees run $50-$85 per adult (as of Q3 2026), so verification before applying saves real money. brightplace's guide on how to rent an apartment walks through what to prepare before you start touring.

A two-bedroom townhouse with an attached garage in suburbs like The Woodlands, Sugar Land, or Pearland typically rents for $1,600 to $2,200/mo. Can an Attached Garage Lower Your Auto Insurance?

Yes, in many cases. Parking your vehicle in a private, enclosed garage reduces the risk of theft, vandalism, hail damage, and weather exposure, and multiple major insurers offer a discount for it (as of Q3 2026).

Allstate, Progressive, and USAA offer garaging discounts of up to 18% on comprehensive coverage when you park in a locked garage at your residence. AAA offers up to 10% off comprehensive coverage under similar conditions. Even semi-private garages shared by a few residents in a condo-style building can qualify for a reduced rate, though the discount is smaller than for a fully private attached garage.

The practical impact depends on your current premium. On a $1,200/year comprehensive policy, an 18% discount saves $216 annually. That partially offsets the rent premium you pay for the attached garage unit. When comparing the true cost of a garage apartment versus a standard unit, factor in this potential insurance savings alongside any avoided parking fees.

Tell your insurance provider that you park in a locked, attached residential garage. This is a self-reported factor on most policy applications, and many renters do not realize they qualify.

Is EV Charging Available in Garage Apartments?

EV charging access is becoming a deciding factor for renters who drive electric vehicles, and attached garages offer the simplest path to home charging in a rental setting (as of Q3 2026).

The current gap is significant. Only about 5% of U.S. rental properties currently offer EV charging infrastructure, compared to 84-94% of single-family EV owners who charge at home. For renters, an attached garage with a standard 120V outlet provides Level 1 charging (roughly 3-5 miles of range per hour), which is enough for many daily commuters. A 240V outlet (Level 2) delivers 20-30 miles per hour and is the preferred setup.

What to ask your landlord before signing. If you drive an EV or plan to buy one, ask three questions: (1) Is there a 240V outlet in the garage, or can one be installed? (2) Who pays for the electricity used for charging? (3) Does the lease or HOA prohibit modifications to the garage electrical system?

The 2026 trend. ChargePoint and OBE Power announced a partnership in 2026 to bring 2,500 new EV charging ports to multifamily housing. More apartment operators are adding shared Level 2 chargers in parking areas and garages, often through revenue-sharing models where a third-party company installs the hardware at no upfront cost to the building. Newer build-to-rent communities in Dallas, Houston, Phoenix, and Denver are increasingly pre-wiring garages for Level 2 charging as a standard feature.

If EV charging is a priority, attached garage units give you the most control because you have a dedicated, enclosed space with its own electrical panel. That is significantly easier to work with than shared parking lots or structures. For more on budgeting for an EV-friendly rental, see brightplace's guide to your true monthly cost.

What Do Renters Actually Complain About with Garage Apartments?

Attached garages solve real problems, but they come with constraints that renters frequently mention. Knowing these tradeoffs before you sign helps you avoid surprises.

Garage door noise travels through walls and floors. In townhouse communities with shared walls, a neighbor's garage door opening at 5:30 AM can wake you up. Most garage doors operate at 50-70 decibels, roughly the volume of normal conversation, but that is enough to carry through framing, walls, and floors in the early morning. Some HOA-managed communities restrict garage door use to 7 AM-10 PM, but enforcement varies. Ask neighbors about noise before you commit.

Stairs are unavoidable in most townhouse formats. The garage occupies the ground floor, so living space sits above it. Renters with young children, mobility limitations, or heavy grocery loads should factor this into their decision. The Fair Housing Act requires that multifamily buildings with four or more units meet accessibility standards, but single-family rentals and small townhouse communities may not include ground-floor living space. brightplace's Fair Housing Act guidelines article covers how these rules apply to renters.

Car dependency is the norm. Attached garage units are concentrated in suburban areas with minimal walkability. Expect to drive for groceries, dining, and most errands.

HOA rules may restrict garage use. In condo-style and HOA-managed communities, garages are designated for vehicle parking only. Using the space for storage, a home gym, or a workshop may violate community rules and result in fines ranging from $50 to $500. Ask about restrictions before signing.

These units lease quickly. In markets where attached garages are scarce, qualified units receive applications within days of listing. Be prepared to apply fast and have your documents ready. brightplace's guide to renting an apartment covers what to prepare.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find an apartment with an attached garage?

Filter your search for townhouse or single-family rental formats in suburban areas. Generic "parking" filters on rental platforms combine multiple parking types into one category and will not isolate attached garages. Contact the leasing office directly and ask whether the garage connects to the unit interior before applying. Expanding your search radius 10-15 miles from the city center typically triples available options.

What do you call an apartment with a garage?

Units with attached garages are most commonly listed as townhouses, townhomes, or single-family rental homes. The term "garage apartment" sometimes refers to a unit built above a detached garage, which is a different configuration entirely. Always verify whether the garage connects directly to the living space before scheduling a tour or submitting an application.

Is the garage usually included in the rent for apartments?

It depends on the property. Some operators include the attached garage in the listed rent. Others bill it separately as a monthly add-on, typically $75 to $200 per month (as of Q3 2026). Ask the leasing office whether the garage is included before comparing rental prices across properties so you are budgeting accurately.

Are attached garages more common in townhouses than apartment buildings?

Yes. Townhouses are the most common building type for attached garages because the ground-floor layout allows a garage to share a wall with the living space. Traditional mid-rise and high-rise apartment buildings almost never include attached garages due to structural constraints. If you want this amenity, filter for townhouse or single-family rental formats.

What is a typical rent premium for an apartment with an attached garage?

Units with attached garages typically rent at a 10-20% premium over comparable units without dedicated parking (as of Q3 2026). On a $1,600 base rent, that translates to $160-$320 more per month. A two-car garage commands a higher premium, typically $250-$400/mo above a comparable unit without parking.

Can I use my attached garage for storage?

That depends on the lease and community rules. In HOA-managed and condo-style communities, garages are often restricted to vehicle parking only, and violations can result in fines of $50 to $500. Single-family rental homes with private landlords tend to be more flexible. Review the lease terms and ask the landlord directly before assuming you can use the space for storage or a workshop.

What should I ask before renting an apartment with a garage?

Ask five questions: (1) Does the garage connect directly to the interior of my unit? (2) Is the garage cost included in the listed rent or is it an additional monthly fee? (3) Are there any restrictions on how I can use the garage space? (4) Is there a 240V electrical outlet for EV charging? (5) What are the garage dimensions (1-car or 2-car)? These answers prevent the most common surprises renters encounter after move-in.

Does parking in a garage lower auto insurance rates?

Many major insurers offer a garaging discount of up to 18% on comprehensive coverage when you park in a private, locked garage (as of Q3 2026). Allstate, Progressive, USAA, and AAA all offer this discount. On a $1,200/year comprehensive policy, that can save roughly $216 annually. Tell your insurer you park in an attached residential garage to receive the reduced rate.

Which cities have the most apartments with attached garages?

Dallas-Fort Worth has the highest concentration nationally, followed by Houston, Phoenix, Austin, and Denver (as of Q3 2026). Midwest cities including Columbus, Indianapolis, and Kansas City also have growing inventory at lower price points, with two-bedroom townhouses starting below $1,500/mo. Attached garages are concentrated in suburban rings, not urban cores.

Can I charge an electric vehicle in my garage apartment?

Yes, if the garage has the right electrical setup. A standard 120V outlet provides Level 1 charging at 3-5 miles of range per hour, enough for many commuters. A 240V outlet delivers Level 2 charging at 20-30 miles per hour. Ask your landlord whether a 240V outlet exists or can be installed, who pays for the electricity, and whether the lease or HOA allows electrical modifications.

How big is a standard attached garage in a rental?

A standard 1-car attached garage measures roughly 12 feet wide by 20 feet deep, approximately 240 square feet. A 2-car attached garage measures roughly 20 feet wide by 20 feet deep, approximately 400 square feet. Most rental townhouses have 1-car garages, while single-family rental homes are more likely to offer 2-car garages.
