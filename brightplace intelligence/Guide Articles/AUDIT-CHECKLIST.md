## SYSTEMIC GAPS (affect almost every article)

These are not one-off mistakes. These are patterns across the entire Guides collection that need batch fixes.

### GAP 1: ZERO FAQ SECTIONS (28 of 30 articles)
- **28 articles have NO FAQ section at all**
- Only `how-to-rent-an-apartment` (14 FAQs) and `your-true-monthly-cost` (8 FAQs) have any
- FAQ sections are the #1 structure for AI engine citation (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)
- **Impact:** Massive lost ranking potential. Each missing FAQ = 10+ lost PAA entry points per article
- **Fix:** Add 10+ FAQ pairs to every guide. Source questions from PAA, Reddit research, and community research agents

### GAP 2: ZERO DATE STAMPS ON DOLLAR FIGURES (27 of 30 articles)
- **27 articles cite rent prices without any "(as of Q[N] YYYY)" date stamps**
- These articles contain 600+ dollar figures collectively with ZERO freshness markers
- Only `how-to-rent-an-apartment` and `your-true-monthly-cost` have date stamps
- **Impact:** Google's freshness ranking factor penalizes undated claims. AI engines skip stale-looking content
- **Fix:** Add "(as of Q3 2026)" to every dollar figure, rent range, and statistic

### GAP 3: ZERO CTAs IN EVERY GUIDE (30 of 30 articles)
- **Not a single guide has CTA links to app.brightplace.ai**
- Zero conversion pathways. Readers finish the guide and have nowhere to go
- Workflow requires 3 CTAs per article (after first H2, mid-article, end)
- **Impact:** Zero traffic to app. Guides generate awareness but no action
- **Fix:** Add 3 CTAs per article with informational framing ("Search [city] apartments on brightplace")

### GAP 4: FOCUS KEYWORD APPEARS 0 TIMES (30 of 30 articles)
- **Every article's focus keyword has 0 exact-match instances in the body**
- This means the keyword in the CMS `focus-keyword` field never appears verbatim in the article text
- Target: 7-12 exact-match instances for ranking
- **Impact:** Critical SEO failure. Google cannot strongly associate these articles with their target keywords
- **Fix:** Naturally weave the exact-match focus keyword into H1, first paragraph, 2+ H2 headings, and body (7-12 total)

### GAP 5: NON-QUESTION H2 HEADINGS (29 of 30 articles)
- **29 articles use declarative H2 headings instead of question-format**
- Question-format H2s capture featured snippets at 3x the rate of label-format
- Examples of what needs to change:
  - "South End" → "What Makes South End One of Charlotte's Most Popular Rental Areas?"
  - "Budget Breakdown" → "How Much Does It Really Cost to Rent in Denver?"
- **Impact:** 3x lower featured snippet capture across the entire Guides collection
- **Fix:** Rewrite H2 headings to match PAA queries (question format)

### GAP 6: WEAK INTERNAL LINKING (12 of 30 articles below target)
- 12 articles have fewer than 7 internal links
- 5 articles have ZERO internal links: brooklyn, dog-friendly-san-diego, relocating-to-austin, minneapolis (1), your-true-monthly-cost (2)
- **Impact:** No topical authority passed between pages. Orphaned content doesn't rank
- **Fix:** Add 7+ internal links per article from the live sitemap

### GAP 7: MISSING EXTERNAL AUTHORITY LINKS (8 of 30 articles)
- 8 articles have ZERO external links to .gov/.edu/official sources
- Google's 2026 core updates reward pages that cite primary sources
- **Impact:** Lower E-E-A-T signals, weaker ranking
- **Fix:** Add 3-5 .gov/.edu/official transit/parks links per article
