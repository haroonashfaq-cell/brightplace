## TOP KEYWORD OPPORTUNITIES (Ranked by potential)

### TIER 1: HIGH VOLUME + LOW KD (write these first)

#### 1. "cheap apartments austin tx"
- **Est. Volume:** 3,000-5,000/mo
- **Est. KD:** 15-25
- **Intent:** Transactional/informational
- **SERP reality:** ILS platforms dominate but thin on actual neighborhood guidance. Google shows a mix of listings + guides.
- **Our angle:** Neighborhood-by-neighborhood breakdown of where sub-$1,000 and sub-$1,200 inventory actually exists (Riverside, Rundberg, Wells Branch, Parker Lane). Include the data that 839 units under $1,000 exist as of 2026. No competitor publishes neighborhood-specific affordable guides with real price floors.
- **Why we win:** ILS pages list apartments. We explain WHERE the affordable pockets are and WHY, with date-stamped data.
- **Internal link to:** how-to-rent-an-apartment, your-true-monthly-cost, rent-affordability-18-an-hour

#### 2. "cost of living austin texas"
- **Est. Volume:** 8,000-12,000/mo
- **Est. KD:** 30-40
- **Intent:** Informational
- **SERP reality:** Mix of calculators and guides. ApartmentList, RentCafe, JVM Lending rank. No strong renter-focused guide with worked monthly budgets.
- **Our angle:** Full monthly budget breakdown for different renter profiles (single professional, couple, family). Rent ($1,412 avg) + utilities + insurance + transit + food + entertainment. Include the fact that Austin rent is 15% below national average and has fallen faster than any other large US city.
- **Why we win:** Competitors give averages. We give worked budgets by lifestyle, date-stamped.
- **Internal link to:** your-true-monthly-cost, austin-young-professionals, relocating-to-austin

#### 3. "pet friendly apartments austin tx"
- **Est. Volume:** 2,500-4,000/mo
- **Est. KD:** 20-30
- **Intent:** Transactional/informational
- **SERP reality:** ILS listings dominate. Only AptAmigo and TacoStreet have guide-style content. 80% of Austin listings allow pets (Zillow data).
- **Our angle:** Neighborhood guide for dog owners in Austin: Mueller (most walkable, pocket parks), South Austin (Lady Bird Lake, Zilker, Barton Creek), East Austin (off-leash areas). Include pet fee structures ($300-$600 deposit, $25-$75/mo pet rent), breed restrictions by neighborhood, dog park inventory.
- **Why we win:** We cover neighborhoods by pet infrastructure, not just "pet friendly = yes/no" from listing sites.
- **Internal link to:** apartments-with-dog-parks, pet-deposit-vs-pet-fee, cat-friendly-apartments

#### 4. "apartments near the domain austin"
- **Est. Volume:** 2,000-3,500/mo
- **Est. KD:** 15-25
- **Intent:** Transactional/informational
- **SERP reality:** ILS platforms + Domain property sites. No independent renter guide covers Domain living comprehensively.
- **Our angle:** The Domain as a neighborhood: rent ranges ($2,300-$2,600 for 1BR), tech employer proximity (Apple, Meta, Oracle, Indeed), walkability within Domain vs car-dependency outside it, comparison to Mueller as alternative master-planned community. Include the 8-10 weeks free concessions currently running in North Austin.
- **Why we win:** No guide frames Domain as a neighborhood decision. They all list apartments. We explain the lifestyle tradeoffs.
- **Internal link to:** austin-young-professionals, relocating-to-austin

#### 5. "austin apartments under 1000"
- **Est. Volume:** 1,500-2,500/mo
- **Est. KD:** 10-20

- **SERP reality:** Listing pages only. Zero guide content.

- **Est. KD:** 5-15
- **Our angle:** Mueller as a neighborhood: master-planned walkability, rent ranges ($2,015 1BR, $2,612 2BR avg), comparison to The Domain, family-friendliness, retail/restaurant scene.

#### 14. "north loop austin apartments"
- **Est. Volume:** 300-600/mo
- **Est. KD:** 5-10
- **Our angle:** North Loop living: vintage Austin character, 1BR avg $1,364, 5% rent drop YoY, walkable local businesses, proximity to UT and Hyde Park.

#### 15. "austin apartments with pool"
- **Est. Volume:** 500-1,000/mo
- **Est. KD:** 10-20
- **Our angle:** Pool guide for Austin renters: which complexes have resort-style pools, rooftop pools, and year-round heated pools. Neighborhood breakdown of best pool amenities.

#### 16. "rent vs buy austin texas 2026"
- **Est. Volume:** 300-800/mo
- **Est. KD:** 10-20
- **Our angle:** The math on renting vs buying in Austin right now: median home price vs equivalent rent, monthly cost comparison, the breakeven timeline, why Austin's rent correction makes renting rational for 3-5 year stays.

#### 17. "austin neighborhoods to avoid" / "where not to live austin"
- **Est. Volume:** 1,000-2,000/mo
- **Est. KD:** 15-25
- **CAUTION:** Fair Housing risk. Cannot describe areas by demographics or crime.
- **Our angle:** Reframe as "Austin Neighborhoods: What to Know Before Signing a Lease" — cover flood zones, noise corridors (airport, I-35), RUBS utility billing traps, commute dead zones. Useful content without Fair Housing violations.
