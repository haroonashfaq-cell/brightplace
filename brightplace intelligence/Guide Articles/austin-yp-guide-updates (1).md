## 1. AMLI Eastside card: full rebuild (property was renamed and sold)

The building at 1000 San Marcos St is no longer AMLI Eastside. It is now
Bridge at Eastside, owned under the Bridge brand and operated by AMP Texas
(Apartment Management Pros). AMLI is out. The whole card needs changing, not
just the price.

| Field | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Property name | AMLI Eastside | Bridge at Eastside |
| Operator label | AMLI Residential | Bridge |
| Outbound link | amli.com AMLI Eastside page | https://www.bridgeateastside.com |
| Price line | From ~$1,771/month | From ~$1,785/month |

Notes:
- Price is the 1BR base from the property's own leasing feed. Aggregators list a
  $1,695 one-bedroom, so ~$1,695 to ~$1,785 is the honest range. I used the
  higher figure so it matches what a reader sees after clicking through to the
  property site.
- Building is 1-2 bed only now, no studios.
- Live concession is 6 weeks free on select units, not the old "1 month free."
- If any card copy still says "AMLI," "AMLI Residential," or references AMLI
  amenities by that name, scrub it.
