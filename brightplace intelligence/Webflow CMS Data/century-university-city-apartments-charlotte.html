What Is Century University City?

Century University City is an apartment community at 5340 Periwinkle Hill Ave, Charlotte, NC 28213, in the University City neighborhood. The property offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans with starting rents from the $1,100s for a one-bedroom (as of Q3 2026). Managed by Asset Living, the community sits a short drive from UNC Charlotte and the University Research Park employment corridor. What the Apartments Include

Century University City units feature quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, custom cabinets, subway tile backsplash, and kitchen islands with pendant lighting. The University City Neighborhood in Charlotte

University City sits in northeast Charlotte along the I-85 corridor. UNC Charlotte anchors the area, and University Research Park provides a second employment cluster nearby. The CATS Lynx Blue Line serves the corridor with stops at University City Blvd and JW Clay Blvd stations, connecting to Uptown Charlotte in approximately 25-30 minutes.

Grocery options include Harris Teeter and Food Lion within a short drive. Dining is concentrated along W.T. Harris Blvd and University City Blvd, with a mix of chains and independent restaurants. Mallard Creek Greenway and the UNC Charlotte Botanical Gardens provide outdoor access nearby.

The honest tradeoff: University City is a car-friendly area with improving transit access, but renters who prioritize walkable retail and dining density will find South End, NoDa, or Midtown Charlotte a better lifestyle fit. Academic lease cycle: The University City rental market follows an academic lease cycle, which is the pattern of availability and pricing that follows UNC Charlotte's enrollment calendar.
