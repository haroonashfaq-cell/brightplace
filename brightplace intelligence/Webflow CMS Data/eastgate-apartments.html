A few locations, including Manchester NH and New Prague MN, offer townhome-style units with private entrances.

Common amenities across multiple Eastgate properties include swimming pools, fitness centers, on-site laundry, and outdoor spaces such as patios, balconies, and playgrounds. Not every community has every feature. The Spokane Valley WA location, built in 2017, includes a dog park and 24-hour fitness center. How to Evaluate an Eastgate Apartment Before Signing

Five practical steps apply to any Eastgate community, regardless of city. No competing property page covers these, so this section fills a gap that renters searching by name will find useful.

Confirm the correct property. Always use the street address, not just the name. Multiple unrelated properties share the "Eastgate" name. Add the city and zip code to every search to avoid confusion.

Ask about effective rent, not listed rent. Effective rent is what you actually pay over the lease term after accounting for concessions like free months or reduced move-in costs. Listed rent and effective rent can differ by hundreds of dollars. Ask what you will pay in month one and month 13.

Understand the affordability classification. Market-rate and income-qualified Eastgate properties look similar online but have very different application processes. Income-qualified communities require income verification, may have waiting lists, and participate in federal programs like Section 8 PBRA or LIHTC. Market-rate properties do not.

Verify the pet policy in writing. Breed restriction lists vary by property and management company. If you have a dog, especially a mixed breed, confirm the policy before paying an application fee.

Check the management company. Eastgate properties are managed by different companies: Parawest in Tulsa, Monarch Investment in Wichita, JCM Management in Manchester, and others. Management quality varies. Ask about maintenance response times and online rent payment options during your tour. Eastgate Apartments for Rent: Market-Rate vs. Income-Qualified Properties

Some Eastgate-branded communities are fully income-qualified, and this distinction matters more than almost any other detail.

Market-rate Eastgate communities have no income limits. You apply, qualify based on credit and income, and sign a lease. Examples include Tulsa OK (rent starting at $800/month as of Q3 2026), Wichita KS ($750-$1,260/month as of Q3 2026), Spokane Valley WA ($1,210-$1,725/month as of Q3 2026), and Manchester NH ($1,500-$1,975/month as of Q3 2026).

Income-qualified Eastgate communities require household income below a specific percentage of Area Median Income (AMI). Area Median Income is the midpoint of household income for a region, calculated annually by HUD and used to determine eligibility for income-restricted housing. Programs vary:

Project-Based Rental Assistance (PBRA): PBRA is a federal HUD program that attaches a rent subsidy to a specific apartment unit. Qualified residents pay approximately 30% of their income for rent while the program covers the remainder. When a resident moves out, the subsidy stays with the unit. The Mishawaka IN Eastgate community operates under this program and requires household income at or below 50% of AMI.

Low Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC): The LIHTC program gives tax credits to property owners who build or renovate apartments rented at below-market rates to income-qualified residents. The Portland OR Eastgate Station (61 units, including 60 income-restricted) and the Elmira NY property (102 units, 60% AMI limit) both participate in LIHTC. At Elmira, one-bedroom rents run $701-$979/month and two-bedroom rents run $834-$1,236/month (as of Q3 2026).

Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (HCV): A tenant-held rental subsidy that qualified low-income renters can use at any participating landlord. LIHTC properties are federally required to accept HCVs. If you hold a voucher, confirm with the specific Eastgate property whether they accept it before applying.
