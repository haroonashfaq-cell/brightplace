# Parkside at Legacy Is Now Legacy North: What Plano Renters Should Know

## What Is Legacy North (Formerly Parkside at Legacy)?

Legacy North, formerly known as Parkside at Legacy, is a Class A apartment community at 5765 Bozeman Dr, Plano, TX 75024. The property sits within walking distance of Legacy West's retail and restaurant strip in the Legacy corridor. It offers studio, 1BR, and 2BR floor plans starting from approximately $1,102/month (as of Q3 2026).

The property was rebranded from Parkside at Legacy to Legacy North and is now managed by Pacific Living. If you searched for Parkside at Legacy Plano, this is the same community under its new name. Legacy North encompasses what was previously known as Northside at Legacy (phases I and II) and Parkside at Legacy (phases III, IV, and V), totaling 1,465 units across 99 distinct floor plans.

A Class A apartment community is a rental property built within the last 15 years with premium finishes, full amenity packages, and professional management, typically at the upper end of local pricing. Legacy North was built in 2012 and remains pet-friendly, though breed and weight restrictions apply. Confirm the current pet policy and [pet fees vs. pet deposits](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/pet-deposit-vs-pet-fee) directly with the leasing office before applying.

### What Are the In-Unit Features at Legacy North?

Interior finishes vary by unit tier. Legacy North offers multiple finish levels including Eclipse, Classic, Aurora, Equinox, and Meridian packages.

**Countertops:** Granite or quartz depending on unit tier.

**Appliances:** Stainless steel package standard across all tiers.

**Flooring:** Wood-style flooring throughout.

**Laundry:** In-unit washer and dryer.

**Outdoor space:** Private balconies on select floor plans.

### What Community Amenities Does Legacy North Have?

**Pools:** Six resort-style swimming pools across the community.

**Fitness:** Two-level fitness center.

**Recreation:** Full-size sand volleyball court and pet parks with dog wash and grooming stations.

**Business:** Business center with coworking space.

**Parking:** Controlled-access garage parking and covered parking options. Garage parking is a practical advantage during Texas summers, where surface lot temperatures regularly exceed 100 degrees. Ask the leasing team whether garage spots carry an additional monthly fee, as pricing structures vary by floor plan and availability.

## Why Does the Legacy Corridor Location Matter for Renters?

The Legacy West submarket is one of the few areas in suburban DFW where walkable retail and corporate employment proximity coexist. The Legacy corridor refers to the stretch of Plano running along the Dallas North Tollway between roughly Legacy Drive and Headquarters Drive, anchored by Legacy Business Park and the Legacy West mixed-use development.

### Which Employers Are Near Legacy North?

The following employers all maintain major campuses within approximately 1-3 miles of Legacy North in the Legacy corridor:

**Toyota North America:** Headquartered at its $1B, 100-acre Legacy West campus in Plano.

**JPMorgan Chase:** Over 11,000 employees at its expanded Legacy campus (Phase II completed), making it Plano's largest employer.

**AT&T:** Building a $1.35 billion, 54-acre global headquarters at 5400 Legacy Drive in Plano. This campus will consolidate approximately 10,000 employees from Dallas, Plano, and Irving locations. First phase delivery expected mid-2026, with full completion by end of 2029.

**Liberty Mutual:** Approximately 5,000 employees at its West Plano campus.

**FedEx Office:** Headquartered at 7900 Legacy Dr with approximately 1,200 employees.

**Ericsson:** North American headquarters at Ericsson Village in the Legacy corridor.

**HP:** Maintains operations in the Plano area.

For corporate relocators following a [relocation playbook](https://brightplace.ai/guides/nashville-corporate-relocation-neighborhoods) similar to other Sun Belt metros, Legacy North puts the daily commute at minutes rather than highway segments.

### How Walkable Is the Legacy West Area?

Walking access to Legacy West from Legacy North puts Whole Foods, Shops at Legacy, and a concentrated restaurant row within reach on foot. That kind of walkability is uncommon in suburban DFW and accounts for part of the rent premium in the Legacy corridor.

### How Do You Get to Downtown Dallas from Legacy North?

The CityLine/Bush [DART station](https://www.dart.org/) on the Red and Orange Lines sits approximately 2-3 miles from Legacy North. DART rail connects to Downtown Dallas without a car, with travel times of roughly 30-40 minutes depending on the line and time of day.

A major transit improvement since 2025: the DART Silver Line opened in October 2025. This 26-mile regional rail service connects Plano to Grapevine, Addison, Richardson, Dallas, and DFW Airport Terminal B. The Silver Line connects at the CityLine/Bush station, adding a direct airport link that did not previously exist from the Legacy corridor. Weekday trains run from 4am to 1am with 30-minute peak headways.

### What Schools Serve the Legacy North Area?

[Plano ISD](https://www.pisd.edu/) serves the Legacy North area. The district holds a TEA rating of B (82/100), with math proficiency at 58% (vs. 44% state average) and reading proficiency at 64% (vs. 51% state average) (as of 2024-2025). Plano ISD is consistently ranked among the strongest large school districts in Texas. ## What Are the Honest Tradeoffs of Living Near Legacy West?

**Tollway congestion.** The Dallas North Tollway and SH-121 (Sam Rayburn Tollway) interchange creates significant congestion during peak commute hours. At current [NTTA](https://www.ntta.org/) rates of $0.22/mile with TollTag, a round-trip daily commute from the Legacy corridor to Downtown Dallas runs approximately $8-$14/day (as of Q3 2026). Over a 20-day work month, that adds $160-$280 to your effective housing cost.

**Car dependency outside the corridor.** Walkability within Legacy West is strong by DFW standards, but destinations outside the immediate corridor still require a car. Grocery runs beyond Whole Foods, medical appointments, and weekend activities in other parts of the metroplex all mean driving.

**Construction disruption.** The Legacy corridor is in an active construction phase (as of Q3 2026). AT&T's $1.35B headquarters campus, a 22-story luxury tower at the former JCPenney HQ site (Park at Legacy), and 385 new apartments at Legacy Central are all under construction simultaneously. Expect increased traffic, noise, and visual disruption through at least 2029.

## How Does Legacy North Compare to Nearby Apartments?

No single Legacy corridor community is the right fit for every renter. The differences come down to walkability, price positioning, and concession timing rather than dramatic quality gaps.

**Legacy North (formerly Parkside at Legacy):** Walks to Legacy West retail. Optimal corporate campus commute for Legacy corridor employers. Six pools and the largest unit count in the submarket (1,465 units). Currently offering up to four weeks free (as of Q3 2026).

**Cortland Legacy (Frisco):** Walk to Legacy West retail varies by unit. Similar price-per-square-foot tier with 1BR units starting from approximately $1,190 (as of Q3 2026). Located in Frisco rather than Plano, which means Frisco ISD instead of Plano ISD. Currently offering $500 off.

**Instrata at Legacy West:** Newer construction directly within the Legacy West mixed-use development. Premium positioning with higher price per square foot. Strong walkability to retail and dining.

**The Grand at Legacy West:** Luxury positioning in the Legacy West area. Comparable corporate campus commute. Higher price point than Legacy North.

All of these communities compete for the same renter profile, which means concession packages shift frequently. The Plano/Frisco Class A market continues to see elevated vacancy (as of Q3 2026), so concessions may be available at any of these properties. Ask each leasing office what they are currently offering before committing.

Renters open to a different lifestyle entirely should consider communities in Uptown Dallas or Turtle Creek, which trade corporate campus proximity for a more urban environment.

## What Are Renters Saying About Legacy North?

Legacy North (formerly Parkside at Legacy) has accumulated over 2,500 reviews across platforms, with overall ratings between approximately 3.4 and 4.0 out of 5 depending on the source (as of Q3 2026).

Consistent positive feedback includes the Legacy West location, gym quality, and the convenience of having retail, dining, and employers within walking distance. Renters consistently cite the Legacy corridor area as one of the most convenient in suburban DFW.

Consistent negative feedback centers on management responsiveness, maintenance turnaround times, and move-in readiness of units. Recent reviews from 2025-2026 note specific concerns about elevator reliability, communication with the leasing office, and grounds maintenance. These are common themes across large Class A communities in high-turnover submarkets but are worth asking about directly.

Rather than relying on aggregated review scores, ask specific questions during your tour. Request the average maintenance response time for non-emergency work orders. Ask how many parking spots are allocated per unit and whether overflow parking is available. Walk the property and stand near the unit you are considering to assess ambient noise levels yourself.
