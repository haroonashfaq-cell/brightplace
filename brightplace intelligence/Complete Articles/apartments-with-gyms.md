# Apartments with Gyms: What Renters Should Know About Fitness Centers by City in 2026

Apartments with gyms are now the norm, with 93% of complexes including a fitness center as of Q3 2026. An included gym saves renters $600 to $1,800 per year over a commercial membership. Equipment varies widely by property class. This guide covers what to expect at each tier and where to find them by state.

## Do Apartments Have Gyms and Are They Free?

Yes. About 93% of apartment communities now include some form of on-site fitness center, and that number rises to nearly 100% for Class A properties built or renovated after 2015 (as of Q3 2026). Access is almost always bundled into your monthly rent with no separate gym fee.

The word "free" needs a disclaimer, though. You pay for the gym whether you use it or not. The cost is folded into rent or, at some luxury communities, charged as a mandatory monthly amenity fee ranging from $25 to $75 per month (as of Q3 2026). That fee typically covers the pool, clubhouse, and other shared spaces alongside the fitness center. Opting out is not an option at most properties. Renters who need help with overall housing costs can review [HUD's rental assistance resources](https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance) for federal programs.

For renters calculating their [true monthly cost of renting](https://www.brightplace.ai/guides/your-true-monthly-cost), the bundled gym can be a genuine perk or a line item you never touch. Surveys show 82% of renters call an on-site fitness center "important" when choosing an apartment. At the same time, 42% of residents say they rarely or never step inside the building gym. That gap between stated preference and actual behavior is worth being honest about before you factor gym savings into your budget.

## How Much Do Apartment Gyms Save You Compared to a Gym Membership?

An apartment fitness center that replaces even a mid-tier commercial membership saves renters $360 to $720 per year (as of Q3 2026). Replace a premium or boutique studio membership, and the annual savings climb well above $1,000. Here is how the math works across four membership tiers.

**Budget gym (Planet Fitness, Crunch):** $10 to $25/mo, or $120 to $300/yr (as of Q3 2026). Basic cardio and machine circuit. If your apartment gym matches this level, the savings are modest.

**Mid-tier gym (LA Fitness, Gold's Gym):** $30 to $60/mo, or $360 to $720/yr (as of Q3 2026). Free weights, group classes, pool at some locations. Most premium apartment gyms now rival this tier.

**Premium gym (Equinox, Lifetime Fitness):** $100 to $300/mo, or $1,200 to $3,600/yr (as of Q3 2026). Full amenity stack including spa, sauna, towel service. Only top-tier luxury apartment gyms come close.

**Boutique studio (CrossFit box, cycling studio, yoga):** $150 to $250/mo, or $1,800 to $3,000/yr (as of Q3 2026). Specialized programming. Apartment gyms rarely replicate this experience.

**Apartment fitness center:** $0 additional at most communities. Included in rent.

One real-world example captures the dynamic well: a renter who canceled a $275/month Equinox membership after discovering the building gym met their cardio and light lifting needs saved $3,300 over a year. That said, renters who need Olympic lifting platforms, heavy squat racks, or a specific class format will likely still need a commercial gym. The apartment gym works for the renter whose routine centers on cardio, dumbbells, and bodyweight work. For dedicated powerlifters, it probably does not. Knowing [what percentage of income should go to rent](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/what-percentage-of-income-should-go-to-rent) helps you decide whether the rent premium for a better gym is worth it.

## What Equipment Do Apartment Fitness Centers Typically Have?

Equipment varies dramatically by property class and year of construction. brightplace reviewed fitness center specifications across hundreds of communities and found three distinct tiers that renters can expect in 2026.

### Basic Tier (Class C and Older Properties)

Expect two to four cardio machines: a treadmill, an elliptical, and a stationary bike. A single multi-station weight machine handles resistance training. Dumbbells typically max out at 50 pounds, and a stretching mat occupies one corner. The room itself is often small, sometimes without air conditioning. Hours may be restricted (5 AM to 11 PM). This is what renters describe as "just cardio machines."

### Premium Tier (Class A and Newer Construction)

Eight to 15 cardio machines including Peloton bikes and rowing machines. A full free weight section with dumbbells up to 75 or 100 pounds, cable machines, and TRX suspension rigs. A dedicated stretching or yoga room sits adjacent. Some properties add a separate spin room. Access is 24 hours via key fob. This tier matches or exceeds a budget commercial gym.

### Boutique-Level Tier (Luxury and High-Rise)

A full gym rivaling commercial facilities. CrossFit-style functional training zones with plyo boxes and battle ropes. Rowing machines, Peloton bikes and treadmills, a dedicated yoga or Pilates studio, and a scheduled group fitness class calendar. Personal training may be available on site. Some properties partner with fitness brands like Tonal or Technogym for smart equipment. Towel service and filtered water stations round out the experience.

A notable 2026 trend: CrossFit-style equipment demand among renters has reached 40%, and TRX demand sits at 41%, both surpassing Peloton bike demand at 28%. Properties still investing only in connected bikes are already behind renter preferences. Apartment communities with [attached garages](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/apartments-with-attached-garages) and premium gyms tend to cluster in the same Class A developments.

## What Should You Ask About the Gym During an Apartment Tour?

The fitness center looks different at 2 PM on a Tuesday than it does at 6 PM on a Monday. Renters who treat the gym as a serious factor in their decision should go beyond the standard tour and ask pointed questions.

Start with hours: Is the fitness center open 24 hours, or does it lock at 10 PM? Ask what equipment is available and request a walk-through of the actual space, not just a photo. Confirm whether group fitness classes exist and if they cost extra. Ask about any separate monthly amenity fee for gym access.

Two questions most renters skip: When was the equipment last replaced or upgraded? Good management companies refresh machines every three to five years. And are there capacity limits or reservation requirements during peak hours? Buildings with 300-plus units and only six machines will have congestion problems that photos never reveal.

If the gym matters to you, brightplace's [questions to ask when touring an apartment](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/questions-to-ask-when-touring-an-apartment) covers the full checklist. Before signing any lease, the [CFPB's renter resources](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/housing/housing-insecurity/help-for-renters/) provide a solid overview of your rights regarding fees and disclosures.

## Are Apartment Gyms Open 24 Hours?

Most Class A apartment communities built since 2015 offer 24-hour fitness center access controlled by key fob or smart lock (as of Q3 2026). The pandemic permanently shifted workout habits toward early morning and late-night sessions, and operators adapted by removing staffed-hour requirements.

Older or smaller communities may still restrict access to 5 AM through 11 PM. Luxury high-rises sometimes run a hybrid model: staffed hours during the day with unstaffed fob access overnight. Ask about holiday closures and scheduled maintenance windows, which can catch renters off guard in December and January.

Peak usage hits between 6 and 8 AM and again from 5 to 7 PM on weekdays. In smaller buildings with 50 to 100 units, you may share the gym with three to five people at peak times. In communities with 300-plus units, expect wait times for treadmills and popular machines during those same windows. Off-peak hours, especially late morning and mid-afternoon, are typically empty. Renters who already keep a [month-to-month lease](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/month-to-month-vs-12-month-lease) for flexibility often value 24-hour gym access as part of that same convenience-first approach.

Communities that also have [dog parks](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/apartments-with-dog-parks) tend to be the same newer developments offering round-the-clock fitness access and modern equipment. The amenity package clusters together at well-managed properties.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Apartments with Gyms

### Do some apartments have gyms?

Yes. Approximately 93% of apartment communities in the United States include an on-site fitness center as of Q3 2026. The number is even higher for newer Class A and Class B properties built or renovated since 2015. Most are included in rent at no additional charge. Equipment ranges from basic cardio machines at older communities to CrossFit-style training zones at luxury developments.

### Can you have a gym in an apartment?

Apartment fitness centers are shared community amenities, not private in-unit gyms. They occupy a dedicated space within the complex, accessible to all residents via key fob or smart lock. Some renters supplement with compact home equipment like resistance bands, adjustable dumbbells, or smart fitness mirrors in their units for days when the shared gym is crowded.

### Why do some apartments have gyms?

Apartment operators add fitness centers to attract and retain residents. On-site gyms consistently rank among the most requested amenities in renter surveys, with 82% of renters calling them important. For operators, a quality fitness center reduces turnover and improves competitiveness. For renters, it eliminates the cost and commute time of maintaining a separate gym membership.

### Are apartment gyms any good?

Quality varies significantly by property class and management company. Newer Class A communities offer fitness center quality comparable to mid-tier commercial gyms, with cardio machines, free weights, cable systems, and sometimes Peloton bikes or TRX equipment. Older Class C properties may only stock a few aging machines. Always tour the fitness center during peak hours before signing a lease.

### Is the gym at my apartment free?

In most cases, yes. Apartment fitness centers are typically bundled into your monthly rent with no separate gym fee. Some luxury properties charge a mandatory monthly amenity fee of $25 to $75 (as of Q3 2026) that covers the gym, pool, and other shared amenities. You cannot opt out of this fee. Ask about any additional charges before signing your lease.

### How much does an apartment gym save you?

An apartment gym can save renters $360 to $3,600 per year depending on the commercial gym they would otherwise use (as of Q3 2026). A mid-tier membership costs $30 to $60 per month. A premium membership runs $100 to $300 per month. An apartment fitness center replaces that expense entirely when the cost is already included in rent.

### Do apartment gyms have group fitness classes?

Some do. Premium and luxury apartment communities increasingly offer group fitness classes including yoga, cycling, HIIT, and strength training sessions. Many use on-demand digital platforms like Fitness On Demand or Peloton streaming to supplement in-person programming. Availability varies by property. Ask the leasing office about the class schedule and whether classes carry any extra cost.

### What is the difference between an apartment gym and a commercial gym?

Apartment gyms are smaller and carry less equipment variety than commercial gyms. They typically lack swimming lanes, basketball courts, saunas, and extensive group class schedules. The tradeoff is convenience (steps from your unit), cost (included in rent), and low crowding (residents only). Renters who need specialized training equipment or programming may still need a commercial membership alongside.

### Can non-residents use an apartment gym?

Typically no. Apartment fitness centers are restricted to residents and sometimes their registered guests. Most communities require key fob or app-based access that is tied to your lease. Some properties allow a limited guest pass for visitors. Unauthorized gym use can result in lease violations. Check your community's guest policy with the leasing office before bringing visitors.

### What apartment communities have strong gym facilities?

Operators known for premium fitness amenities include Cortland (Peloton bikes, free group classes), AMLI (resort-style fitness centers with spin rooms), Camden Property Trust (24-hour gyms across Sun Belt markets), and MAA (yoga and TRX studios). Luxury high-rises in cities like Miami, Dallas, Denver, and Nashville tend to have the most extensive gym facilities and equipment budgets.

### Are apartment gyms crowded?

Peak hours between 6 and 8 AM and 5 and 7 PM on weekdays see the most traffic. Smaller communities with 50 to 100 units may have three to five people using the gym at peak times. Larger communities with 300-plus units can experience real wait times for popular equipment. Off-peak hours and 24-hour access help renters avoid the congestion entirely.

### Do apartment gyms have free weights?

Most newer apartment fitness centers include free weights, typically dumbbells ranging from 5 to 50 pounds. Premium communities extend the rack to 75 or 100 pounds and add benches, squat racks, and cable machines. Basic fitness centers at older properties may only carry a multi-station machine with no free weight section at all. Tour the gym and count the equipment before signing.
