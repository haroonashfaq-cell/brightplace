# What to Know About Camden Copper Square in Downtown Phoenix

Camden Copper Square apartments in Phoenix AZ is a 332-unit gated apartment community located at 901 E. Van Buren St., Phoenix, AZ 85006, in the Copper Square district of downtown Phoenix. The property spans approximately 8 acres across nine 3-story buildings and offers studio, 1-bedroom, and 2-bedroom floor plans ranging from 718 to 1,307 sq ft. Originally developed and managed by Camden Property Trust, the community was acquired by ColRich in November 2025 for $77 million (as of Q4 2025).

## What the $77 Million Sale Means for Renters

ColRich acquired Camden Copper Square from Camden Property Trust, an S&P 500 real estate investment trust, in November 2025 for $77 million (as of Q4 2025). That works out to approximately $231,928 per unit (as of Q4 2025).

This type of transaction is what the industry calls a "value-add acquisition." In plain terms, it means the buyer purchased the property at a price that assumes future improvements will justify higher rents. The acquirer typically plans renovations to unit interiors, common areas, or both, then raises rents to reflect the upgraded product.

For renters, this creates a two-sided picture. On one hand, value-add renovations often bring genuinely improved finishes, updated appliances, and refreshed common areas. On the other hand, those improvements are funded by and reflected in rent increases. ColRich owns 13 multifamily communities across metro Phoenix (as of Q2 2026), so the company has an established operational footprint in the market.

If you are touring Camden Copper Square, ask the leasing office directly about the current management company, any planned renovation timeline, and how rent pricing will be handled for new and renewing leases. These are standard questions, and a transparent leasing team will have clear answers.
