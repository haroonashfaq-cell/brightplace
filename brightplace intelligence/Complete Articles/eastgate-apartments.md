# What to Know About Eastgate Apartments Before You Rent

*Last reviewed: August 2026*

## "Eastgate Apartments" Is a Name Shared by Communities in Dozens of U.S. Cities

Eastgate Apartments is not a single company or national brand. The name is used by unrelated property owners in more than ten U.S. cities, including Tulsa OK, Wichita KS, Mishawaka IN, Manchester NH, Portland OR, and Spokane Valley WA. Some Eastgate communities are market-rate rentals. Others participate in federal affordability programs with income limits and waiting lists. The first step in any search for Eastgate Apartments is confirming which city, address, and affordability classification applies to you.

In reviewing listings across these markets, brightplace confirmed Eastgate-branded communities under different ownership groups, different management companies, and different subsidy structures. This article covers what each property offers and how to evaluate the one in your market.

## What Eastgate Apartment Communities Typically Offer

Most Eastgate communities share a similar profile: one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments with square footage ranging from roughly 595 to 1,200 sq ft depending on the market and unit type. A few locations, including Manchester NH and New Prague MN, offer townhome-style units with private entrances.

Common amenities across multiple Eastgate properties include swimming pools, fitness centers, on-site laundry, and outdoor spaces such as patios, balconies, and playgrounds. Not every community has every feature. The Spokane Valley WA location, built in 2017, includes a dog park and 24-hour fitness center. The Manchester NH property sits on 17 acres and includes a basketball court and tot lot. Always confirm specific amenities directly with the leasing office before applying.

Pet policies vary by property but follow a consistent pattern. Many Eastgate communities allow pets with restrictions: a two-pet maximum, weight limits (30 lbs in Tulsa, higher in other markets), breed restrictions covering Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Chow Chows, and similar breeds, a refundable pet deposit, and monthly pet rent. In Wichita, pet rent runs $40/month for one pet or $75/month for two pets, with a $300 pet fee for the first pet (as of Q3 2026). Confirm your property's policy in writing before applying. For more on how [pet deposits and pet fees](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/pet-deposit-vs-pet-fee) work, see brightplace's breakdown.

Standard lease terms at market-rate Eastgate communities are 12 months. Some properties offer concessions during promotional periods. The Wichita location recently offered $500 off the first full month's rent (as of Q3 2026). The New Prague MN property advertised up to $2,000 in free rent with a 13-month lease (as of Q3 2026). These promotions change frequently, so ask about current specials when you call.

## How to Evaluate an Eastgate Apartment Before Signing

Five practical steps apply to any Eastgate community, regardless of city. No competing property page covers these, so this section fills a gap that renters searching by name will find useful.

**Confirm the correct property.** Always use the street address, not just the name. Multiple unrelated properties share the "Eastgate" name. Add the city and zip code to every search to avoid confusion.

**Ask about effective rent, not listed rent.** Effective rent is what you actually pay over the lease term after accounting for concessions like free months or reduced move-in costs. Listed rent and effective rent can differ by hundreds of dollars. Ask what you will pay in month one and month 13.

**Understand the affordability classification.** Market-rate and income-qualified Eastgate properties look similar online but have very different application processes. Income-qualified communities require income verification, may have waiting lists, and participate in federal programs like Section 8 PBRA or LIHTC. Market-rate properties do not.

**Verify the pet policy in writing.** Breed restriction lists vary by property and management company. If you have a dog, especially a mixed breed, confirm the policy before paying an application fee.

**Check the management company.** Eastgate properties are managed by different companies: Parawest in Tulsa, Monarch Investment in Wichita, JCM Management in Manchester, and others. Management quality varies. Ask about maintenance response times and online rent payment options during your tour. For a full list of [questions to ask when touring an apartment](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/questions-to-ask-when-touring-an-apartment), see brightplace's checklist.

## Eastgate Apartments for Rent: Market-Rate vs. Income-Qualified Properties

Some Eastgate-branded communities are fully income-qualified, and this distinction matters more than almost any other detail.

**Market-rate Eastgate communities** have no income limits. You apply, qualify based on credit and income, and sign a lease. Examples include Tulsa OK (rent starting at $800/month as of Q3 2026), Wichita KS ($750-$1,260/month as of Q3 2026), Spokane Valley WA ($1,210-$1,725/month as of Q3 2026), and Manchester NH ($1,500-$1,975/month as of Q3 2026).

**Income-qualified Eastgate communities** require household income below a specific percentage of Area Median Income (AMI). Area Median Income is the midpoint of household income for a region, calculated annually by [HUD](https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance) and used to determine eligibility for income-restricted housing. Programs vary:

**Project-Based Rental Assistance (PBRA):** PBRA is a federal HUD program that attaches a rent subsidy to a specific apartment unit. Qualified residents pay approximately 30% of their income for rent while the program covers the remainder. When a resident moves out, the subsidy stays with the unit. The Mishawaka IN Eastgate community operates under this program and requires household income at or below 50% of AMI.

**Low Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC):** The LIHTC program gives tax credits to property owners who build or renovate apartments rented at below-market rates to income-qualified residents. The Portland OR Eastgate Station (61 units, including 60 income-restricted) and the Elmira NY property (102 units, 60% AMI limit) both participate in LIHTC. At Elmira, one-bedroom rents run $701-$979/month and two-bedroom rents run $834-$1,236/month (as of Q3 2026).

**Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (HCV):** A tenant-held rental subsidy that qualified low-income renters can use at any participating landlord. LIHTC properties are federally required to accept HCVs. If you hold a voucher, confirm with the specific Eastgate property whether they accept it before applying.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Eastgate Apartments

### What cities have Eastgate Apartments?

Confirmed Eastgate Apartments communities exist in Tulsa OK, Wichita KS, Mishawaka IN, Manchester NH, Spokane Valley WA, Portland OR, Elmira NY, New Prague MN, Triangle VA, and Gainesville FL, among others. The name is used by unrelated property owners in many markets. Always confirm the specific address when searching.

### Are Eastgate Apartments income-restricted or market-rate?

Some Eastgate-branded communities are market-rate, requiring standard income and credit qualification. Others, including communities in Mishawaka IN, Portland OR, Gainesville FL, and Elmira NY, participate in federal affordability programs like Section 8 PBRA or LIHTC and have income limits and waiting lists. Check the specific property's program status before applying.

### Do Eastgate Apartments allow pets?

Most market-rate Eastgate communities are pet-friendly with restrictions. Common policies include a two-pet maximum, weight limits around 30 pounds, breed restrictions covering Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, and similar breeds, a refundable pet deposit, and monthly pet rent. Income-qualified properties may have different rules. Confirm the exact policy with the specific property.

### What floor plan sizes do Eastgate Apartments offer?

Most Eastgate communities offer one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments. Some locations, including Manchester NH and New Prague MN, offer townhome-style layouts with private entrances. Square footage ranges from approximately 595 sq ft for a one-bedroom to over 1,200 sq ft for three-bedroom units, depending on the market.

### How do I apply for Eastgate Apartments with a Section 8 voucher?

LIHTC-participating Eastgate communities are required to accept Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers. PBRA properties attach the subsidy to the unit rather than the resident, so vouchers may not apply there. Contact the specific Eastgate property directly, confirm their subsidy program type, and ask whether they accept HCVs before submitting an application.

### What amenities do Eastgate Apartments typically have?

Common amenities across Eastgate-branded communities include swimming pools, fitness centers, on-site laundry, outdoor patios or balconies, and playground areas. Some locations offer business centers, dog parks, and barbecue areas. Amenities vary by property and market. Confirm available amenities at your specific community directly with the leasing office.

### What is the typical lease term at Eastgate Apartments?

Most market-rate Eastgate communities offer 12-month leases as the standard term. During promotional periods, some communities have offered concessions including $500 off first month's rent or up to $2,000 in free rent with extended lease terms (as of Q3 2026). Ask about current lease terms and any specials when contacting the leasing office.
