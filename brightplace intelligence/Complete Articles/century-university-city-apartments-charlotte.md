# Century University City Apartments in Charlotte: What to Know Before You Tour

## What Is Century University City?

Century University City is an apartment community at 5340 Periwinkle Hill Ave, Charlotte, NC 28213, in the University City neighborhood. The property offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans with starting rents from the $1,100s for a one-bedroom (as of Q3 2026). Managed by Asset Living, the community sits a short drive from UNC Charlotte and the University Research Park employment corridor. If you are researching this property before touring or comparing it to nearby options, this guide covers what the apartments include, what they cost, and how Century fits into the broader University City rental market.

## What the Apartments Include

Century University City units feature quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, custom cabinets, subway tile backsplash, and kitchen islands with pendant lighting. Bathrooms include double-sink vanities, soaking garden tubs, and ceramic tile floors. Units have 9- to 10-foot ceilings, ceiling fans, in-unit washer/dryer, built-in USB ports, and walk-in closets.

### Community Amenities

The property includes a pool with a covered deck and cabanas, outdoor dining terraces with grilling stations, a sports pub with billiards, and a cybercafe with coffee and tea service. ## The University City Neighborhood in Charlotte

University City sits in northeast Charlotte along the I-85 corridor. UNC Charlotte anchors the area, and University Research Park provides a second employment cluster nearby. The CATS Lynx Blue Line serves the corridor with stops at University City Blvd and JW Clay Blvd stations, connecting to Uptown Charlotte in approximately 25-30 minutes.

Grocery options include Harris Teeter and Food Lion within a short drive. Dining is concentrated along W.T. Harris Blvd and University City Blvd, with a mix of chains and independent restaurants. Mallard Creek Greenway and the UNC Charlotte Botanical Gardens provide outdoor access nearby.

The honest tradeoff: University City is a car-friendly area with improving transit access, but renters who prioritize walkable retail and dining density will find South End, NoDa, or Midtown Charlotte a better lifestyle fit. University City suits commuters and campus-adjacent renters well. brightplace's [guide to renting an apartment](https://www.brightplace.ai/guides/how-to-rent-an-apartment) covers what to prepare before you start touring in any market.

**Preferred Employer Program:** Century University City offers a Preferred Employer Program. A Preferred Employer Program is a leasing incentive in which tenants employed by designated companies or institutions receive benefits such as reduced deposits, waived fees, or preferred pricing. Renters employed by UNC Charlotte, Atrium Health, or other major Charlotte employers should ask the leasing team whether their employer participates.

**Student resources:** The property has a dedicated student program and is positioned to serve renters attending UNC Charlotte. Ask about student-specific lease terms or move-in timing options.

**Academic lease cycle:** The University City rental market follows an academic lease cycle, which is the pattern of availability and pricing that follows UNC Charlotte's enrollment calendar. Availability increases in late spring and summer as students complete leases. Renters not tied to the academic calendar often find more options and negotiating room between May and August. Fall and spring create the highest competition.
