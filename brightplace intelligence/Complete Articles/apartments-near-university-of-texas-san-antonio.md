# A Practical Guide to Apartments Near University of Texas San Antonio

*Last reviewed: July 2026. Rent figures reflect available listing data as of Q3 2026 and should be verified directly with each property.*

Apartments near the University of Texas at San Antonio are concentrated along UTSA Boulevard, Babcock Road, and the Loop 1604 corridor. One-bedroom units within two miles of the main campus run $900-$1,400 per month (as of Q3 2026). Units further out along Babcock Road and into Leon Valley drop to $800-$1,100 per month. This guide is for UTSA students and incoming Roadrunners who want to understand the rental market before starting a listing search.

## What Apartments Near UTSA Actually Cost

Rent near UTSA depends almost entirely on distance from the main campus at 1 UTSA Circle, San Antonio, TX 78249. Purpose-built student housing along UTSA Boulevard prices at a premium for walkability and shuttle access. Market-rate apartments two to five miles out offer more space at lower prices but require a car or reliable shuttle connection.

One-bedroom apartments within two miles of campus list for $900-$1,400/month (as of Q3 2026). Two-bedroom units in the same zone run $1,100-$1,800/month. Student housing communities commonly use **per-bedroom pricing**, a pricing structure where rent is quoted per individual bedroom rather than for the full unit. A $750/bedroom price on a 4BR unit means the full unit rents for $3,000/month. Compare carefully.

## The Three Zones Around UTSA Campus

The rental market around UTSA breaks into three geographic zones. Each has a distinct price range, commute profile, and renter type.

**UTSA Boulevard Corridor (0-2 miles):** 1BR $900-$1,400/mo. 2BR $1,100-$1,800/mo. Car optional (shuttle/walk). Purpose-built student housing dominates.

**Babcock/Vance Jackson Corridor (2-5 miles):** 1BR $800-$1,100/mo. 2BR $1,000-$1,400/mo. Car recommended. Mix of student and market-rate communities.

**Leon Valley / West San Antonio (5+ miles):** 1BR $700-$950/mo. 2BR $850-$1,200/mo. Car required. Lowest prices, most space.

### UTSA Boulevard and the Immediate Campus Area (0-2 miles)

This is where purpose-built student apartment communities cluster. Campus Edge on UTSA Boulevard (6802 UTSA Blvd), Prado Student Living (7114 UTSA Blvd), Hill Country Place (6222 UTSA Blvd), and University Oaks (6685 UTSA Blvd) all sit within walking distance or a short shuttle ride from the main campus.

The convenience comes at a cost. Rent runs 20-40% higher than comparable units five miles out (as of Q3 2026). Density is high, particularly during the August lease cycle. Noise levels reflect the student population. Per-bedroom pricing is standard, which makes direct comparisons with market-rate apartments difficult unless you convert to full-unit cost.

### The Babcock and Vance Jackson Corridor (2-5 miles)

This zone offers a meaningful price drop with a moderate commute. Villas at Babcock (14333 Babcock Rd), The Reserve San Antonio (13903 Babcock Rd), Maverick Creek Villas (15651 Chase Hill Blvd), and High View (15949 Chase Hill Blvd) are representative options.

Most commutes from this zone are by car or UTSA shuttle, not on foot. The Runner shuttle serves parts of this corridor, but confirm whether your specific complex is on the route before signing. Grocery, dining, and retail along Babcock Road make daily errands manageable without driving to campus.

### Further Out: Leon Valley and West San Antonio (5+ miles)

Leon Valley and the broader west San Antonio market offer the lowest rents and largest unit sizes. One-bedroom units regularly list under $900/month (as of Q3 2026). The tradeoff is clear: driving is required for everything, including campus access. Students who own a car and prioritize budget or space over proximity find strong value here. ## On-Campus vs. Off-Campus: What the Difference Costs

UTSA offers seven on-campus housing options: Alvarez Hall, Blanco Hall, Chaparral Village, Chisholm Hall, Guadalupe Hall, Laurel Village, and University Oaks. The first six are managed by [UTSA Housing and Residence Life](https://www.utsa.edu/housing/). University Oaks is managed separately by Campus Living Villages and prices from approximately $671-$1,456 per month depending on unit type and lease term (as of 2025-2026 academic year).

**Blanco Hall** is the newest addition, opening in January 2026 (Spring 2026 semester). This 155,000 sq ft, 4-floor residence hall at Barshop Blvd and Tobin Ave offers approximately 600 beds in single and double room configurations. Single room rate: $11,498/year ($5,749/semester) for the 2025-2026 academic year.

UTSA also announced **The Continental Hotel** downtown housing option in June 2026, accommodating approximately 50 students near the UTSA Downtown Campus at 322 W. Commerce Street, planned for Fall 2026. This provides a new housing category for students in downtown-focused academic programs.

UTSA has partnered with College Pads to provide a dedicated off-campus housing marketplace. Students can use this platform alongside brightplace to compare off-campus options.

On-campus housing typically bundles utilities and may require a meal plan, which increases the total cost beyond the headline rate. Off-campus apartments usually exclude utilities, adding $100-$200/month for electric, water, internet, and gas (as of Q3 2026).

When comparing, add utility costs to off-campus rent before measuring against on-campus rates. A $950/month off-campus apartment plus $150/month in utilities costs $1,100/month total, which may be comparable to or higher than an on-campus option depending on the residence hall. ## How UTSA Students Get Around

Transportation determines how far from campus you can practically live.

**The Runner** is UTSA's free campus shuttle service that connects main campus parking lots to select nearby apartment complexes, funded by the student transportation fee. It runs during the fall and spring semesters. If your apartment is on the Runner route, you can live 2-3 miles from campus without needing to park on campus daily. Confirm whether your specific complex is on the current route before relying on it.

**VIA U-Pass** is a program that provides current UTSA students, faculty, and staff with free, unlimited bus rides throughout San Antonio on the [VIA Metropolitan Transit](https://www.viainfo.net/) system. This extends your practical radius beyond the immediate campus area, particularly for students without cars.

Most apartments beyond one mile from campus require either a car, a seat on the Runner, or a VIA bus connection. Walking is realistic only for complexes directly on UTSA Boulevard.

## What to Check Before Signing a Lease Near UTSA

Five lease details matter more in the UTSA student rental market than in general market-rate leasing.

1. **Individual liability vs. joint lease.** An **individual liability lease** is a lease in which each resident is responsible only for their own rent installment, not their roommates' portion. This is standard in purpose-built student housing. Market-rate apartments typically use joint leases, where all tenants share responsibility for the full rent. Know which type you are signing.

2. **Lease start date.** Most UTSA-area student leases start in August, aligned with the fall semester. If you need a different start date, ask early. Mid-year availability is limited.

3. **Utilities bundled or separate.** Student housing communities often bundle utilities into rent. Market-rate apartments usually do not. Confirm what is included before comparing prices.

4. **Parking availability and cost.** On-campus parking is limited and permit-based. Off-campus complexes vary; some include parking, others charge $50-$150/month (as of Q3 2026).

5. **Pet policy.** If you have a pet, confirm the policy, deposit, and monthly pet rent before applying. For more on pet fees, see brightplace's guide to [pet-friendly apartments](https://www.brightplace.ai/resources/pet-friendly-apartments-greenville-sc).

## Common Questions About Renting Near UTSA

### Is it cheaper to live on or off campus at UTSA?

It depends on total cost, not headline rent. On-campus housing bundles utilities and may require a meal plan. Off-campus rent is often lower, but utilities add $100-$200/month (as of Q3 2026). Compare the all-in monthly cost, not just the base rent, before deciding. Off-campus is typically cheaper for students sharing a 2BR or larger unit.

### How much does UTSA housing cost per month?

UTSA on-campus housing ranges from approximately $671 to $1,456 per month depending on the residence hall and unit type (as of 2025-2026). University Oaks, managed by Campus Living Villages, sits at the upper end. Traditional residence halls with shared rooms sit at the lower end. Utilities are typically included.

### Where do UTSA students typically live off campus?

Most UTSA students living off campus rent along the UTSA Boulevard corridor (within 2 miles of campus) or the Babcock Road corridor (2-5 miles). Purpose-built student communities dominate UTSA Boulevard. Market-rate apartments with lower rents are more common along Babcock and Vance Jackson Roads.

### What is The Runner shuttle and which apartments does it serve?

The Runner is UTSA's free shuttle connecting main campus to select nearby apartment complexes along the UTSA Boulevard and Babcock Road corridors. It runs during fall and spring semesters and is funded by the student transportation fee. Not all off-campus complexes are on the route. Confirm with UTSA Transportation Services before signing a lease.

### What is an individual liability lease?

An individual liability lease means each tenant is responsible only for their own bedroom's rent, not for their roommates' payments. If a roommate stops paying, you are not liable for their share. This is standard in purpose-built student housing near UTSA and reduces financial risk for students sharing a unit.

### What is the average rent for a 1-bedroom apartment near UTSA?

One-bedroom apartments within two miles of UTSA's main campus list for $900-$1,400 per month (as of Q3 2026). Units in the Babcock/Vance Jackson corridor (2-5 miles) run $800-$1,100. Further out in Leon Valley, one-bedrooms drop below $900. Distance from campus is the primary price driver.

### Are there apartments near UTSA under $1,000 per month?

Yes. One-bedroom apartments under $1,000/month are available along the Babcock Road corridor and in Leon Valley (as of Q3 2026). These units are typically 3-5+ miles from campus and require a car or shuttle access. Per-bedroom rates in shared student housing units can also fall under $1,000 in the UTSA Boulevard corridor.

### How far from UTSA can I live without needing a car?

Without a car, your practical range is roughly one mile from the main campus on foot, or up to three miles if your apartment complex is on The Runner shuttle route. Beyond that, you need either a car or a VIA bus connection. Confirm shuttle availability for your specific complex before committing.
