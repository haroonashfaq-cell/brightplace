# What Renters Should Know About Foxcroft Apartments

*Last reviewed: August 2026*

## What Are Foxcroft Apartments?

Foxcroft Apartments is not a national apartment brand. It is a name used independently by at least seven unrelated apartment communities across the United States. Monthly rents range from approximately $610 in Montgomery, Alabama, to $2,200 or more in Scarborough, Maine (as of Q3 2026). Each property operates under separate ownership with different pricing, amenities, and management companies.

If you searched "foxcroft apartments" and landed here, the first step is identifying which Foxcroft community matches your city. The properties share a name but nothing else. Rent structures, pet policies, unit types, and management quality vary by location. Here is what brightplace has compiled across the known Foxcroft communities.

## Where Are Foxcroft Apartments Located?

Foxcroft Apartments communities are confirmed in seven cities across the eastern and central United States. Here is a breakdown of each known location:

**Scarborough, Maine:** 2BR townhomes starting from $2,200/month (as of Q3 2026). Managed by Princeton Properties. Located approximately 15 minutes from Portland. Quiet, tree-lined community with a community garden. Units include the Fennec (2BR/1BA, 950 sq ft) and Swift (2BR/1.5BA, 950 sq ft) floor plans.

**Tampa, Florida:** 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR units near USF and Tampa International Airport. Managed by Pacifica Residential at 3808 Hunt Rd. Dale Mabry Highway access for commuters. Contact the leasing office for current pricing.

**Oklahoma City, Oklahoma:** 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR layouts at 6810 NW 16th. Managed by RPM Living (Luxia Collection portfolio). Move-in specials have been as low as $399 for select units (as of Q3 2026). Pool, playground, and courtyard on site.

**Hampton, Virginia:** 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR apartments plus townhomes starting from $900/month (as of Q3 2026). Located near NASA Langley Research Center and Peninsula Town Center. Independent management.

**Green Bay, Wisconsin:** 1BR and 2BR units managed by FRED Inc. Contact the leasing office for current pricing. Two pets maximum with breed restrictions.

**Statesville, North Carolina:** 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, and townhome layouts. Independent management. Contact the leasing office for current pricing.

**Montgomery, Alabama:** 1BR units from $610/month and 3BR units from $870/month (as of Q3 2026). Managed by Hubbard Properties. $65 application fee and deposit equal to one month's rent.

## What Should You Compare Before Applying at Any Foxcroft Property?

Foxcroft is not a national brand with consistent standards. Each property operates independently, and quality varies. Here are four factors to evaluate before signing.

**Management company quality.** Ask who manages the property and confirm they have responsive maintenance. Request the average turnaround time for non-emergency work orders.

**Unit vintage.** Several Foxcroft communities are older construction (1970s-1990s) with partial renovations. Ask whether a specific unit is "classic" or "renovated" and what that means for appliances, HVAC age, and flooring. A classic unit typically means original finishes. A renovated unit has been updated with new flooring, appliances, or fixtures, and usually carries a higher monthly rent.

**True monthly cost.** Check whether utilities are separately metered (common in older buildings). Ask about pet rent, amenity fees, and any recurring charges beyond base rent. An amenity fee is a recurring charge for access to community features like a pool or fitness center, billed on top of base rent.

**Lease concession availability.** Older suburban communities often have move-in specials that are not advertised publicly. Ask the leasing office directly what incentives they can offer. For a full breakdown of how to evaluate these costs, see [your true monthly cost as a renter](https://www.brightplace.ai/guides/your-true-monthly-cost).

## What Are the Pet Policies at Foxcroft Apartments?

Pet policies at Foxcroft communities differ significantly by location. Most locations accept pets, but fees, weight limits, and breed restrictions vary. Here is what is confirmed:

**Scarborough, Maine:** $60/month for dogs, $50/month for cats. Two pets maximum.

**Green Bay, Wisconsin:** $40/month per pet, $300 one-time fee. Two pets maximum. Breed restrictions include Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Wolf-Hybrids, Akita, Chow Chow, and German Shepherd. Verify the current restricted breed list with the property directly before applying.

**Hampton, Virginia:** $300 non-refundable fee per pet, $25/month for the first pet, $15/month for the second. 55 lbs. maximum weight. Two pets maximum.

**Statesville, North Carolina:** $350 one-time fee, $35-$70/month pet rent depending on animal size. 45 lbs. maximum. Two pets maximum.

**Montgomery, Alabama:** $300 non-refundable fee per pet. 30 lbs. maximum weight.

**Tampa and OKC:** Both listed as pet-friendly. Contact the leasing office for current terms, fees, and any breed restrictions.

Pet rent is a monthly fee charged per pet in addition to base rent, separate from any one-time pet deposit or non-refundable pet fee. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Foxcroft Apartments

### 1. Where are Foxcroft Apartments located?

Foxcroft Apartments communities are located in Scarborough (Maine), Tampa (Florida), Oklahoma City (Oklahoma), Hampton (Virginia), Green Bay (Wisconsin), Statesville (North Carolina), and Montgomery (Alabama). Each property is independently owned and managed. There is no corporate parent connecting them.

### 2. What are rent prices at Foxcroft Apartments?

Rents range from approximately $610/month in Montgomery, Alabama to $2,200/month or more in Scarborough, Maine (as of Q3 2026). OKC has offered move-in specials from $399. Hampton starts around $900/month. Tampa and Green Bay require direct contact for current pricing.

### 3. Are Foxcroft Apartments pet-friendly?

Most Foxcroft locations accept pets, but policies differ at every property. Weight limits range from 30 lbs. (Montgomery) to 55 lbs. (Hampton). Monthly pet rent ranges from $15 to $60 depending on location. Green Bay enforces breed restrictions. Always confirm the full pet policy in writing before applying.

### 4. What is Foxcroft Apartments in OKC?

Foxcroft Apartments in Oklahoma City is located at 6810 NW 16th, managed by RPM Living under the Luxia Collection. It offers 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR units with a pool, playground, and courtyard. Move-in specials have been as low as $399 for select units (as of Q3 2026).

### 5. What is Foxcroft Apartments in Tampa?

Foxcroft Apartments in Tampa is located at 3808 Hunt Rd, managed by Pacifica Residential. It offers 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR floor plans with a pool and fitness center. The property sits near the University of South Florida and Tampa International Airport with Dale Mabry Highway commute access.

### 6. How do I find Foxcroft Apartments near me?

Search "Foxcroft Apartments" followed by your city name to narrow results to the specific property near you. Verify the management company and street address on the property's official website. Contact the leasing office directly to confirm current availability, pricing, and any active move-in specials.

### 7. What floor plans does Foxcroft Apartments offer?

Floor plans vary by location. Scarborough offers 2BR townhomes at 950 sq ft. OKC and Tampa offer 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR layouts. Montgomery ranges from 1BR to 3BR. Hampton includes apartments and townhomes. Tulsa offers 1BR only. Confirm specific floor plans and square footage with the property directly.

### 8. Does Foxcroft Apartments accept housing vouchers?

Housing voucher acceptance varies by location. Contact each Foxcroft property's leasing office directly to confirm whether they participate in the Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher program. Acceptance policies are set by individual property owners and can change.

### 9. What is the application process at Foxcroft Apartments?

Application requirements differ by location. Montgomery charges a $65 application fee with a deposit equal to one month's rent (as of Q3 2026). Other locations have their own fee structures. Ask the leasing office for the full application checklist, required documents, and timeline before submitting.

### 10. Are Foxcroft Apartments the same company?

No. "Foxcroft Apartments" is not a national brand. Each property using the Foxcroft name is independently owned and managed by a different company. Princeton Properties manages Scarborough, RPM Living manages OKC, Pacifica Residential manages Tampa, and Hubbard Properties manages Montgomery. There is no shared ownership.
