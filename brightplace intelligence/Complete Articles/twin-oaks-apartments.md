Pet policies vary by location. Twin Oaks Memphis allows dogs under 30 lbs only, one pet per unit, with a $350 one-time fee and $40/month pet rent (as of Q3 2026). Other locations may have different weight limits and fee structures. Confirm the full pet policy in writing with the specific property before applying.

### 4. What is the rent at Twin Oaks Apartments?

Rents across Twin Oaks communities range from approximately $781/month for a 1BR in Memphis to $1,950/month for a 3BR townhome in Madison, Alabama (as of Q3 2026). Tulsa and Hattiesburg require direct contact for current pricing. The Decatur, IL property is short-term corporate housing with separate rate structures.

### 5. Does Twin Oaks Apartments accept housing vouchers?

Twin Oaks Memphis accepts housing vouchers, including Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers. A housing voucher is a federal rental assistance program that subsidizes rent for income-qualifying households. Voucher acceptance at other Twin Oaks locations is not confirmed. Contact each property directly to verify participation.

### 6. What floor plans does Twin Oaks Apartments offer?

Floor plans vary by location. Memphis offers 1BR (640 sq ft), 2BR townhomes (1,040 sq ft), and 3BR townhomes (1,375 sq ft). Madison, AL offers 2BR and 3BR single-story townhomes with attached two-car garages. Tulsa offers 1BR only at 640 sq ft. Hattiesburg offers 1BR, 2BR, and 3BR apartments.

### 7. What is the difference between Twin Oaks Memphis and Twin Oaks Madison?

Twin Oaks Memphis (Multi-South Group) offers apartments and townhomes from $781/month in a gated community near I-240, accepting housing vouchers. Twin Oaks Madison (Freeman Webb) offers upscale townhomes from $1,850/month with granite countertops and attached garages near Redstone Arsenal. They serve entirely different renter profiles and price points.

### 8. Is Twin Oaks in Decatur, IL a regular apartment?

No. Twin Oaks II in Decatur is short-term corporate housing with fully furnished units designed for business travelers and relocating employees. Units include cable, internet, and in-unit washer/dryer. This property is not structured for standard 12-month leases. It is managed by Swartz Property at 2555 Twin Oaks Court.

### 9. What utilities do residents pay at Twin Oaks Memphis?

At Twin Oaks Memphis, residents are responsible for cable, electricity, internet, pest control, renter's insurance, sewer, trash, and water. These costs are separate from base rent and can add $150-$300/month depending on usage and season. Ask the leasing office for average monthly utility estimates before signing.
